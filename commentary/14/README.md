Fierce Hector rages across the battlefield, his heart full of fury, until mighty Aiax strikes him with a huge stone and knocks him flat. The Trojans rush to Hector’s aid and wash him in the waters of Xanthus as he vomits torrents of blood. Fighting then resumes, with terrible slaughter on both sides and the earth running with blood. Polydamas strikes Prothoenor a powerful blow; Aiax, identified as the son of Telamon, strikes Archelochus, son of Antenor; and Acamas strikes Promachus the Boeotian. Acamas is then laid low by the right hand of fierce Peneleus. The book ends amid the continuing battle, with Priam’s young warriors falling.
