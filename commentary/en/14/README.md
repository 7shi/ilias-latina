Hector, fierce, rages everywhere with violent heart, until mighty Ajax strikes him with a huge stone, drives him back, and lays him prostrate at full length. The Trojan band runs together, and they wash the young man in the flowing waters of Xanthus as he vomits streams of blood. They then return to the fight, with very great slaughter on both sides and the earth running with gore. Polydamas strikes Prothoenor with a powerful blow; Telamonian Ajax strikes Archelochus, called Antenorides, the son of Antenor; and Acamas strikes Promachus the Boeotian. Acamas is then laid low by the right hand of fierce Peneleus. The book ends amid the continuing battle, as the youth of Priam fall.
