# Proper-name correspondences (English)

Latin names and expressions and their English renderings in the
translation and commentary, organized by book. The English forms were
checked in the corresponding English book while separating the Latin
and English columns of the [Japanese table](../ja/proper_noun.md).

The Latin column gives the headword forms identified in the Latin
poem, original edition notes and indexes, with spelling variants where
needed. A dash marks a supplementary name for which no corresponding
Latin form was identified in those sources, including Greek names used
only in Homeric scenes and modern names. It does not mean that the
person or place has no Latin name elsewhere.

The English column includes names kept in Latin and descriptive
translations such as "Saturn’s son". Parentheses distinguish namesakes
or record a geographical name expressed through an adjective. Locations
follow the reviewed Japanese entries and refer to translated verses,
section commentary and book summaries; they may identify a person
through an epithet or family relationship rather than repeat the name.
The Category / Description column summarizes the identifications and
distinctions made in the English commentary, including namesakes and
variant readings. Japanese forms are retained in the Japanese table.
