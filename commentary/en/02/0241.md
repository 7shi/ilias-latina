### 241 (*Iliad* 2.870–871)

> 241 Ibat et Amphimachus Nastesque, insignis uterque,
> (Amphimachus and Nastes too marched on, both distinguished,)

The catalogue of Troy’s allies continues with Amphimachus and Nastes, brothers who lead the Carians, a people from southwestern Asia Minor, south of Troy. In Homer, they are the sons of Nomion, praised as his “glorious children” (*Iliad* 2.871). The Latin leaves out their father’s name but preserves that praise in “both distinguished.” As with the leaders just named, their homeland and the people under their command disappear from the shortened account. What remains is the impression of an expanding company of notable warriors marching to support Troy.
