### 244 (*Iliad* 2.864)

> 244 cum quibus et Mesthles atque Antiphus et bonus armis
> (with them too Mesthles and Antiphus, and, skilled in arms,)

Mesthles and Antiphus join the gathering of Troy’s allies. They command the Maeonians, a people of western Asia Minor associated with the region known as Lydia. Homer introduces the pair as leaders of that people (*Iliad* 2.864); the Latin keeps their names but again leaves out the people they lead, linking them directly to the commanders already listed. The closing phrase, “skilled in arms,” looks ahead to another warrior, whose name follows in the next verse.
