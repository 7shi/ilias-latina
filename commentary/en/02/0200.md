### 200 (*Iliad* 2.540)

> 200 Euboeae magnis Elephenor finibus ortus
> (Elephenor, born in the broad lands of Euboea)

The list of commanders continues with Elephenor, leader of the Abantes, the people of Euboea, a long island lying close to the eastern coast of mainland Greece. He brings a separate contingent to the Greek army, rather than sharing command of Prothous’ men.

Homer introduces him as “Elephenor, a scion of Ares,” who commanded his people (*Iliad* 2.540). This connection with the war god marks him as a warrior. The Latin leaves out that distinction and identifies him instead by his homeland, compressing Homer’s fuller account of the Euboean contingent into a brief catalogue entry.
