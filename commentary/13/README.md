Neptune supplies the Danaans with strength and courage, and a great battle breaks out, with the enemy raging on both sides. Asius falls by the right hand of Idomeneus, while Hector slays “the fierce Amphimachus.” Alcathous, the son-in-law of Anchises, also falls, laid low with a sword by “the great-hearted leader Rhytieus,” the Cretan leader Idomeneus. The book ends with Deiphobus, “fiery with his spear,” striking Ascalaphus and plunging him beneath the waves. The fighting remains unresolved.
