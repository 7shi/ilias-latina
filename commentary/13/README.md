Neptunus gives the Danai strength and courage, and a great battle breaks out, with both sides fighting furiously. Asius falls by the right hand of Idomeneus, while Hector slays “fierce Amphimachus.” Alcathous, identified as the son-in-law of Anchises, is also slain, laid low with a sword by “the great-hearted leader Rhytieus.” The omissions in the supplied text leave parts of the sequence incomplete. The book ends with Deiphobus, “fierce with his spear,” striking Ascalaphum and plunging him beneath the waves. The fighting remains unresolved.
