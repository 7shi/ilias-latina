### 196 (*Iliad* 2.653–654)

> 196 Tlepolemusque nouem Rhodius, quos uiribus acer
> (and Tlepolemus of Rhodes nine; these, keen in strength,)

After Teucer’s three ships come the nine commanded by Tlepolemus. His homeland, Rhodes, is a large island in the southeastern Aegean, near the coast of Asia Minor. Unlike the preceding entry, this one preserves both the commander and the number of ships given in Homer.

In the *Iliad*, Tlepolemus is introduced as a son of Hercules, leading nine ships of Rhodian warriors (*Iliad* 2.653–654). The Latin reduces that introduction to his name, his island origin, and his contribution to the fleet. His famous parentage drops away as the catalogue moves briskly from one contingent to another. The closing words begin a clause that continues in the next verse.
