### 189 (*Iliad* 2.527, 2.534)

> 189 et bis uicenas Locrum fortissimus Aiax
> (and Aiax, bravest of the Locri, filled forty ships.)

Aiax is the son of Oileus, often called the “lesser Ajax” to distinguish him from the other Ajax, son of Telamon, who is one of the Greeks’ foremost warriors. The Locri are the Locrians of central Greece, from the mainland opposite the island of Euboea.

The Latin compresses their contingent into a single line, carrying forward the preceding description of commanders filling their ships with oarsmen. It preserves Homer’s forty ships but replaces his description of Aiax as “swift” (*Iliad* 2.527) with praise of his courage as the bravest of his people. Homer’s “forty black ships” following their leader (*Iliad* 2.534) thus become another fleet being manned for the expedition.
