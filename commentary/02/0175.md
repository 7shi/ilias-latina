### 175 (*Iliad* 2.609–610)

> 175 insequitur totidemque ferox Agapenoris ira;
> (follows, and with as many Agapenor’s fierce fury;)

Agapenor, leader of the Arcadians from the mountainous interior of the Peloponnese in southern Greece, follows Menelaus with an equal fleet of sixty ships. The catalogue continues to measure the strength of the expedition while presenting its commanders as men eager for battle.

Homer introduces Agapenor as the son of Ancaeus and a mighty leader, then notes the many men aboard each ship (*Iliad* 2.609–610). The Latin leaves out his parentage and the crowded ships, replacing these details with “fierce fury.” Like Menelaus’ ardor in the preceding line, this fury stands for the warrior himself: it suggests his fighting spirit, not anger over any particular grievance.
