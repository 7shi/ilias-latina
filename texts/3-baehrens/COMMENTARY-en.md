# Baehrens: commentary

English translation of [COMMENTARY.md](COMMENTARY.md), with the items
taken from [ilias-en.md](ilias-en.md); see [README.md](README.md).

The notes in Baehrens's apparatus that bear on the interpretation of
the *Ilias Latina*.  The apparatus is almost entirely a record of
readings and conjectures, which are left out; what remains are a few
remarks on Homer, Vergil and prosody, and the borrowings in the *Gesta
Berengarii* and the quotation in Lactantius.

- 14 of the 784 items are kept, whole or in part.
- What is kept is quoted from ilias-en.md, cut at the same places as
  in COMMENTARY.md; "…" marks an omission within an item.
- Pages and labels are those of ilias.md: Baehrens's own verse
  numbers (see README.md for where they differ from The Latin
  Library).
