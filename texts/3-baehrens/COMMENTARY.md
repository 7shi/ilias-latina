# Baehrens: commentary

The notes in Baehrens's apparatus that bear on the interpretation of
the *Ilias Latina*, taken from [ilias.md](ilias.md).  The apparatus is
almost entirely a record of readings and conjectures, which are left
out; what remains are a few remarks on Homer, Vergil and prosody, and
the borrowings in the *Gesta Berengarii* and the quotation in
Lactantius.  See [README.md](README.md) for the edition.

- 14 of the 784 items are kept, whole or in part.
- What is kept is quoted as it stands in ilias.md; "…" marks an
  omission within an item.
- Pages and labels are those of ilias.md: Baehrens's own verse
  numbers (see README.md for where they differ from The Latin
  Library).
