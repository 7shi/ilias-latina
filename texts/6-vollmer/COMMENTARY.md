# Vollmer: commentary

The notes of Vollmer's edition that bear on the interpretation of the
*Ilias Latina*, taken from [ilias.md](ilias.md): the testimonia
(quotations and borrowings in later authors) and, from the apparatus,
the glosses, the references to Homer and the parallels in Latin
poets.  The readings of the manuscripts and the conjectures are left
out.  See [README.md](README.md) for the edition.

- All 18 testimonia and 46 of the 770 items of the apparatus are
  kept, the apparatus items in part.
- What is kept is quoted as it stands in ilias.md; "…" marks an
  omission within an item.
- A parallel cited in support of a reading is kept only when Vollmer
  prints that reading.
- The lines of the *Iliad* in the left margin of the text are not
  repeated here; they are in the Margin column of ilias.md.
- Pages and labels are those of ilias.md.
