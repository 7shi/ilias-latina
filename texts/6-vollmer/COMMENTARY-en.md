# Vollmer: commentary

English translation of [COMMENTARY.md](COMMENTARY.md), with the items
taken from [ilias-en.md](ilias-en.md); see [README.md](README.md).

The notes of Vollmer's edition that bear on the interpretation of the
*Ilias Latina*: the testimonia (quotations and borrowings in later
authors) and, from the apparatus, the glosses, the references to Homer
and the parallels in Latin poets.  The readings of the manuscripts and
the conjectures are left out.

- All 18 testimonia and 46 of the 770 items of the apparatus are
  kept, the apparatus items in part.
- What is kept is quoted from ilias-en.md, cut at the same places as
  in COMMENTARY.md; "…" marks an omission within an item.
- A parallel cited in support of a reading is kept only when Vollmer
  prints that reading.
- The lines of the *Iliad* in the left margin of the text are not
  repeated here; they are in the Margin column of ilias.md.
- Pages and labels are those of ilias.md.
