# Plessis: commentary

The notes of Plessis's edition that bear on the interpretation of the
*Ilias Latina*, taken from [ilias.md](ilias.md).  The readings of the
manuscripts (Codices), the verses printed below the text and the
conjectures recorded in the notes are left out.  What remains are the
references to the *Iliad*, most of them identifying the persons named
in the catalogues and battles, and the parallels in Latin poets.  See
[README.md](README.md) for the edition.

- 44 of the 286 notes are kept, in part.
- What is kept is quoted as it stands in ilias.md, whose notes have
  been checked against the page images; "…" marks an omission,
  usually the name of the scholar who proposed the reading.
- A parallel cited in support of a reading is kept only when Plessis
  prints that reading.
- Pages and labels are those of ilias.md.
