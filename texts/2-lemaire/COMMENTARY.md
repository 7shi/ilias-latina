# Lemaire: commentary

Wernsdorf's notes on the *Ilias Latina*, with the additions of the
Paris editor (marked "Ed."), taken from [ilias.md](ilias.md) and
reduced to what bears on the interpretation of the text: the passages
of Homer that a verse follows or leaves, the Latin models and
parallels, the persons, and the language and style.  The readings of
the manuscripts and editions, the conjectures and the arguments for
them are left out.  See [README.md](README.md) for the edition.

- 427 of the 769 notes are kept, whole or in part.  A note that deals
  only with the text is left out entirely.
- What is kept is quoted as it stands in ilias.md, whose notes have
  been checked against the page images; "…" marks an omission within
  a note.
- Where a name or a reading was restored from Homer, the reference to
  Homer is kept, since it identifies the passage that the verse
  follows.  A parallel cited in support of a reading is kept only when
  the edition prints that reading.
- Pages and labels are those of ilias.md: Wernsdorf's verse number,
  followed by "(LL n)" where the verse of The Latin Library differs.
