# Texts

Processed texts from the public-domain sources.  Each edition in the
[Internet Archive](../README.md#internet-archive) list is organized for
reference in its own directory:

- [2-lemaire/](2-lemaire/README.md) — Lemaire's reprint of Wernsdorf's
  edition: prooemium, testimonia, text with Wernsdorf's notes, excursus.
- [3-baehrens/](3-baehrens/README.md) — Baehrens's edition: preface, text
  with the book numbers in the margin and the apparatus.
- [4-plessis/](4-plessis/README.md) — Plessis's edition: preface,
  introduction, text with the readings of the manuscripts and the notes,
  index of names and subjects.
- [6-vollmer/](6-vollmer/README.md) — Vollmer's edition: preface, text
  with the *Iliad* lines in the margin and the apparatus, index of names.

## The Latin Library text

[ilias.txt](ilias.txt) is the Latin text of The Latin Library, one verse
per line (`N TEXT`), with a Markdown heading `## N` (the book number)
before each of the 24 books.  It is generated by `make text` in
[src/](../src/README.md).

## Books

Each book is defined by its first verse and runs up to the verse before
the next book.  The first verses follow the Portuguese translation, which
is based on Scaffai's edition, and were checked against The Latin Library
text (see [Structure](../README.md#structure) in the main README).

| Book | First verse | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | Invocation.  Apollo sends a plague for the wrong done to his priest Chryses; on Calchas' advice Agamemnon gives Chryseis back but takes Briseis from Achilles.  Pallas stops Achilles from drawing his sword; Thetis asks Jupiter to avenge him; Juno's complaint is settled by Vulcan. |
| 2 | 111 | Jupiter sends Sleep to Agamemnon, who calls the leaders to council.  Ulysses strikes Thersites with the sceptre; Nestor recalls the omen of the serpent at Aulis.  Catalogue of the Greek ships; Iris warns Priam, and Hector leads out the Trojans and their allies, listed by name. |
| 3 | 252 | Paris shrinks from Menelaus and is rebuked by Hector.  A truce is sworn and the two fight; Menelaus' sword breaks, he drags Paris by the helmet, and Venus carries Paris off in a cloud.  Helen reproaches Paris; Menelaus searches the field and Agamemnon demands Helen back. |
| 4 | 344 | Pandarus breaks the truce by wounding Menelaus, who is healed by Podalirius.  General battle, with single kills on both sides. |
| 5 | 389 | Diomedes' exploits with the help of Pallas: he kills Phegeus and Pandarus, fells Aeneas with a stone and wounds Venus, who flees to heaven; Apollo saves Aeneas.  Hector and Agamemnon drive back the enemy in turn; Sarpedon kills Tlepolemus; Pallas wounds Mars, who complains to Jupiter. |
| 6 | 538 | Ajax kills Acamas and Menelaus captures Adrastus.  Hector goes to the city and has Hecuba pray to Minerva.  Glaucus attacks Diomedes, who warns him off; they exchange shields and part. |
| 7 | 564 | Hector with Andromache and the infant Astyanax.  Hector challenges the Greek leaders; the lot falls to Ajax, and their duel is broken off at nightfall; recalling Hesione, they exchange a sword and a belt.  Agamemnon rejects the Trojan offer to give back Helen; the Greeks burn their dead and fortify the camp. |

Hector drives the Greeks back; Diomedes kills Agelaus; Teucer's arrows kill Gorgythion and Hector's charioteer until Hector fells him with a stone.  The Greeks are shut in their camp and the Trojans spend the night on the plain. |
| 9 | 686 | On Nestor's advice envoys are sent to Achilles, who refuses the gifts and Briseis. |
| 10 | 696 | Diomedes and Ulysses go out by night, capture the Trojan spy Dolon, learn the Trojan plans and kill him; they kill Rhesus in his sleep and take his Thracian horses. |
| 11 | 741 | Agamemnon kills Antiphus, Pisander and Hippolochus and attacks Iphidamas; wounded in the hand by his brother, a son of Antenor, he kills him.  Hector attacks; Paris wounds Eurypylus in the thigh. |
| 12 | 758 | The Trojans storm the Greek wall; Hector breaks the gate with a stone and the Greeks flee to the ships. |
