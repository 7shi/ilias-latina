# Japanese readings of the Latin

Each section of the *Ilias Latina* is read in Japanese in the order of
the Latin, one word at a time, from what is already known to what is
new. The Latin words are embedded in Japanese sentences, and the
[Japanese commentary](../../commentary/ja/README.md) is drawn on for
the content. The readings are generated by
[generate.py](../generate.py); see the [Makefile](../Makefile) for
its use.

Books 1 and 2 were generated first and revised afterwards by the
former review.py. From book 3 onwards generate.py reviews each draft
in turn, giving the reviewer the places found by mechanical checks.
The raw output of each book is committed before its corrections.

## Files

| File or directory | Contents |
|---|---|
| `NN/VVVV.md` | The reading of a section, with the same filename as in [commentary/ja/](../../commentary/ja/README.md): `NN` is the two-digit book number and `VVVV` the four-digit first Latin verse number. |
| [01/0001.md](01/0001.md) | The example reading of verses 1–8, given to the models with every section. |
| [ONESHOT.md](ONESHOT.md) | How the example reading was built, step by step; also given to the models. |

The drafts, the model of each draft and review (`models.tsv`) and the
errors of failed rounds (`errors.tsv`) are kept locally in `../tmp/`,
which is not tracked.
