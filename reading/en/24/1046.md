### 1046 (*Iliad* 24.689–691)

#### Line 1046

> 1046 in patriam Priamus tristesque ex more suorum

The homeland comes in, given as *in patriam*. The one who carries them back is Priam, called *Priamus*. Some are mournful as well, described as *tristesque*, though what they are is not yet said. Custom comes in, given as *ex more*. Their own people come in, which close the line as *suorum*. What they are comes only in the next section.
