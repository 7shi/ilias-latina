### 1047 (*Iliad* 24.777–783)

#### Line 1047

> 1047 apparat exsequias extremaque funera ducit.

He prepares them, told with *apparat*. What they are is obsequies, named *exsequias*. Some are last as well, described as *extremaque*. Funeral rites come in, named *funera*. He conducts them, which closes the line as *ducit*.
