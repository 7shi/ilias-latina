### 1027 (*Iliad* 24.477–478)

#### Line 1027

> 1027 affusus genibus tendens ad sidera palmas

He is prostrate, described as *affusus*. Knees come in, named *genibus*. He is stretching, described as *tendens*. The stars come in, given as *ad sidera*. What they are is palms, which close the line as *palmas*. What follows comes only in the next section.
