### 1048–1050 (*Iliad* 24.784–786, 23.175–176, 23.171–172)

#### Lines 1048–1050

> 1048 Tum pyra construitur, qua bis sex corpora Graium
>
> 1049 quadrupedesque adduntur equi currusque tubaeque
>
> 1050 et clipei galeaeque cauae argutaque tela.

A time is set, marked by *Tum*. A pyre comes in, named *pyra*. It is built, told with *construitur*. The pyre comes back, as *qua*. Twice six come in, given as *bis sex*. Bodies come in, named *corpora*. The Greeks come in, called *Graium*.

Some are four-footed as well, described as *quadrupedesque*, though what they are is not yet said. Some are added, told with *adduntur*. What they are is horses, named *equi*. Chariots come in as well, named *currusque*. Trumpets come in as well, named *tubaeque*. More is added, marked by *et*. Shields come in, named *clipei*. Helmets come in as well, named *galeaeque*. They are hollow, described as *cauae*. Some are whistling as well, described as *argutaque*. Missiles come in, which close the line as *tela*.
