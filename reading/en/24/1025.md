### 1025–1026 (*Iliad* 24.483–484)

#### Lines 1025–1026

> 1025 Mirantur Danaum proceres, miratur et ipse
>
> 1026 Aeacides animum miseri senis; ille trementes

Some marvel, told with *Mirantur*. The Danaans come in, called *Danaum*. As the commentary says, the Danaans are the Greeks. Those who marvel are chieftains, named *proceres*. Someone marvels, told with *miratur*. More is added, marked by *et*. Someone himself comes in, as *ipse*.

Who it is is Aeacides, called *Aeacides*. As the commentary says, Aeacides is Achilles, grandson of Aeacus. Courage comes in, named *animum*. Someone is wretched, described as *miseri*. An old man comes in, named *senis*. Someone comes in, as *ille*. Some are trembling, described as *trementes*. What they are comes only in the next section.
