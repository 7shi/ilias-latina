### 1017 (*Iliad* 22.405–406, 22.430–431)

#### Line 1017

> 1017 infelix Hecube saeuisque arat unguibus ora

Someone is unhappy, described as *infelix*. Who pours out the laments is Hecuba, called *Hecube*. As the commentary says, Hecuba is Hector's mother and the wife of Priam, Troy's king. Some are savage as well, described as *saeuisque*, though what they are is not yet said. She furrows something, told with *arat*. What they are is nails, named *unguibus*. A face comes in, which closes the line as *ora*.
