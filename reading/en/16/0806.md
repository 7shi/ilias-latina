### 806 (*Iliad* 16.130–131)

#### Line 806

> 806 Patroclus subitoque armis munitus Achillis

Who he is is Patroclus, called *Patroclus*. As the commentary says, Patroclus is the close companion of Achilles. Something is sudden as well, described as *subitoque*. Armor comes in, named *armis*, though whose it is is not yet said. He is fortified, described as *munitus*. Whose it is is Achilles', who closes the line as *Achillis*. What Patroclus does comes only in the next section.
