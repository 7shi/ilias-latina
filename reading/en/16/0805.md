### 805 (*Iliad* 16.2–3)

#### Line 805

> 805 Non ualet ulterius cladem spectare suorum

A denial comes, marked by *Non*. Someone has the strength, told with *ualet*. It is any longer, as *ulterius*. Slaughter comes in, named *cladem*. To watch comes next, told with *spectare*. The men are his own, which closes the line as *suorum*. Who he is comes only in the next section.
