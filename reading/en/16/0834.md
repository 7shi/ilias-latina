### 834 (*Iliad* 16.820–821)

#### Line 834

> 834 irruit et iuuenem nudato pectore ferro

He rushes upon him, told with *irruit*. More is added, marked by *et*. The young man comes in, named *iuuenem*. Something is laid bare, described as *nudato*, though what it is is not yet said. What is laid bare is his breast, named *pectore*. The sword comes in, which closes the line as *ferro*. What follows comes only in the next section.
