### 835 (*Iliad* 17.125)

#### Line 835

> 835 traicit et uictor Vulcania detrahit arma.

He runs him through, told with *traicit*. More is added, marked by *et*. He is the victor, named *uictor*. Something belongs to Vulcan, described as *Vulcania*, though what it is is not yet said. As the commentary says, Vulcan is the divine smith, the Roman counterpart of Hephaestus. He strips it off, told with *detrahit*. What belongs to Vulcan is the armor, which ends the sixteenth book as *arma*.
