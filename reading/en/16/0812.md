### 812–814 (*Iliad* 16.684–687, 16.731–732)

#### Line 812

> 812 et nunc hos cursu nunc illos praeterit ardens

More is added, marked by *et*. A time is set, given as *nunc*. These come in, as *hos*. His course comes in, named *cursu*. Another time is set, marked by *nunc*. Those come in, as *illos*. He passes them, told with *praeterit*. He is burning, which closes the line as *ardens*.

#### Line 813

> 813 proeliaque horrendi sub imagine uersat Achillis.

Battle comes in as well, named *proeliaque*. Someone is dread, described as *horrendi*, though who it is is not yet said. It is under a semblance, given as *sub imagine*. He wages it, told with *uersat*. The dread one is Achilles, who closes the line as *Achillis*.

#### Line 814

> 814 Quem postquam socias miscentem caede cateruas

Patroclus comes back, as *Quem*. A time is set, marked by *postquam*. Something is allied, given as *socias*, though what it is is not yet said. He is confounding them, described as *miscentem*. It is with slaughter, named *caede*. What is allied is the companies, which close the line as *cateruas*. As the commentary says, the allied companies are the forces fighting on Troy's side. What follows comes only in the next section.
