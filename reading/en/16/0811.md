### 811 (*Iliad* 16.477–482)

#### Line 811

> 811 sternit et ingenti Sarpedona uulnere fundit

He lays men low, told with *sternit*. More is added, marked by *et*. Something is huge, described as *ingenti*, though what it is is not yet said. Sarpedon comes in, called *Sarpedona*. As the commentary says, Sarpedon is a son of Jupiter and the leader of the Lycians. What is huge is a wound, named *uulnere*. He fells him, which closes the line as *fundit*.
