### 688 (*Iliad* 9.93–94)

#### Line 688

> 688 sed miseri sua fata gemunt. Mox Nestore pulsi

Something stands against this, marked by *sed*. They are in misery, described as *miseri*. Something is their own, described as *sua*. What was their own is their fate, named *fata*. They lament it, told with *gemunt*. A time is set, marked by *Mox*. Nestor comes in, named *Nestore*. They are urged on, which closes the line as *pulsi*. What they do comes only in the next section.
