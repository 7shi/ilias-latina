### 695 (*Iliad* 9.712–713)

#### Line 695

> 695 et dapibus curant animos lenique sopore.

More is added, marked by *et*. Food comes back, named *dapibus*. They refresh something, told with *curant*. What they refresh is their spirits, named *animos*. Something is gentle as well, described as *lenique*, though what it is is not yet said. What is gentle is sleep, which ends the ninth book as *sopore*.
