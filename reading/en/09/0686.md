### 686–687 (*Iliad* 9.1–3, 9.90–92)

#### Lines 686–687

> 686 Attoniti Danaum proceres discrimine tanto
>
> 687 nec dapibus releuant animos nec corpora curant,

Some are stunned, described as *Attoniti*. The Danaans come in, named *Danaum*. As the commentary says, the Danaans are the Greeks. Those stunned are their leaders, named *proceres*. A crisis comes in, named *discrimine*. The crisis is so great, which closes the line as *tanto*.

A denial comes, marked by *nec*. Food comes in, named *dapibus*. They refresh something, told with *releuant*. Their spirits come in, named *animos*. Another denial follows, marked by *nec*. Bodies come in, named *corpora*. They care for them, which closes the line as *curant*.
