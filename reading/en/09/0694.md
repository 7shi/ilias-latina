### 694 (*Iliad* 9.669–692)

#### Line 694

> 694 Irrita legati referunt responsa Pelasgis

Something is fruitless, described as *Irrita*, though what it is is not yet said. The envoys come back, named *legati*. They bring back something, told with *referunt*. What was fruitless is the reply, named *responsa*. The reply is to the Pelasgians, who close the line as *Pelasgis*. As the commentary says, the Pelasgians are the Greeks.
