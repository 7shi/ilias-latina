### 689–690 (*Iliad* 9.165–172)

#### Lines 689–690

> 689 legatos mittunt dextramque hortantur Achillis,
>
> 690 ut ferat auxilium miseris. Thetideius heros

Envoys come in, named *legatos*. They send them, told with *mittunt*. A right hand comes in as well, named *dextramque*. They appeal to it, told with *hortantur*. The right hand belongs to Achilles, who closes the line as *Achillis*.

A purpose comes in, marked by *ut*. He should bring something, told with *ferat*. What he should bring is aid, named *auxilium*. It is to those in misery, described as *miseris*. The son of Thetis comes in, described as *Thetideius*. As the commentary says, the son of Thetis is Achilles. He is a hero, who closes the line as *heros*. What he does comes only in the next section.
