### 360–361 (*Iliad* 4.457–461)

#### Lines 360–361

> 360 Occidit Antilochi rigido demersus in umbras
>
> 361 ense Thalysiades optataque lumina linquit.

Someone falls, told with *Occidit*. Antilochus comes in, named *Antilochi*. Something is hard, described as *rigido*, though what it is is not yet said. The fallen man has been plunged down, described as *demersus*. Where he goes is into the shades, which closes the line as *in umbras*.

What was described as *rigido* is his sword, called *ense*. The one who fell is the son of Thalysius, named *Thalysiades*. As the commentary says, this son of Thalysius is Echepolus. Something is longed for as well, described as *optataque*. What is longed for is the light, given as *lumina*. He leaves it behind, which closes the line as *linquit*.
