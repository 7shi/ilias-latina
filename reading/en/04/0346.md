### 346–347 (*Iliad* 4.104–105, 4.124–127, 4.134)

#### Lines 346–347

> 346 foederaque intento turbauit Pandarus arcu,
>
> 347 te, Menelae, petens; laterique uolatile telum

The treaty comes in, named *foederaque*. Something is drawn, described as *intento*, though what it is is not yet said. Someone broke the treaty, told with *turbauit*. The one who broke it is Pandarus, named *Pandarus*. What was drawn is a bow, which closes the line as *arcu*.

Someone is addressed, pointed to as *te*. It is Menelaus, called *Menelae*. Pandarus aims at him, told with *petens*. His side comes in, named *laterique*. Something is flying, described as *uolatile*. It is a missile, which closes the line as *telum*. What the missile does comes only in the next section.
