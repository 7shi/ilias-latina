### 355–356 (*Iliad* 4.446–451)

#### Lines 355–356

> 355 Bellum ingens oritur multumque utrimque cruoris
>
> 356 funditur et totis sternuntur corpora campis

War comes in, named *Bellum*. It is huge, described as *ingens*. It rises, told with *oritur*. Something is much, described as *multumque*, though what it is is not yet said. It is on both sides, marked by *utrimque*. What was much is blood, which closes the line as *cruoris*.

The blood is shed, told with *funditur*. More follows, joined by *et*. Something is whole, described as *totis*, though what it is is not yet said. Something is strewn, told with *sternuntur*. What is strewn is bodies, named *corpora*. What was whole is the fields, which closes the line as *campis*.
