### 348 (*Iliad* 4.135–136)

#### Line 348

> 348 incidit et tunicam ferro squamisque rigentem

The missile struck, told with *incidit*. More follows, joined by *et*. A tunic comes in, named *tunicam*. Iron comes in, called *ferro*. Scales come in as well, named *squamisque*. The tunic was stiff with them, which closes the line as *rigentem*. What happens to the tunic comes only in the next section.
