### 344–345 (*Iliad* 4.1–4)

#### Lines 344–345

> 344 Dumque inter sese proceres certamen haberent,
>
> 345 concilium omnipotens habuit regnator Olympi

Time is marked as continuing, given as *Dumque*. It is between themselves, marked by *inter sese*. The chiefs come in, named *proceres*. A contest comes in, called *certamen*. They held it, which closes the line as *haberent*.

A council comes next, named *concilium*. Someone is almighty, described as *omnipotens*. He held it, told with *habuit*. The almighty one is the ruler, named *regnator*. His realm is Olympus, which closes the line as *Olympi*. As the commentary says, the ruler of Olympus is Jupiter.
