### 353–354 (*Iliad* 4.223–225, 4.221–222)

#### Lines 353–354

> 353 Armauit fortes Agamemnonis ira Pelasgos
>
> 354 et dolor in pugnam cunctos communis agebat.

Someone armed them, told with *Armauit*. They are brave, described as *fortes*, though who they are is not yet said. Agamemnon comes in, named *Agamemnonis*. It is his anger, called *ira*. Those who are brave are the Pelasgians, which closes the line as *Pelasgos*. As the commentary says, the Pelasgians are the Greeks.

More follows, joined by *et*. Grief comes in, named *dolor*. It is into battle, given as *in pugnam*. It is all of them, named *cunctos*. The grief is shared, described as *communis*. It drove them, which closes the line as *agebat*.
