### 351–352 (*Iliad* 4.213–219)

#### Lines 351–352

> 351 Paeoniis curat iuuenis Podalirius herbis,
>
> 352 itque iterum in caedes horrendaque proelia uictor.

Something is Paeonian, described as *Paeoniis*, though what it is is not yet said. Someone treats him, told with *curat*. The healer is a youth, called *iuuenis*. The one who was taught is Podalirius, named *Podalirius*. What was Paeonian is herbs, which closes the line as *herbis*.

Menelaus goes, told with *itque*. It is again, marked by *iterum*. He goes into slaughter, given as *in caedes*. Something is dreadful as well, described as *horrendaque*. It is battles, named *proelia*. He goes as the victor, which closes the line as *uictor*.
