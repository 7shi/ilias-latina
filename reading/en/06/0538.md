### 538 (*Iliad* 6.5–8)

#### Line 538

> 538 Interea magnis Acamantem uiribus Aiax

Time moves on, marked by *Interea*. Something is great, described as *magnis*, though what it is is not yet said. Acamas comes in, named *Acamantem*. What was great is strength, named *uiribus*. The one with this strength is Ajax, who closes the line as *Aiax*. What he does to Acamas comes only in the next section.
