### 538 (*Iliad* 6.5–8)

#### Line 538

> 538 Interea magnis Acamantem uiribus Aiax

A time meanwhile comes in, marked by *Interea*. Something is great, described as *magnis*, though what it is is not yet said. Another warrior appears, named *Acamantem*. What was great is strength, called *uiribus*. The one with this strength is Ajax, who closes the line as *Aiax*, though what he does comes only in the next section.
