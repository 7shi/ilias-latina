### 551–552 (*Iliad* 6.293–310)

#### Lines 551–552

> 551 Dumque preces Hecube supplex ad templa Mineruae
>
> 552 pro caris genetrix natis et coniuge fundit,

A time while something happens is marked by *Dumque*. Prayers come in, named *preces*, though what is done with them is not yet said. The queen is Hecuba, called *Hecube*. She is a suppliant, described as *supplex*. She is at the temple, given as *ad templa*. The temple belongs to Minerva, which closes the line as *Mineruae*.

It is for dear ones, given as *pro caris*. She appeals as a mother, named *genetrix*. The dear ones are her children, called *natis*. More follows, marked by *et*. She prays for her husband as well, named *coniuge*. She pours them out, told with *fundit*, though what happens while she does so comes only in the next section.
