### 551–552 (*Iliad* 6.293–310)

#### Lines 551–552

> 551 Dumque preces Hecube supplex ad templa Mineruae
>
> 552 pro caris genetrix natis et coniuge fundit,

A time is set, marked by *Dumque*. Prayers come in, named *preces*. Hecuba comes in, named *Hecube*. She is a suppliant, described as *supplex*. It is at the temple, given as *ad templa*. The temple is Minerva's, which closes the line as *Mineruae*.

Something is dear, given as *pro caris*, though what it is is not yet said. She is a mother, named *genetrix*. What is dear is her children, named *natis*. More follows, joined by *et*. Her husband comes in, named *coniuge*. She pours out the prayers, told with *fundit*. What happens while she does so comes only in the next section.
