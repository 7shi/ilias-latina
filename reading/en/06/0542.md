### 542–546 (*Iliad* 6.73–76, 6.94–97, 6.86–87, 6.237)

#### Lines 542–546

> 542 Incumbunt Danai, cedit Troiana iuuentus
>
> 543 tergaque nuda tegit. Sensit Mauortius Hector
>
> 544 pro Danais pugnare deos ualidasque suorum
>
> 545 uirginis armigerae subduci numine uires
>
> 546 continuoque petit muros Hecubamque uocari

Someone presses on, told with *Incumbunt*. They are the Danaans, named *Danai*. As the commentary says, the Danaans are the Greeks. Someone gives way, told with *cedit*. Something is Trojan, described as *Troiana*. What is Trojan is the youth, which closes the line as *iuuentus*.

Backs come in as well, named *tergaque*. They are exposed, described as *nuda*. The youth covers them, told with *tegit*. Someone perceived something, told with *Sensit*. He is of Mars, described as *Mauortius*. The one who perceived is Hector, named *Hector*.

Something is for the Danaans, given as *pro Danais*. Fighting comes next, told with *pugnare*. Those fighting for them are gods, named *deos*. Something is mighty as well, described as *ualidasque*, though what it is is not yet said. It belongs to his men, named *suorum*.

A maiden comes in, named *uirginis*. She is armed, described as *armigerae*. As the commentary says, the armed maiden is Minerva, a powerful supporter of the Greeks. It is by her divine power, named *numine*. Something is being drawn away, told with *subduci*. What was mighty is strength, which closes the line as *uires*.

It is at once, marked by *continuoque*. He heads for something, told with *petit*. It is the walls, named *muros*. Hecuba comes in as well, named *Hecubamque*. She is to be summoned, told with *uocari*. Who orders it comes only in the next section.
