### 539–541 (*Iliad* 6.37–38, 6.52–53)

#### Lines 539–541

> 539 interimit uastumque capit Menelaus Adrastum
>
> 540 et rapit ad classes manibus post terga reuinctis,
>
> 541 ut ui deducat laetos ex hoste triumphos.

He slays him, told with *interimit*. Another is huge as well, described as *uastumque*, though who it is is not yet said. Someone captures him, told with *capit*. The captor is Menelaus, called *Menelaus*. The huge one captured is Adrastus, who closes the line as *Adrastum*.

More follows, marked by *et*. He drags him away, told with *rapit*. It is to the ships, given as *ad classes*. His hands come in, named *manibus*. They are behind his back, given as *post terga*. They were bound there, described as *reuinctis*.

A purpose follows, marked by *ut*. It is by force, called *ui*. He would bring them off, told with *deducat*. They are joyful, described as *laetos*. It is over his enemy, given as *ex hoste*. The joyful things are triumphs, which close the line as *triumphos*.
