### 539–541 (*Iliad* 6.37–38, 6.52–53)

#### Lines 539–541

> 539 interimit uastumque capit Menelaus Adrastum
>
> 540 et rapit ad classes manibus post terga reuinctis,
>
> 541 ut ui deducat laetos ex hoste triumphos.

Ajax slays him, told with *interimit*. Someone is huge as well, described as *uastumque*, though who it is is not yet said. Someone captures him, told with *capit*. The captor is Menelaus, named *Menelaus*. The huge one is Adrastus, who closes the line as *Adrastum*.

More follows, joined by *et*. Menelaus drags him off, told with *rapit*. It is to the ships, given as *ad classes*. Hands come in, named *manibus*. It is behind the back, given as *post terga*. The hands are bound, which closes the line as *reuinctis*.

A purpose follows, marked by *ut*. It is by force, named *ui*. He would win something, told with *deducat*. Something is joyful, described as *laetos*, though what it is is not yet said. It is over his enemy, given as *ex hoste*. What is joyful is triumphs, which close the line as *triumphos*.
