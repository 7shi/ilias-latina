### 563 (*Iliad* 6.230, 6.235–236)

#### Line 563

> 563 commutant clipeos inimicaque proelia linquunt.

They exchange something, told with *commutant*. It is shields, named *clipeos*. Something is hostile as well, described as *inimicaque*. It is combat, named *proelia*. They leave it, which ends the sixth book as *linquunt*.
