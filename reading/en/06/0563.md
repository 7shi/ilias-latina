### 563 (*Iliad* 6.230, 6.235–236)

#### Line 563

> 563 commutant clipeos inimicaque proelia linquunt.

They exchange them, told with *commutant*. What they exchange is shields, called *clipeos*. Their combat was hostile as well, described as *inimicaque*. The hostile struggle was battle, named *proelia*. They abandon it, told with *linquunt*. With this, the sixth book ends.
