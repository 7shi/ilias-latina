### 951–958 (*Iliad* 22.248, 22.273)

#### Lines 951–952

> 951 Concurrunt iactis inter se comminus hastis
>
> 952 inuicti iuuenes: hic uastis intonat armis,

Some rush together, told with *Concurrunt*. Something has been hurled, described as *iactis*, though what it is is not yet said. It is at each other, given as *inter se*. Something is at close quarters, marked by *comminus*. What has been hurled is spears, which close the line as *hastis*.

The unconquered young men come in, given as *inuicti iuuenes*. One of them comes in, as *hic*. Something is mighty, described as *uastis*, though what it is is not yet said. He thunders, told with *intonat*. What is mighty is his armor, which closes the line as *armis*.

#### Lines 953–954

> 953 ille hostem ualidum nequiquam umbone repellit
>
> 954 alternisque ferox mutat congressibus ictus.

The other comes in, as *ille*. His powerful enemy comes in, given as *hostem ualidum*. It is in vain, marked by *nequiquam*. A shield-boss comes in, named *umbone*. He thrusts him back, which closes the line as *repellit*.

Something is by turns as well, described as *alternisque*, though what it is is not yet said. He is fierce, described as *ferox*. He varies something, told with *mutat*. What is by turns is clashes, named *congressibus*. What he varies is blows, which close the line as *ictus*.

#### Lines 955–956

> 955 Sudor agit riuos, ensem terit horridus ensis
>
> 956 collatusque haeret pede pes et dextera dextrae.

Sweat comes in, named *Sudor*. It drives something, told with *agit*. Streams come in, named *riuos*. A sword comes in, named *ensem*. Something grinds against it, told with *terit*. A savage sword comes in, which closes the line as *horridus ensis*.

Something is pressed close together as well, described as *collatusque*. It holds, told with *haeret*. A foot comes in, named *pede*. Another foot comes in, named *pes*. More is added, marked by *et*. A right hand comes in, named *dextera*. It is against another right hand, which closes the line as *dextrae*.

#### Lines 957–958

> 957 Hastam iam manibus saeuus librabat Achilles
>
> 958 inque uirum magnis emissam uiribus egit,

A spear comes in, named *Hastam*. A time is set, marked by *iam*. Hands come in, named *manibus*. Someone is fierce, described as *saeuus*, though who it is is not yet said. He was poising something, told with *librabat*. Who is fierce is Achilles, who closes the line as *Achilles*.

His opponent comes in as well, given as *inque uirum*. Something is great, described as *magnis*, though what it is is not yet said. The spear has been sent forth, described as *emissam*. What is great is force, named *uiribus*. He drove it, which closes the line as *egit*. What follows comes only in the next section.
