### 977–979 (*Iliad* 22.324–327, 22.337)

#### Line 977

> 977 et medias rigida transfixit cuspide fauces.

More is added, marked by *et*. Something is the middle, described as *medias*, though what it is is not yet said. Something is hard, described as *rigida*, though what it is is not yet said. He pierced something, told with *transfixit*. What is hard is a spear-point, named *cuspide*. What is the middle is the throat, which closes the line as *fauces*.

#### Line 978

> 978 Exsultant Danai, Troes sua uulnera deflent.

Some exult, told with *Exsultant*. The Danaans come in, named *Danai*. The Trojans come in, named *Troes*. Their own wounds come in, given as *sua uulnera*. They lament them, which closes the line as *deflent*.

#### Line 979

> 979 Tunc sic amissis infelix uiribus Hector:

A time is set, marked by *Tunc*. A manner is set, marked by *sic*. Something is lost, described as *amissis*, though what it is is not yet said. Someone is ill-fated, described as *infelix*. What is lost is strength, named *uiribus*. Hector comes in, who closes the line as *Hector*. What he says comes only in the next section.
