### 996–997 (*Iliad* 22.361–363, 22.395)

#### Lines 996–997

> 996 ore truci iactat, uitam miserabilis Hector
>
> 997 reddidit. Hunc animi nondum satiatus Achilles

A savage mouth comes in, given as *ore truci*. He hurls them, told with *iactat*. Life comes in, named *uitam*. Pitiable Hector comes in, who closes the line as *miserabilis Hector*.

He gave it up, told with *reddidit*. Hector comes back, as *Hunc*. The heart comes in, named *animi*. Something has not yet happened, marked by *nondum*. Someone is satisfied, described as *satiatus*. Achilles comes in, who closes the line as *Achilles*. What he does to him comes only in the next section.
