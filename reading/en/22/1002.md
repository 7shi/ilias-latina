### 1002–1003 (*Iliad* 22.405–411)

#### Lines 1002–1003

> 1002 Laetantur Danai, plangunt sua uulnera Troes
>
> 1003 et pariter captos deflent cum funere muros.

Some rejoice, told with *Laetantur*. The Danaans come in, named *Danai*. Some lament, told with *plangunt*. Their own wounds come in, given as *sua uulnera*. The Trojans come in, which close the line as *Troes*.

More is added, marked by *et*. Equally comes in, marked by *pariter*. Some are captured, described as *captos*, though what they are is not yet said. They mourn them, told with *deflent*. A death comes in, given as *cum funere*. What are captured is walls, which ends the twenty-second book as *muros*.
