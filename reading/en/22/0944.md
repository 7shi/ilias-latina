### 944–946 (*Iliad* 22.25–35)

#### Line 944

> 944 Spectant de muris miseri sua fata parentes

Some watch, told with *Spectant*. The walls come in, given as *de muris*. Some are wretched, described as *miseri*, though who they are is not yet said. Their own fate comes in, given as *sua fata*. Who are wretched are the parents, who close the line as *parentes*. As the commentary says, the parents are Priam and Hecuba.

#### Lines 945–946

> 945 pallentemque uident supremo tempore natum
>
> 946 quem iam summa dies suprema luce premebat.

Someone is pale as well, described as *pallentemque*, though who it is is not yet said. They see him, told with *uident*. His final hour comes in, given as *supremo tempore*. Who is pale is their son, who closes the line as *natum*. As the commentary says, their son is Hector.

Hector comes back, as *quem*. A time is set, marked by *iam*. His last day comes in, given as *summa dies*. Its final light comes in, given as *suprema luce*. It bore down on him, which closes the line as *premebat*.
