### 749 (*Iliad* 11.122–123, 11.143–144)

#### Line 749

> 749 Pisandrumque simul fratremque ad bella ruentem

Pisander comes in as well, named *Pisandrumque*. It is at the same time, marked by *simul*. His brother comes in as well, named *fratremque*. War comes in, given as *ad bella*. He is rushing, which closes the line as *ruentem*. What becomes of them comes only in the next section.
