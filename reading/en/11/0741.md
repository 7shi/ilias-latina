### 741–742 (*Iliad* 11.1)

#### Lines 741–742

> 741 Lux exorta uiros in pristina bella remisit,
>
> 742 instaurantque animos recreato milite pugnae

Light comes in, named *Lux*. It has arisen, described as *exorta*. The men come in, named *uiros*. Something is former, given as *in pristina*, though what it is is not yet said. What is former is wars, named *bella*. Daybreak sent them back to them, which closes the line as *remisit*.

They renew something as well, told with *instaurantque*. What they renew is spirits, named *animos*. Someone is refreshed, described as *recreato*, though who it is is not yet said. The one who is refreshed is the soldier, named *milite*. The fight comes in, which closes the line as *pugnae*.
