### 754–755 (*Iliad* 11.284–285)

#### Lines 754–755

> 754 Hector tunc pugnae subit acri concitus ira
>
> 755 Priamides et percussos agit undique Graios;

Hector comes in, named *Hector*. A time is set, marked by *tunc*. The battle comes in, given as *pugnae*. He enters it, told with *subit*. Something is fierce, described as *acri*, though what it is is not yet said. He is stirred, described as *concitus*. What is fierce is anger, which closes the line as *ira*.

He is also Priamides, named *Priamides*. As the commentary says, the name marks Hector as the son of Priam. More is added, marked by *et*. Some are stricken, described as *percussos*, though who they are is not yet said. He drives them, told with *agit*. It is on every side, marked by *undique*. Those stricken are the Greeks, who close the line as *Graios*.
