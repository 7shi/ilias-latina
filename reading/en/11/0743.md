### 743–747 (*Iliad* 11.15, 11.55–56, 11.67–71, 11.91–92)

#### Lines 743–746

> 743 Dardanidum Danaumque duces: uolat undique nubes
>
> 744 telorum et ferro ferrum sonat, undique mixtis
>
> 745 inter se strident mucronibus: instat utrimque
>
> 746 densa acies mixtusque fluit cum sanguine sudor.

The Dardanians come in, named *Dardanidum*. As the commentary says, the Dardanians are the Trojans. The Danaans come in as well, named *Danaumque*. As the commentary says, the Danaans are the Greeks. Those who renew it are the leaders, named *duces*. Something flies, told with *uolat*. It is from every side, marked by *undique*. What flies is a cloud, which closes the line as *nubes*.

The cloud is of missiles, named *telorum*. More is added, marked by *et*. Iron comes in, named *ferro*. Iron rings against it, named *ferrum*. It sounds, told with *sonat*. It is on every side, marked by *undique*. Some are mingled, which closes the line as *mixtis*, though what they are is not yet said.

They are against one another, given as *inter se*. They grate, told with *strident*. What is mingled is blades, named *mucronibus*. Something presses forward, told with *instat*. It is on both sides, which closes the line as *utrimque*.

Something is dense, described as *densa*, though what it is is not yet said. What is dense is the battle line, named *acies*. Something is mingled as well, described as *mixtusque*. It flows, told with *fluit*. Blood comes in, given as *cum sanguine*. What is mingled with it is sweat, which closes the line as *sudor*.

#### Line 747

> 747 Tandem feruenti Danaum rex concitus ira

At last something changes, marked by *Tandem*. Something is seething, described as *feruenti*, though what it is is not yet said. The Danaans come back, named *Danaum*. Their king comes in, named *rex*. As the commentary says, the king of the Danaans is Agamemnon. He is roused, described as *concitus*. What is seething is anger, which closes the line as *ira*. What he does comes only in the next section.
