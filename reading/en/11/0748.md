### 748 (*Iliad* 11.101, 11.109)

#### Line 748

> 748 Antiphon ingenti prostratum uulnere fundit

Antiphus comes in, named *Antiphon*. As the commentary says, Antiphus is a son of Priam. Something is huge, described as *ingenti*, though what it is is not yet said. He is struck down, described as *prostratum*. What is huge is a wound, named *uulnere*. Agamemnon lays him low, which closes the line as *fundit*.
