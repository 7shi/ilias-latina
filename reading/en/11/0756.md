### 756 (*Iliad* 11.369–370, 11.376–378)

#### Line 756

> 756 nec Paris hostiles cessat prosternere turmas

A denial comes, marked by *nec*. Paris comes in, named *Paris*. Some belong to the enemy, described as *hostiles*, though what they are is not yet said. He does not cease, told with *cessat*. Laying them low comes next, told with *prosternere*. What belongs to the enemy is squadrons, which close the line as *turmas*.
