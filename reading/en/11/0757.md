### 757 (*Iliad* 11.581–584)

#### Line 757

> 757 Eurypylique femur contento uulnerat arcu.

Eurypylus comes in as well, named *Eurypylique*. His thigh comes in, spoken of as *femur*. Something is drawn, described as *contento*, though what it is is not yet said. Paris wounds him, told with *uulnerat*. What is drawn is a bow, which ends the eleventh book as *arcu*.
