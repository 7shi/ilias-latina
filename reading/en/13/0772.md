### 772–773 (*Iliad* 13.43–45)

#### Lines 772–773

> 772 Neptunus uires Danais animumque ministrat:
>
> 773 pugna ingens oritur, furit istinc hostis et illinc.

Neptune comes in, called *Neptunus*. Strength comes in, named *uires*. The Danaans come in, named *Danais*. As the commentary says, the Danaans are the Greeks. Courage comes in as well, named *animumque*. He supplies them, told with *ministrat*.

A battle comes in, named *pugna*. It is great, described as *ingens*. It breaks out, told with *oritur*. Someone rages, told with *furit*. It is on this side, marked by *istinc*. The one who rages is the enemy, named *hostis*. More is added, marked by *et*. It is on that side as well, which closes the line as *illinc*.
