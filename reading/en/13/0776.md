### 776–777 (*Iliad* 13.427–434)

#### Lines 776–777

> 776 Anchisae gener Alcathous, quem fuderat ense
>
> 777 magnanimus ductor Rhytieus. Tunc feruidus hasta

Anchises comes in, called *Anchisae*. His son-in-law comes in, named *gener*. The son-in-law is Alcathous, called *Alcathous*. Alcathous comes back, as *quem*. Someone had laid him low, told with *fuderat*. A sword comes in, which closes the line as *ense*.

Someone is great-hearted, described as *magnanimus*, though who it is is not yet said. The great-hearted one is the leader, named *ductor*. He is from Rhytion, described as *Rhytieus*. As the commentary says, Rhytieus is Idomeneus. A time is set, marked by *Tunc*. Someone is fiery, described as *feruidus*, though who it is is not yet said. A spear comes in, which closes the line as *hasta*. Who is fiery comes only in the next section.
