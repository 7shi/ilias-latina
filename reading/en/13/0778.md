### 778 (*Iliad* 13.516–520)

#### Line 778

> 778 Deiphobus ferit Ascalaphum mergitque sub undas.

The one who is fiery is Deiphobus, called *Deiphobus*. He strikes someone, told with *ferit*. The one he strikes is Ascalaphus, named *Ascalaphum*. He plunges him as well, told with *mergitque*. The waves come in, which ends the thirteenth book as *sub undas*.
