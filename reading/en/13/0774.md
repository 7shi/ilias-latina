### 774 (*Iliad* 13.384–393)

#### Line 774

> 774 Idomenei dextra cadit Asius; Hector atrocem

Idomeneus comes in, called *Idomenei*. His right hand comes in, named *dextra*. Someone falls, told with *cadit*. The one who falls is Asius, named *Asius*. Hector comes in, called *Hector*. Someone is fierce, which closes the line as *atrocem*. Who is fierce and what Hector does comes only in the next section.
