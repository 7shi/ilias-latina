### 775 (*Iliad* 13.183–187)

#### Line 775

> 775 Amphimachum obtruncat nec non occumbit in armis

The one who is fierce is Amphimachus, named *Amphimachum*. Hector slays him, told with *obtruncat*. There is more still, given as *nec non*. Someone falls, told with *occumbit*. Arms come in, which close the line as *in armis*. Who falls comes only in the next section.
