### 182–183 (*Iliad* 2.740, 2.745, 2.747)

#### Lines 182–183

> 182 et bis uicenas Polypoetes atque Leonteus
>
> 183 instruxere rates ornatas milite forti.

More follows, joined by *et*. The count is forty, given as *bis uicenas*, though forty of what is not yet said. Polypoetes comes in, named *Polypoetes*. Another joins him, marked by *atque*. He is Leonteus, named *Leonteus*. They fitted them out, told with *instruxere*. The forty are ships, named *rates*. They were equipped, described as *ornatas*. It was with soldiers, brave ones, who close the line as *milite forti*.
