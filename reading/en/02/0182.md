### 182–183 (*Iliad* 2.740, 2.745, 2.747)

#### Lines 182–183

> 182 et bis uicenas Polypoetes atque Leonteus
>
> 183 instruxere rates ornatas milite forti.

Another force is joined by *et*, numbering forty, counted as *bis uicenas*, though of what is not yet said. The first commander is Polypoetes, named *Polypoetes*. His comrade is joined by *atque*, entering as *Leonteus*. Together they fitted them out, told with *instruxere*. What was counted as *bis uicenas* is now revealed as the ships, spoken of as *rates*. The vessels were fully equipped, described as *ornatas*. They were manned by brave warriors, designated as *milite forti*.
