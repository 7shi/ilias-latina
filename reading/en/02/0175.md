### 175 (*Iliad* 2.609–610)

#### Line 175

> 175 insequitur totidemque ferox Agapenoris ira;

The fire of Menelaus follows, told with *insequitur*. As many come next, given as *totidemque*. Something is fierce, described as *ferox*. It belongs to Agapenor, named *Agapenoris*. What is fierce is his anger, which closes the line as *ira*.
