### 175 (*Iliad* 2.609–610)

#### Line 175

> 175 insequitur totidemque ferox Agapenoris ira;

Another contingent follows right behind, told with *insequitur*. Its force matches in equal number, joined as *totidemque*. The warrior is fierce in temper, described as *ferox*. The leader is Agapenor, appearing as *Agapenoris*. His fighting fury represents the man, closing the line as *ira*.
