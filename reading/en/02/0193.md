### 193–194 (*Iliad* 2.678, 2.680)

#### Lines 193–194

> 193 Thessalici iuuenes Phidippus et Antiphus ibant
>
> 194 altaque ter denis pulsarunt aequora proris

Young men of Thessaly come next, named *Thessalici iuuenes*. The first is Phidippus, named *Phidippus*. More follows, joined by *et*. The second is Antiphus, named *Antiphus*. They went forth, told with *ibant*.

Something is deep, described as *altaque*, though what is deep is not yet said. The count is three tens, given as *ter denis*. They struck the deep thing, told with *pulsarunt*. What was described as *altaque* is the seas, named *aequora*. They struck them with prows, which close the line as *proris*.
