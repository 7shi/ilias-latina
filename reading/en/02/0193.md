### 193–194 (*Iliad* 2.678, 2.680)

#### Lines 193–194

> 193 Thessalici iuuenes Phidippus et Antiphus ibant
>
> 194 altaque ter denis pulsarunt aequora proris

Young men of Thessaly come next, appearing as *Thessalici iuuenes*. The first is Phidippus, named *Phidippus*. His brother is joined by *et*, entering as *Antiphus*. Together they went forth, told with *ibant*.

The sea they enter is deep, joined as *altaque*, though what is deep is not yet said. Their fleet numbers thirty, counted as *ter denis*. They strike against the waters, expressed by *pulsarunt*. What was described as *altaque* is the expanse of the waves, entering as *aequora*. They cut through it with their prows, closing the line as *proris*.
