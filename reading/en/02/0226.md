### 226–235 (*Iliad* 2.816–818, 2.809–810)

#### Lines 226–227

> 226 Priamides Hector totamque in proelia pubem
>
> 227 festinare iubet portisque agit agmen apertis.

The warrior who answers his father's command is the son of Priam, appearing as *Priamides*. His own name follows at once, entering as *Hector*. He calls upon the whole company, joined as *totamque*, though what body of men it is is not yet said. Their mission is directed into battle, spoken of as *in proelia*. What was joined as *totamque* is revealed as the youth of the city, closing the line as *pubem*.

He commands them to make haste, told with *festinare*. The order is given, instructed with *iubet*. He also leads them through the city gates, joined as *portisque*, though in what condition they stand is not yet said. He drives them on, expressed by *agit*. The force in motion is the marching column, named *agmen*. What was joined as *portisque* turns out to be wide open, closing the verse as *apertis*.

#### Lines 228–232

> 228 Cui fulgens auro cassis iuuenile tegebat
>
> 229 omni parte caput, munibat pectora thorax
>
> 230 et clipeus laeuam, dextram decorauerat hasta
>
> 231 ornabatque latus mucro; simul alta nitentes
>
> 232 crura tegunt ocreae, quales decet Hectoris esse.

The warrior is referred to with *Cui*. His armor is gleaming, described as *fulgens*. The radiance comes from gold, told with *auro*. The piece that shines is his helmet, appearing as *cassis*. It protects what is youthful, designated as *iuuenile*, though what part it guards is not yet said. It covered him completely, expressed by *tegebat*. It sheltered him on every side, recorded as *omni parte*. What was designated as *iuuenile* is revealed as his head, named *caput*. Another piece guarded him, declared with *munibat*. It shielded his chest, standing as *pectora*. That defense is a breastplate, closing the line as *thorax*.

A further defense is added by *et*, a shield, named *clipeus*. It covered his left hand, designated as *laeuam*. His right hand was also armed, entering as *dextram*. It had been graced with a weapon, praised with *decorauerat*. The weapon that graced it is a spear, closing the verse as *hasta*.

A weapon also adorned him, told with *ornabatque*. It hung at his flank, designated as *latus*. That weapon is a sword, appearing as *mucro*. At the same time further equipment is brought in, marked by *simul*. They gleam from on high, described as *alta nitentes*, though what they cover is not yet said. What was described as *alta nitentes* is revealed as his legs, entering as *crura*. They cover his limbs, expressed by *tegunt*. These guards are greaves, spoken of as *ocreae*. They are of such fine quality, shown with *quales*. It is fitting for a prince, declared with *decet*. The armor is worthy of Hector, named as *Hectoris*. It was right for them to be so, standing at the end of the line as *esse*.

#### Lines 233–235

> 233 Hunc sequitur forma melior, tunc fortis in armis,
>
> 234 belli causa Paris, patriae funesta ruina,
>
> 235 Deiphobusque Helenusque simul fortisque Polites

Hector is referred to again as *Hunc*. Another warrior follows after him, told with *sequitur*. He is handsomer in appearance, described as *forma melior*, though who he is is not yet said. At this moment he shows his courage, marked by *tunc*. He stands brave before the host, praised as *fortis*. His valor is displayed in warfare, spoken of as *in armis*. He is the origin of the conflict, designated as *belli causa*. What was described as *forma melior* is revealed as Paris, entering as *Paris*. His burden falls upon his country, expressed by *patriae*, though what he brings upon it is not yet said. What was expressed by *patriae* is revealed as deadly ruin, closing the line as *funesta ruina*.

Another royal brother joins the march, named *Deiphobusque*. A second brother is added, appearing as *Helenusque*. They advance together, accompanied as *simul*. Another companion joins them, valiant in spirit, praised as *fortisque*. That brother comes forward, closing the line as *Polites*.
