### 204 (*Iliad* 2.631, 2.637)

#### Line 204

> 204 et bis sex Ithaci naues sollertia duxit,

More follows, joined by *et*. The count is twice six, given as *bis sex*. Someone is from Ithaca, named *Ithaci*. As the commentary says, the Ithacan is Ulysses. Ships come next, named *naues*. What belongs to the Ithacan is his resourcefulness, named *sollertia*. It led them, which closes the line as *duxit*.
