### 204 (*Iliad* 2.631, 2.637)

#### Line 204

> 204 et bis sex Ithaci naues sollertia duxit,

Another contingent is joined by *et*, bringing twelve in all, counted as *bis sex*. The commander is from Ithaca, appearing as *Ithaci*. As the commentary says, this Ithacan is Ulysses. His fleet consists of ships, named *naues*. His ingenuity guides the voyage, described as *sollertia*. It led them across the sea, closing the line as *duxit*.
