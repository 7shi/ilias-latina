### 244 (*Iliad* 2.864)

#### Line 244

> 244 cum quibus et Mesthles atque Antiphus et bonus armis

Others come with these, given as *cum quibus*. One is added, marked by *et*. He is Mesthles, named *Mesthles*. Another joins him, marked by *atque*. He is Antiphus, named *Antiphus*. A third is added, marked by *et*. He is good, described as *bonus*, though who he is comes only in the next section. He is good in arms, which close the line as *armis*.
