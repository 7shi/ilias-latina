### 244 (*Iliad* 2.864)

#### Line 244

> 244 cum quibus et Mesthles atque Antiphus et bonus armis

More allies march in company with those just named, linked as *cum quibus*. Another commander is added by *et*, entering as *Mesthles*. His comrade is joined to him by *atque*, appearing as *Antiphus*. A further warrior is added by *et*, skilled in warfare, described as *bonus*, though who it is is not yet said. His prowess is proven in battle, closing the line as *armis*.
