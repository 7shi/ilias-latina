### 212 (*Iliad* 2.620–621)

#### Line 212

> 212 Amphimachusque ferox et Thalpius, Elide nati,

Amphimachus comes next, as *Amphimachusque*. He is fierce, described as *ferox*. More follows, joined by *et*. Then Thalpius, named *Thalpius*. Elis comes in, named *Elide*. They were born there, which closes the line as *nati*.
