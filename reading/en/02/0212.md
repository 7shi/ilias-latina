### 212 (*Iliad* 2.620–621)

#### Line 212

> 212 Amphimachusque ferox et Thalpius, Elide nati,

Another commander is added to the catalogue, appearing as *Amphimachusque*. His spirit is fierce, described as *ferox*. A comrade is joined to him by *et*. That fellow leader comes forward as *Thalpius*. They come from Elis, named *Elide*. Both were born there, closing the line as *nati*.
