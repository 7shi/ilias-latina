### 236 (*Iliad* 2.819–821)

#### Line 236

> 236 et sacer Aeneas, Veneris certissima proles,

Another leader is joined by *et*, holy in his standing, described as *sacer*. That hero enters under his own name, appearing as *Aeneas*. He is linked to the goddess Venus, designated as *Veneris*. He stands as her undoubted offspring, closing the line as *certissima proles*.
