### 236 (*Iliad* 2.819–821)

#### Line 236

> 236 et sacer Aeneas, Veneris certissima proles,

More follows, joined by *et*. Someone is sacred, described as *sacer*. He is Aeneas, named *Aeneas*. Venus comes in, named *Veneris*. He is her undoubted son, which closes the line as *certissima proles*.
