### 238 (*Iliad* 2.826)

#### Line 238

> 238 nec non et proles generosa Lycaonis ibat

There is more still, given as *nec non*. More follows, joined by *et*. Someone is a son, a noble one, named *proles generosa*. His father is Lycaon, named *Lycaonis*. He was marching, which closes the line as *ibat*. Who the son is comes only in the next section.
