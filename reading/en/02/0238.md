### 238 (*Iliad* 2.826)

#### Line 238

> 238 nec non et proles generosa Lycaonis ibat

Nor is the line of march complete, opened with *nec*. The host does not lack another champion, told with *non*. He is joined by *et* as a noble son, appearing as *proles generosa*. His father is Lycaon, designated as *Lycaonis*. As the commentary says, the son of Lycaon is Pandarus. He marched forward with the army, closing the line as *ibat*.
