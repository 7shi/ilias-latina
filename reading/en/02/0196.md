### 196 (*Iliad* 2.653–654)

#### Line 196

> 196 Tlepolemusque nouem Rhodius, quos uiribus acer

Tlepolemus is next, as *Tlepolemusque*. His count is nine, given as *nouem*. He is from Rhodes, described as *Rhodius*. Teucer and Tlepolemus come back, as *quos*. Someone is keen in strength, given as *uiribus acer*. Who he is, and what he does after them, comes only in the next section.
