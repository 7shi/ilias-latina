### 196 (*Iliad* 2.653–654)

#### Line 196

> 196 Tlepolemusque nouem Rhodius, quos uiribus acer

Another leader is added, entering as *Tlepolemusque*. His fleet numbers nine in all, counted as *nouem*. He comes from Rhodes, appearing as *Rhodius*. Those he commands are referred to as *quos*. A warrior of formidable power, he stands described as *uiribus acer*.
