### 191–192 (*Iliad* 2.685)

#### Lines 191–192

> 191 quos iuxta Graium murus comitatur Achilles
>
> 192 cum quinquaginta materna per aequora uectus.

The ones already named come back, as *quos*. Someone is beside them, given as *iuxta*. The Greeks come in, named *Graium*. He is their wall, named *murus*. He comes along with them, told with *comitatur*. He is Achilles, who closes the line as *Achilles*.

He has fifty with him, given as *cum quinquaginta*. Something is his mother's, described as *materna*, though what it is is not yet said. It is the seas he crosses, given as *per aequora*. He was carried over them, which closes the line as *uectus*.
