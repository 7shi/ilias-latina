### 191–192 (*Iliad* 2.685)

#### Lines 191–192

> 191 quos iuxta Graium murus comitatur Achilles
>
> 192 cum quinquaginta materna per aequora uectus.

The leaders just mentioned are referred to again as *quos*. Close beside them comes another warrior, positioned with *iuxta*. He stands for the Greeks, appearing as *Graium*. He serves as their protecting wall, celebrated as *murus*. He accompanies the host, told with *comitatur*. The warrior himself is Achilles, entering as *Achilles*.

He brings fifty vessels, counted as *cum quinquaginta*. His path crosses waters belonging to his mother, described as *materna*, though what they are is not yet said. What was described as *materna* is the expanse of the sea, spoken of as *per aequora*. He was carried across the water, closing the line as *uectus*.
