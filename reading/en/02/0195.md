### 195 (*Iliad* 2.671)

#### Line 195

> 195 et tribus assumptis ratibus secat aequora Teucer

More follows, joined by *et*. The count is three, given as *tribus*. They were taken on, described as *assumptis*. They are ships, named *ratibus*. Someone cuts, told with *secat*. What he cuts is the seas, named *aequora*. He is Teucer, who closes the line as *Teucer*.
