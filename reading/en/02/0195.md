### 195 (*Iliad* 2.671)

#### Line 195

> 195 et tribus assumptis ratibus secat aequora Teucer

Another commander is joined by *et*, numbering three in his force, counted as *tribus*. These he has gathered to himself, expressed by *assumptis*. They cross the water in ships, spoken of as *ratibus*. He cuts through the water, described as *secat*. His course lies across the open sea, entering as *aequora*. The warrior who leads them is Teucer, closing the line as *Teucer*.
