### 197–198 (*Iliad* 2.713–714, 2.557)

#### Lines 197–198

> 197 Eumelus sequitur, minus una naue profectus
>
> 198 quam duxit Telamone satus Salaminius Aiax.

The next commander is Eumelus, appearing as *Eumelus*. He follows in the catalogue, told with *sequitur*. His number is smaller, marked by *minus*. He brought one ship fewer, counted as *una naue*. He set out on the expedition, described as *profectus*.

The comparison is drawn with *quam*. The other leader led his fleet forward, expressed by *duxit*. That warrior is the son of Telamon, entered as *Telamone satus*. His island home is Salamis, designated as *Salaminius*. He is called by his own name, closing the line as *Aiax*.
