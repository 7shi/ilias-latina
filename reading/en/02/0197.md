### 197–198 (*Iliad* 2.713–714, 2.557)

#### Lines 197–198

> 197 Eumelus sequitur, minus una naue profectus
>
> 198 quam duxit Telamone satus Salaminius Aiax.

The keen one is Eumelus, named *Eumelus*. He follows them, told with *sequitur*. Something is fewer, marked by *minus*. It is one ship, given as *una naue*. He set out, described as *profectus*.

Another is set against him, marked by *quam*. That one led, told with *duxit*. He was born of Telamon, given as *Telamone satus*. He is of Salamis, described as *Salaminius*. The man of Salamis is Ajax, who closes the line as *Aiax*.
