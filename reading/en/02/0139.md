### 139 (*Iliad* 2.236, 2.244)

#### Line 139

> 139 uertere iter, quem consiliis illustris Vlixes

What Thersites urges is to turn, told with *uertere*. What they are to turn is their course, named *iter*. Then Thersites comes back, as *quem*. Someone has counsels, named *consiliis*. He is renowned for them, described as *illustris*. He is Ulysses, who closes the line as *Vlixes*. What Ulysses does to Thersites comes only in the next section.
