### 139 (*Iliad* 2.236, 2.244)

#### Line 139

> 139 uertere iter, quem consiliis illustris Vlixes

The men are urged to change their path, told with *uertere*. What they must turn is their course, named *iter*. Thersites himself is now confronted by another, referred to as *quem*. The man who stands against him is renowned for wisdom, described as *consiliis illustris*. This leader is Ulysses, closing the line as *Vlixes*, though what he does is not yet said.
