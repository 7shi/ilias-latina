### 246 (*Iliad* 2.858)

#### Line 246

> 246 Arsinooque sati Chromiusque atque Ennomus, ambo

The father of the next leaders is added, appearing as *Arsinooque*. The sons born of his stock are spoken of as *sati*. The first brother joins the catalogue, entering as *Chromiusque*. A second commander is linked by *atque*, introduced as *Ennomus*. Both warriors stand together, closing the line as *ambo*.
