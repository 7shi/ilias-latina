### 246 (*Iliad* 2.858)

#### Line 246

> 246 Arsinooque sati Chromiusque atque Ennomus, ambo

Arsinous comes in, as *Arsinooque*. Some are born of him, described as *sati*. Chromius is one, as *Chromiusque*. Another joins him, marked by *atque*. He is Ennomus, named *Ennomus*. The two are taken together, which closes the line as *ambo*. What the two are comes only in the next section.
