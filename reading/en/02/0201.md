### 201 (*Iliad* 2.627)

#### Line 201

> 201 Dulichiusque Meges, animisque insignis et armis,

Someone is of Dulichium, as *Dulichiusque*. He is Meges, named *Meges*. Courage comes in, named *animisque*. He is distinguished, described as *insignis*. More follows, joined by *et*. He is distinguished in arms too, which close the line as *armis*.
