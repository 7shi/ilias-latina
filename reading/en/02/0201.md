### 201 (*Iliad* 2.627)

#### Line 201

> 201 Dulichiusque Meges, animisque insignis et armis,

Another leader is added, named from his island as *Dulichiusque*. The warrior himself is Meges, entering as *Meges*. He is celebrated for courage, joined as *animisque*. He stands out among his peers, described as *insignis*. His renown extends also to battle, added by *et* as *armis*.
