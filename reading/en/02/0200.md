### 200 (*Iliad* 2.540)

#### Line 200

> 200 Euboeae magnis Elephenor finibus ortus

The homeland named next is Euboea, appearing as *Euboeae*. Its expanse is broad, described as *magnis*. The leader who rules there is Elephenor, entering as *Elephenor*. He comes from within its lands, spoken of as *finibus*. His origin there is affirmed with *ortus*.
