### 200 (*Iliad* 2.540)

#### Line 200

> 200 Euboeae magnis Elephenor finibus ortus

Euboea comes in, named *Euboeae*. Something is broad, described as *magnis*, though what is broad is not yet said. The one who comes with Prothous is Elephenor, named *Elephenor*. What was described as *magnis* is the lands of Euboea, named *finibus*. He was born there, which closes the line as *ortus*.
