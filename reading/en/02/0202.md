### 202–203 (*Iliad* 2.638, 2.545, 2.630, 2.644, 2.759)

#### Lines 202–203

> 202 Aetola de gente Thoas Andraemone natus,
>
> 203 hi quadragenas omnes duxere carinas;

Something is Aetolian, described as *Aetola*, though what it is is not yet said. It is the people he comes from, given as *de gente*. He is Thoas, named *Thoas*. Andraemon comes next, named *Andraemone*. Thoas is his son, which closes the line as *natus*.
All those just named come together, pointed to as *hi*. The count is forty each, given as *quadragenas*, though of what is not yet said. It is all of them, named *omnes*. They led them, told with *duxere*. The forties are ships, named by their keels as *carinas*.
