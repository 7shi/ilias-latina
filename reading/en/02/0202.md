### 202–203 (*Iliad* 2.638, 2.545, 2.630, 2.644, 2.759)

#### Lines 202–203

> 202 Aetola de gente Thoas Andraemone natus,
>
> 203 hi quadragenas omnes duxere carinas;

His stock is Aetolian, described as *Aetola*. He comes from among their people, spoken of as *de gente*. The warrior himself is Thoas, entering as *Thoas*. He is named after his father Andraemon, appearing as *Andraemone*. He stands as his son, closing the line as *natus*.

These commanders are gathered together as *hi*. Each brought forty in his contingent, counted as *quadragenas*, though of what is not yet said. Every one of them shares in the total, included as *omnes*. They led them forth to war, told with *duxere*. What was counted as *quadragenas* is revealed as the ships, shown by their keels as *carinas*.
