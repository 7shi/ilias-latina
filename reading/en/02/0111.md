### 111–112 (*Iliad* 2.1–2)

#### Lines 111–112

> 111 Nox erat et toto fulgebant sidera mundo
>
> 112 humanumque genus requies diuumque tenebat,

Darkness settles over the scene, arriving as *Nox*. That quiet time had come, given as *erat*. Light also appears across the expanse, joined by *et* and described as *toto*, though what is encompassed is not yet said. Brightness gleams above, told with *fulgebant*. The lights that shine are the stars, appearing as *sidera*. What was described as *toto* is revealed as the world of the sky, closing the line as *mundo*.

The scene extends also to humankind, described as *humanumque genus*. A calm settles over them, named *requies*. The gods also share in it, returning as *diuumque*. This quiet holds them all, which closes the line as *tenebat*.
