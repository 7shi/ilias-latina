### 152–153 (*Iliad* 2.322, 2.328–330)

#### Lines 152–153

> 152 in decimo labor est, Calchas quem dixerat, anno,
>
> 153 quo caderet Danaum uictricibus Ilion armis."

The time of completion is marked by a tenth period, told as *in decimo*, though what unit of time it is is not yet said. The appointed hardship is the toil of war, named *labor*. It is destined to come upon them, expressed with *est*. The prophet who foretold it is Calchas, appearing as *Calchas*. The toil is referred to again as *quem*. He had spoken of it beforehand, declared with *dixerat*. What was described as *in decimo* is revealed as the year, closing the line as *anno*.

The moment of victory is marked by *quo*. The enemy city is doomed to fall, reported with *caderet*. The triumph belongs to the Danaans, spoken of as *Danaum*. Their assault is victorious, described as *uictricibus*. The stronghold that must perish is Ilion, named *Ilion*. The fall will be accomplished by weapons of war, closing the speech as *armis*.
