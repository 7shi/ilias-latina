### 185–186 (*Iliad* 2.567–568)

#### Lines 185–186

> 185 Tydides ualido pulsarunt remige pontum:
>
> 186 bis quadragenas onerarunt milite puppes;

The warrior declared brave in battle is the son of Tydeus, appearing as *Tydides*. As the commentary says, this son of Tydeus is Diomedes. The power behind the ships is sturdy, described as *ualido*. Together the leaders beat against the sea, told with *pulsarunt*. What was described as *ualido* is their crew of rowers, named *remige*. They strike the open deep, closing the line as *pontum*.

Their total fleet numbers eighty, counted as *bis quadragenas*, though of what is not yet said. They loaded them heavily, expressed by *onerarunt*. The force aboard was their soldiers, spoken of as *milite*. What was counted as *bis quadragenas* is revealed as the ships, shown by their sterns as *puppes*.
