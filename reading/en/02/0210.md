### 210–211 (*Iliad* 2.552, 2.556, 2.685)

#### Lines 210–211

> 210 et totidem puppes clara de gente Menestheus
>
> 211 duxit Athenaeus, quot uiribus ambit Achilles;

More follows, joined by *et*. As many come next, given as *totidem*. They are ships, named by their sterns as *puppes*. Something is renowned, described as *clara*. It is a people, given as *de gente*. Menestheus comes from it, closing the line as *Menestheus*.
He led them, told with *duxit*. He is an Athenian, described as *Athenaeus*. The count is set against another man's, marked by *quot*. That count is in his force, named *uiribus*. He commands so many, told with *ambit*. The man is Achilles, who closes the line as *Achilles*.
