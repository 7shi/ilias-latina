### 210–211 (*Iliad* 2.552, 2.556, 2.685)

#### Lines 210–211

> 210 et totidem puppes clara de gente Menestheus
>
> 211 duxit Athenaeus, quot uiribus ambit Achilles;

Another contingent is joined by *et*, matching in number as *totidem*. These vessels are his ships, shown by their sterns as *puppes*. He comes from a renowned race, described as *clara*, though what people it is is not yet said. He springs from among their stock, spoken of as *de gente*. The leader is Menestheus, closing the line as *Menestheus*.

He led them forth across the sea, told with *duxit*. His homeland is Athens, designated as *Athenaeus*. The total corresponds to another fleet, marked by *quot*. It matches the strength in arms, appearing as *uiribus*. That commander encompasses such might, expressed by *ambit*. The warrior he is compared to is Achilles, closing the line as *Achilles*.
