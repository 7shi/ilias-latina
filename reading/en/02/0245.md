### 245 (*Iliad* 2.840, 2.844)

#### Line 245

> 245 Hippothous uenere Acamasque et Pirous una,

The warrior praised as *bonus armis* comes forward, entering as *Hippothous*. He and his companions arrived on the field, told with *uenere*. Another leader joins the ranks, appearing as *Acamasque*. His comrade is added by *et*, named *Pirous*. They advanced together, closing the line as *una*.
