### 245 (*Iliad* 2.840, 2.844)

#### Line 245

> 245 Hippothous uenere Acamasque et Pirous una,

The one good in arms is Hippothous, named *Hippothous*. They came, told with *uenere*. Acamas too, as *Acamasque*. More follows, joined by *et*. He is Pirous, named *Pirous*. They came together, which closes the line as *una*.
