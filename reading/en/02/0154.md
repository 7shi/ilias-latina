### 154 (*Iliad* 2.333–335)

#### Line 154

> 154 Assensere omnes, laudatur Nestoris aetas

Approval spreads through the ranks, told with *Assensere*. Every man joins in this agreement, appearing as *omnes*. Honor is paid to the speaker, commended with *laudatur*. The one they acclaim is Nestor, named *Nestoris*. What they honor in him is his ripe age, closing the line as *aetas*.
