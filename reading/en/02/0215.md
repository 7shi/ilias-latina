### 215–216 (*Iliad* 2.698, 2.704, 2.710, 2.534)

#### Lines 215–216

> 215 Protesilaus agit totidem fortisque Podarces
>
> 216 instructas puppes, quot duxit Oileos Aiax;

Protesilaus comes next, named *Protesilaus*. He leads, told with *agit*. He has as many, given as *totidem*, though as many of what is not yet said. Someone brave comes with him, described as *fortisque*. He is Podarces, who closes the line as *Podarces*.

What he had as many of is ships, fully equipped, given as *instructas puppes*. The count is matched to another man's, marked by *quot*. That one led, told with *duxit*. He is the son of Oileus, named *Oileos*. He is Ajax, who closes the line as *Aiax*.
