### 215–216 (*Iliad* 2.698, 2.704, 2.710, 2.534)

#### Lines 215–216

> 215 Protesilaus agit totidem fortisque Podarces
>
> 216 instructas puppes, quot duxit Oileos Aiax;

The catalogue turns to Protesilaus, entering as *Protesilaus*. He leads his men forth, told with *agit*. His tally matches an equal number, described as *totidem*, though of what is not yet said. Another warrior joins the command, brave in battle, praised as *fortisque*. That comrade comes forward as *Podarces*.

What was described as *totidem* is revealed as fully equipped ships, appearing as *instructas puppes*. The count is compared to another fleet, marked by *quot*. That other fleet was led by its commander, expressed by *duxit*. That commander is named after his father Oileus, designated as *Oileos*. Only here is he called by his own name, closing the line as *Aiax*.
