### 190 (*Iliad* 2.736–737)

#### Line 190

> 190 instruxit puppes totidemque Euhaemone natus,

Ajax fitted them out, told with *instruxit*. The forty are ships, named by their sterns as *puppes*. As many more come next, given as *totidemque*. Euhaemon comes in, named *Euhaemone*. Someone is his son, who closes the line as *natus*. As the commentary says, the son of Euhaemon is Eurypylus.
