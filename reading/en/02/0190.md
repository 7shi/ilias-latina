### 190 (*Iliad* 2.736–737)

#### Line 190

> 190 instruxit puppes totidemque Euhaemone natus,

He fitted them out for the crossing, told with *instruxit*. These vessels are his ships, shown by their sterns as *puppes*. An equal fleet is added, matched in number as *totidemque*. The new leader is known after his father Euhaemon, appearing as *Euhaemone*. He comes forward as his son, closing the line as *natus*. As the commentary says, the son of Euhaemon is Eurypylus.
