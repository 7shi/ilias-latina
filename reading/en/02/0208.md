### 208–209 (*Iliad* 2.645, 2.650–652)

#### Lines 208–209

> 208 Idomeneus et Meriones, Cretaeus uterque,
>
> 209 bis quadragenis muniti nauibus ibant;

Idomeneus comes first, named *Idomeneus*. More follows, joined by *et*. Then Meriones, named *Meriones*. Each is a Cretan, given as *Cretaeus uterque*.
The count is twice forty, given as *bis quadragenis*. They were backed by them, described as *muniti*. The eighty are ships, named *nauibus*. So they went forth, which closes the line as *ibant*.
