### 208–209 (*Iliad* 2.645, 2.650–652)

#### Lines 208–209

> 208 Idomeneus et Meriones, Cretaeus uterque,
>
> 209 bis quadragenis muniti nauibus ibant;

The first commander is Idomeneus, appearing as *Idomeneus*. His comrade is joined by *et*, named *Meriones*. Both are men of Crete, designated as *Cretaeus uterque*.

Their contingent numbers eighty, counted as *bis quadragenis*. They are supported by this strength, described as *muniti*. Their fleet consists of ships, spoken of as *nauibus*. Together they went forth, closing the line as *ibant*.
