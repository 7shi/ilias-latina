### 242 (*Iliad* 2.856)

#### Line 242

> 242 magnanimique duces Odiusque et Epistrophus ingens

There are great-hearted leaders, named *magnanimique duces*. Odius is one, as *Odiusque*. Another is added, marked by *et*. He is Epistrophus, named *Epistrophus*. He is huge, which closes the line as *ingens*.
