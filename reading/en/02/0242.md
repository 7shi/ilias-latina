### 242 (*Iliad* 2.856)

#### Line 242

> 242 magnanimique duces Odiusque et Epistrophus ingens

More commanders join the allied march, great-hearted leaders, spoken of as *magnanimique duces*. The first of them enters the catalogue, appearing as *Odiusque*. His comrade is added by *et*, named *Epistrophus*. He is a warrior of mighty stature, closing the line as *ingens*.
