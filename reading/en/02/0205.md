### 205 (*Iliad* 2.557)

#### Line 205

> 205 quem sequitur totidem ratibus Telamonius Aiax,

The leader just named is referred to as *quem*. Another commander follows behind him, told with *sequitur*. He matches him with an equal count, described as *totidem*. He brings his force in ships, spoken of as *ratibus*. He is distinguished after his father Telamon, appearing as *Telamonius*. He is called by his own name, closing the line as *Aiax*.
