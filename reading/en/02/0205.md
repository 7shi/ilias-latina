### 205 (*Iliad* 2.557)

#### Line 205

> 205 quem sequitur totidem ratibus Telamonius Aiax,

The Ithacan comes back, as *quem*. Someone follows him, told with *sequitur*. He has as many, given as *totidem*. They are ships, named *ratibus*. He is Telamon's son, described as *Telamonius*. He is Ajax, who closes the line as *Aiax*.
