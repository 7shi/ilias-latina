### 239 (*Iliad* 2.827, 2.876)

#### Line 239

> 239 Pandarus et magnae Glaucus uirtutis in armis

The noble warrior from the previous verse is called by his own name, entering as *Pandarus*. Another ally is joined by *et*, distinguished by high renown, described as *magnae*, though what quality it is is not yet said. The warrior himself comes forward as *Glaucus*. What was described as *magnae* is revealed as his valor, spoken of as *uirtutis*. His prowess is displayed in battle, closing the line as *in armis*.
