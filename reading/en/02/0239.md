### 239 (*Iliad* 2.827, 2.876)

#### Line 239

> 239 Pandarus et magnae Glaucus uirtutis in armis

The son of Lycaon is Pandarus, named *Pandarus*. More follows, joined by *et*. Something is great, described as *magnae*, though what is great is not yet said. Glaucus comes in, named *Glaucus*. What was described as *magnae* is his valor, named *uirtutis*. It is in arms, which close the line as *in armis*.
