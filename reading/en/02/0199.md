### 199 (*Iliad* 2.756)

#### Line 199

> 199 Ast Prothous Magnes Tenthredone natus et una

The catalogue turns, marked by *Ast*. Prothous comes in, named *Prothous*. He is a Magnesian, called *Magnes*. Tenthredon comes next, named *Tenthredone*. Prothous is his son, given as *natus*. More follows, joined by *et*. Someone comes with him, marked by *una*; who it is comes only in the next section.
