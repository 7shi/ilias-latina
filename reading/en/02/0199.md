### 199 (*Iliad* 2.756)

#### Line 199

> 199 Ast Prothous Magnes Tenthredone natus et una

A contrast turns to another commander, marked by *Ast*. The leader is Prothous, entering as *Prothous*. He is a Magnesian, designated as *Magnes*. He is named after his father Tenthredon, appearing as *Tenthredone*. He stands as his son, told with *natus*. Others are joined by *et* to march together with him, closing the line as *una*.
