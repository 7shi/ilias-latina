### 243 (*Iliad* 2.846, 2.848)

#### Line 243

> 243 Euphemusque ferox clarusque aetate Pyraechmes,

Euphemus comes next, as *Euphemusque*. He is fierce, described as *ferox*. Someone is famed, described as *clarusque*, though who it is is not yet said. He is famed for his years, named *aetate*. The famed one is Pyraechmes, who closes the line as *Pyraechmes*.
