### 243 (*Iliad* 2.846, 2.848)

#### Line 243

> 243 Euphemusque ferox clarusque aetate Pyraechmes,

Another commander joins the Trojan allies, entering as *Euphemusque*. His spirit is fierce, described as *ferox*. A comrade is also distinguished by renown, praised as *clarusque*. His distinction is born of his years, spoken of as *aetate*. That elder warrior comes forward, closing the line as *Pyraechmes*.
