### 237 (*Iliad* 2.822–823)

#### Line 237

> 237 Archelochusque Acamasque ferox Antenore creti;

Archelochus comes next, as *Archelochusque*. Acamas too, as *Acamasque*. He is fierce, described as *ferox*. Antenor comes in, named *Antenore*. The two are born of him, which closes the line as *creti*.
