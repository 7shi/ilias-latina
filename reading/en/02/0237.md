### 237 (*Iliad* 2.822–823)

#### Line 237

> 237 Archelochusque Acamasque ferox Antenore creti;

Another leader joins the ranks, entering as *Archelochusque*. A comrade is added beside him, appearing as *Acamasque*. His spirit is fierce, described as *ferox*. They are known after their father Antenor, designated as *Antenore*. Both are sprung from his stock, closing the line as *creti*.
