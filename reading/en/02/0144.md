### 144–146 (*Iliad* 2.278–284, 2.299–302)

#### Lines 144–146

> 144 Tandem sollertis prudentia Nestoris aeuo
>
> 145 compressam miti sedauit pectore turbam
>
> 146 admonuitque duces dictis responsa recordans

At last something changes, marked by *Tandem*. Someone is resourceful, described as *sollertis*, though who it is is not yet said. Wisdom comes next, named *prudentia*. The resourceful one is Nestor, named *Nestoris*. The wisdom comes with his age, which closes the line as *aeuo*.

Something is held down, described as *compressam*, though what it is is not yet said. It is with something gentle, described as *miti*. The wisdom calmed it, told with *sedauit*. The gentle thing is his spirit, named *pectore*. What was described as *compressam* is the crowd, which closes the line as *turbam*.

The wisdom also warned someone, told with *admonuitque*. They are the leaders, named *duces*. It was with words, named *dictis*. There are prophecies, named *responsa*. Nestor is recalling them, which closes the line as *recordans*.
