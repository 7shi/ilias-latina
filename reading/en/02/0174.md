### 174 (*Iliad* 2.586–587)

#### Line 174

> 174 et bis tricenis Menelai nauibus ardor

More follows, joined by *et*. The count is twice thirty, given as *bis tricenis*. Menelaus comes in, named *Menelai*. Ships come next, named *nauibus*. What belongs to Menelaus is his fire, which closes the line as *ardor*. What his fire does comes only in the next section.
