### 174 (*Iliad* 2.586–587)

#### Line 174

> 174 et bis tricenis Menelai nauibus ardor

Another force is joined by *et*, numbering sixty, counted as *bis tricenis*. The leader who brings them is Menelaus, appearing as *Menelai*. They cross the sea in ships, spoken of as *nauibus*. His fiery eagerness stands for the man himself, closing the line as *ardor*.
