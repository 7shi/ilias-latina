### 171–173 (*Iliad* 2.569, 2.579–580, 2.576)

#### Lines 171–173

> 171 Inde Mycenaeis Agamemnon moenibus ortus,
>
> 172 quem sibi bellatrix delegit Graecia regem,
>
> 173 centum egit plenas armato milite puppes;

The account moves to the next leader, marked by *Inde*. His origins belong to Mycenae, described as *Mycenaeis*. The commander himself is named directly, appearing as *Agamemnon*. He was born within its walls, spoken of as *moenibus*. His birth there is confirmed with *ortus*. He is pointed to as the one chosen, opening the clause with *quem*. The choice was made for its own sake, entered as *sibi*. The power that made the choice is warlike Greece, designated as *bellatrix Graecia*. It selected him for itself, told with *delegit*. It set him over the land as its king, named *regem*.

The count of his contingent reaches one hundred, stated as *centum*, though what is numbered is not yet said. He brought them forth, told with *egit*. They were filled to capacity, recorded as *plenas*. The force within was composed of armed troops, represented as *armato milite*. What was counted as *centum* is revealed as the ships, shown by their sterns as *puppes*.
