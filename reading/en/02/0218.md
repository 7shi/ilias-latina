### 218–222 (*Iliad* 2.731–733)

#### Lines 218–219

> 218 quem sequitur iuxta Podalirius atque Machaon,
>
> 219 altaque ter denis sulcarunt aequora proris.

The leader just named is referred to as *quem*. Others follow after him, expressed by *sequitur*. They come close beside him, marked by *iuxta*. The first of these commanders enters as *Podalirius*. His companion is joined to him by *atque*, closing the line as *Machaon*.

Their passage also leads across deep waters, characterized as *altaque*, though what is deep is not yet said. Their tally numbers thirty, counted as *ter denis*, though of what is not yet said. They plowed their way through, told with *sulcarunt*. What was characterized as *altaque* is the open sea, spoken of as *aequora*. What was counted as *ter denis* is revealed as their ships, shown by their prows as *proris*.

#### Lines 220–221

> 220 His ducibus Graiae Troiana ad litora puppes
>
> 221 bis septem uenere minus quam mille ducentae.

The host marches under the commanders assembled so far, appearing as *His ducibus*. The forces that gather are Greek, described as *Graiae*, though what of theirs comes is not yet said. Their course is set toward what belongs to Troy, designated as *Troiana*, though where they land is not yet said. What was designated as *Troiana* is reached at the shore, spoken of as *ad litora*. What was described as *Graiae* is revealed as their ships, shown by their sterns as *puppes*.

Their count is fourteen short, measured as *bis septem*. All of them arrived at their destination, told with *uenere*. They fell below that larger total, expressed by *minus*. The comparison is drawn with *quam*. That full total was twelve hundred, closing the line as *mille ducentae*.

#### Line 222

> 222 Iamque citi appulerant classes camposque tenebant,

The action moves to what had already taken place, joined with *Iamque*. Their advance was swift, described as *citi*. They had brought their force to land, declared with *appulerant*. What they brought to shore were their fleets, entering as *classes*. They spread also across the open ground, designated as *camposque*. They held that expanse, standing at the end of the line as *tenebant*.
