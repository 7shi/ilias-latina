### 129 (*Iliad* 2.35)

#### Line 129

> 129 Dixit, et has repetit per quas modo uenerat auras.

He has spoken, told with *Dixit*. More follows, joined by *et*. Something is pointed to as *has*, though what it is is not yet said. He goes back to it, told with *repetit*. It is the way he passed through, given as *per quas*. That was only just now, marked by *modo*. He had come by it, told with *uenerat*. What was pointed to as *has* is the air, which closes the line as *auras*.
