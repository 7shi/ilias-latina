### 129 (*Iliad* 2.35)

#### Line 129

> 129 Dixit, et has repetit per quas modo uenerat auras.

The speech comes to an end, told with *Dixit*. He turns to something already known, joined by *et* and pointed to with *has*, though what it is is not yet said. He seeks it again, expressed with *repetit*. His journey retraces a path, passing *per quas*. That journey had taken place just moments before, marked by *modo*. Along this way he had come, described with *uenerat*. What was pointed to with *has* is revealed as the breezes of the sky, closing the line as *auras*.
