### 213–214 (*Iliad* 2.622–623, 2.618–619)

#### Lines 213–214

> 213 et clara uirtute Polyxenus atque Diores,
>
> 214 hi bis uicenas onerarunt milite puppes.

More is added to the catalogue by *et*, celebrated for renown in valor as *clara uirtute*. The first warrior is Polyxenus, named *Polyxenus*. His comrade is linked to him by *atque*, closing the line as *Diores*.

All these leaders are gathered together, spoken of as *hi*. Their tally numbers forty, counted as *bis uicenas*, though of what is not yet said. They carried out the loading, told with *onerarunt*. The force placed aboard was soldiers, appearing as *milite*. What was counted as *bis uicenas* is revealed as their ships, shown by their sterns as *puppes*.
