### 213–214 (*Iliad* 2.622–623, 2.618–619)

#### Lines 213–214

> 213 et clara uirtute Polyxenus atque Diores,
>
> 214 hi bis uicenas onerarunt milite puppes.

More follows, joined by *et*. Someone is renowned for valor, given as *clara uirtute*. Polyxenus comes in, named *Polyxenus*. Another joins him, marked by *atque*. He is Diores, who closes the line as *Diores*.
All of them come together, pointed to as *hi*. The count is twice twenty, given as *bis uicenas*, though of what is not yet said. They loaded them, told with *onerarunt*. It was with soldiers, named *milite*. The forty are ships, named by their sterns as *puppes*.
