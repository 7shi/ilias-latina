### 187–188 (*Iliad* 2.512, 2.516)

#### Lines 187–188

> 187 Ascalaphusque potens et Ialmenus, acer uterque,
>
> 188 ter denas ualido complerunt remige naues

Another leader enters, named *Ascalaphusque*. He is mighty in battle, described as *potens*. His brother is joined by *et*, appearing as *Ialmenus*. Both are fierce warriors, designated as *acer uterque*.

Their contingent counts thirty, numbered as *ter denas*, though of what is not yet said. Power is put to work, represented as *ualido*. They manned the vessels fully, told with *complerunt*. What was described as *ualido* is their sturdy oarsmen, entering as *remige*. What was numbered as *ter denas* is revealed as the ships, closing the line as *naues*.
