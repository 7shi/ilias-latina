### 187–188 (*Iliad* 2.512, 2.516)

#### Lines 187–188

> 187 Ascalaphusque potens et Ialmenus, acer uterque,
>
> 188 ter denas ualido complerunt remige naues

Ascalaphus is next, as *Ascalaphusque*. He is mighty, described as *potens*. More follows, joined by *et*. Then Ialmenus, named *Ialmenus*. Each of them is valiant, given as *acer uterque*.

The count is three tens, given as *ter denas*, though of what is not yet said. Something is strong, described as *ualido*. They filled them, told with *complerunt*. The strong ones are oarsmen, named *remige*. The thirty are ships, which close the line as *naues*.
