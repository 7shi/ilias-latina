### 206–207 (*Iliad* 2.748)

#### Lines 206–207

> 206 egregia uirtute potens; simul horrida Guneus
>
> 207 ire bis undenis temptabat in arma carinis.

His valor is outstanding, given as *egregia uirtute*. He is mighty in it, described as *potens*. At the same time something else comes, marked by *simul*. Something is grim, described as *horrida*, though what is grim is not yet said. Guneus comes in, closing the line as *Guneus*.
He is to go, told with *ire*. The count is twice eleven, given as *bis undenis*, though of what is not yet said. He tried it, told with *temptabat*. What was described as *horrida* is war, given as *in arma*. The twenty-two are ships, named by their keels as *carinis*.
