### 206–207 (*Iliad* 2.748)

#### Lines 206–207

> 206 egregia uirtute potens; simul horrida Guneus
>
> 207 ire bis undenis temptabat in arma carinis.

Ajax is renowned for outstanding courage, celebrated as *egregia uirtute*. He stands mighty among the Greeks, appearing as *potens*. At the same time, another commander enters, marked by *simul*. His journey is directed toward something grim, described as *horrida*, though what is grim is not yet said. The warrior himself is Guneus, closing the line as *Guneus*.

He sought to make his way, told with *ire*. His contingent numbers twenty-two, counted as *bis undenis*, though of what is not yet said. He pressed forward with his attempt, expressed by *temptabat*. What was described as *horrida* is the clash of war, spoken of as *in arma*. What was counted as *bis undenis* is revealed as the ships, shown by their keels as *carinis*.
