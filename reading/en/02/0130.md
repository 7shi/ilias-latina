### 130–131 (*Iliad* 2.48–53)

#### Line 130

> 130 Interea lucem terris dedit ignea lampas.

Time moves on, marked by *Interea*. Light comes next, named *lucem*. It is for the earth, named *terris*. Something gave it, told with *dedit*. The giver is a fiery lamp, which closes the line as *ignea lampas*.

#### Line 131

> 131 Conuocat attonitus iussis Pelopeius heros

Someone calls others together, told with *Conuocat*. He is awestruck, described as *attonitus*. What struck him were the commands, named *iussis*. He is the hero of Pelops' line, who closes the line as *Pelopeius heros*. As the commentary says, the Pelopeian hero is Agamemnon. Whom he calls comes only in the next section.
