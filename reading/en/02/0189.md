### 189 (*Iliad* 2.527, 2.534)

#### Line 189

> 189 et bis uicenas Locrum fortissimus Aiax

More follows, joined by *et*. The count is twice twenty, given as *bis uicenas*. The Locrians come in, named *Locrum*. Someone is the bravest of them, described as *fortissimus*. He is Ajax, who closes the line as *Aiax*. As the commentary says, this Ajax is the son of Oileus. What the forty are comes only in the next section.
