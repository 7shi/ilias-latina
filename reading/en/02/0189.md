### 189 (*Iliad* 2.527, 2.534)

#### Line 189

> 189 et bis uicenas Locrum fortissimus Aiax

A further contingent is joined by *et*, numbering forty, counted as *bis uicenas*. They belong to the Locrians, appearing as *Locrum*. Their champion is praised as the bravest among them, described as *fortissimus*. The warrior himself is Ajax, closing the line as *Aiax*.
