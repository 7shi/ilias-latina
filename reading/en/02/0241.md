### 241 (*Iliad* 2.870–871)

#### Line 241

> 241 Ibat et Amphimachus Nastesque, insignis uterque,

Someone was marching, told with *Ibat*. More follows, joined by *et*. It is Amphimachus, named *Amphimachus*. Nastes is with him, as *Nastesque*. Each of them is distinguished, given as *insignis uterque*.
