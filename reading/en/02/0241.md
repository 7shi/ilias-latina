### 241 (*Iliad* 2.870–871)

#### Line 241

> 241 Ibat et Amphimachus Nastesque, insignis uterque,

The march of the allies presses forward, told with *Ibat*. Another leader is added by *et*, entering as *Amphimachus*. His brother also joins the ranks, appearing as *Nastesque*. Both warriors are distinguished, praised as *insignis uterque*.
