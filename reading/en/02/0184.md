### 184 (*Iliad* 2.565, 2.564)

#### Line 184

> 184 Euryalus Sthenelusque duces et fortis in armis

A new commander comes forward, named *Euryalus*. His companion is joined as *Sthenelusque*. Both are leaders of the contingent, appearing as *duces*. Another is added by *et*, a warrior of courage, described as *fortis*, though who is brave is not yet said. His prowess is proven in warfare, closing the line as *in armis*.
