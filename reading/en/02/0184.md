### 184 (*Iliad* 2.565, 2.564)

#### Line 184

> 184 Euryalus Sthenelusque duces et fortis in armis

Euryalus comes first, named *Euryalus*. Sthenelus is with him, as *Sthenelusque*. They are leaders, named *duces*. More follows, joined by *et*. Someone is brave, described as *fortis*, though who he is comes only in the next section. He is brave in battle, given as *in armis*.
