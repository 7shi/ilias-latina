### 240 (*Iliad* 2.830, 2.837, 2.842)

#### Line 240

> 240 Amphiusque et Adrastus et Asius atque Pylaeus.

Another ally among the Trojans joins the catalogue, entering as *Amphiusque*. His brother is added by *et*, appearing as *Adrastus*. A third commander is linked by *et*, coming forward as *Asius*. Another ally is joined to them by *atque*, closing the line as *Pylaeus*.
