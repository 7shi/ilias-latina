### 240 (*Iliad* 2.830, 2.837, 2.842)

#### Line 240

> 240 Amphiusque et Adrastus et Asius atque Pylaeus.

Amphius comes next, as *Amphiusque*. A second is added, marked by *et*. He is Adrastus, named *Adrastus*. A third is added, marked by *et* again. He is Asius, named *Asius*. The last is joined by *atque*. He is Pylaeus, who closes the line as *Pylaeus*.
