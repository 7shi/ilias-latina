### 217 (*Iliad* 2.718–719)

#### Line 217

> 217 et septem Poeante satus tulit arma carinis,

A further contingent is joined by *et*, numbering seven, counted as *septem*, though of what is not yet said. Their leader comes forward as the son of Poeas, appearing as *Poeante satus*. As the commentary says, the son of Poeas is Philoctetes. He brought his force to the gathering, told with *tulit*. What they bore for the war was weapons, spoken of as *arma*. What was counted as *septem* is revealed as their ships, shown by their keels as *carinis*.
