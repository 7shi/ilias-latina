### 217 (*Iliad* 2.718–719)

#### Line 217

> 217 et septem Poeante satus tulit arma carinis,

More follows, joined by *et*. The count is seven, given as *septem*, though of what is not yet said. Someone is born of Poeas, given as *Poeante satus*. As the commentary says, the son of Poeas is Philoctetes. He brought something, told with *tulit*. What he brought is arms, named *arma*. The seven are ships, named by their keels as *carinis*.
