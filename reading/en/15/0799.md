### 799–802 (*Iliad* 15.416–418, 15.674–677, 15.729–731, 15.742–746)

#### Lines 799–801

> 799 apparat. Huic ualidis obsistit uiribus Aiax,
>
> 800 stans prima in puppi, clipeoque incendia saeua
>
> 801 sustinet et solus defendit mille carinas.

He makes ready to do so, told with *apparat*. Hector comes back, as *Huic*. Something is mighty, described as *ualidis*, though what it is is not yet said. Someone opposes him, told with *obsistit*. What is mighty is his strength, named *uiribus*. The one who opposes him is Ajax, who closes the line as *Aiax*.

Ajax is standing, described as *stans*. Something is the very first, described as *prima*, though what it is is not yet said. What is the very first is the stern, given as *in puppi*. His shield comes in as well, named *clipeoque*. Flames come in, named *incendia*. They are savage, which closes the line as *saeua*.

He holds them off, told with *sustinet*. More is added, marked by *et*. He is alone, described as *solus*. He defends something, told with *defendit*. Some are a thousand, described as *mille*. What are a thousand are ships, which close the line as *carinas*.

#### Line 802

> 802 Hinc iaciunt Danai robustae cuspidis hastas,

A place is set, marked by *Hinc*. Some hurl something, told with *iaciunt*. The Danaans come in, named *Danai*. Sturdy points come in, given as *robustae cuspidis*. Spears come in, which close the line as *hastas*. What follows comes only in the next section.
