### 795 (*Iliad* 15.343–345)

#### Line 795

> 795 confugiunt iterum ad classes Agamemnonis alae

Some flee for refuge, told with *confugiunt*. It is again, marked by *iterum*. The fleet comes in, given as *ad classes*. Agamemnon comes in, called *Agamemnonis*, though what belongs to him is not yet said. As the commentary says, Agamemnon is the commander of the Greek expedition. Those who belong to Agamemnon are his squadrons, which close the line as *alae*.
