### 792–793 (*Iliad* 15.1–3)

#### Lines 792–793

> 792 pulsa metu uallumque et muros aggere saeptos
>
> 793 transiliunt, alii fossas uoluuntur in ipsas.

Someone is driven, described as *pulsa*. Fear comes in, given as *metu*. A rampart comes in as well, named *uallumque*. More is added, marked by *et*. Walls come in, called *muros*. An embankment comes in, named *aggere*. They are fenced, which closes the line as *saeptos*.

They leap across, told with *transiliunt*. Others come in, as *alii*. Ditches come in, named *fossas*, though which ones they are is not yet said. They roll, told with *uoluuntur*. What they roll into is the very ones, which closes the line as *in ipsas*.
