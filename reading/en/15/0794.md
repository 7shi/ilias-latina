### 794 (*Iliad* 15.279–280)

#### Line 794

> 794 Aduolat interea Danaum metus impiger Hector:

Someone flies up, told with *Aduolat*. A time is set, marked by *interea*. The Danaans come in, as *Danaum*. As the commentary says, the Danaans are the Greeks. Their terror comes in, named *metus*. Someone is tireless, described as *impiger*, though who it is is not yet said. The tireless one is Hector, who closes the line as *Hector*.
