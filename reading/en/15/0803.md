### 803–804 (*Iliad* 15.743–744, 16.109–111)

#### Line 803

> 803 illinc ardentes taedas Phryges undique iactant;

Another place is set, marked by *illinc*. Blazing torches come in, given as *ardentes taedas*. The Phrygians come in, named *Phryges*. As the commentary says, the Phrygians are the Trojans. It is from every direction, as *undique*. They hurl them, told with *iactant*.

#### Line 804

> 804 per uastos sudor pugnantum defluit artus.

Something is mighty, given as *per uastos*, though what it is is not yet said. Sweat comes in, named *sudor*. The fighters come in, described as *pugnantum*. It streams down, told with *defluit*. What is mighty is their limbs, which ends the fifteenth book as *artus*.
