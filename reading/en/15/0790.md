### 790–791 (*Iliad* 15.306–307)

#### Lines 790–791

> 790 acrius insurgunt Troes ad Achaica bella
>
> 791 \<>

Something happens more fiercely, marked by *acrius*. Some rise up, told with *insurgunt*. The Trojans come in, named *Troes*. What they rise up to is with the Achaeans, given as *ad Achaica*, though what it is is not yet said. As the commentary says, the Achaeans are the Greeks besieging Troy. What they rise up to is war, which closes the line as *bella*.

The verse that followed is lost, and with it the rest of the sentence.
