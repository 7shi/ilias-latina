### 790–791 (*Iliad* 15.306–307)

#### Lines 790–791

> 790 acrius insurgunt Troes ad Achaica bella
>
> 791 \<>

It is more fiercely, marked by *acrius*. Some rise up, told with *insurgunt*. The Trojans come in, named *Troes*. Something is Achaean, given as *ad Achaica*, though what it is is not yet said. As the commentary says, the Achaeans are the Greeks besieging Troy. What is Achaean is war, which closes the line as *bella*.

As the commentary says, the verse that followed is lost, and with it the rest of the sentence.
