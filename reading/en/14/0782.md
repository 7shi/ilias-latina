### 782 (*Iliad* 14.421–432, 14.437)

#### Line 782

> 782 Concurrit Troiana manus iuuenemque uomentem

Someone runs together, told with *Concurrit*. Someone is Trojan, described as *Troiana*, though who it is is not yet said. The Trojan one is a band, named *manus*. More is added, marked by *-que*. The young man comes in, named *iuuenem*. As the commentary says, the young man is Hector. He is vomiting, which closes the line as *uomentem*. What becomes of him comes only in the next section.
