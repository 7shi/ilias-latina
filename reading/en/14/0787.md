### 787 (*Iliad* 14.463–465)

#### Line 787

> 787 Archelochumque Antenoriden Telamonius Aiax,

Archelochus comes in as well, named *Archelochumque*. He is called *Antenoriden*. As the commentary says, Antenorides is a son of Antenor. Someone is Telamonian, described as *Telamonius*, though who it is is not yet said. The Telamonian one is Ajax, who closes the line as *Aiax*.
