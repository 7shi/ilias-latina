### 783–785 (*Iliad* 14.433–437, 14.440–441)

#### Line 783

> 783 sanguineos fluctus Xanthi lauere fluentis.

What he vomits is streams of blood, given as *sanguineos fluctus*. Xanthus comes in, named *Xanthi*, though what belongs to it is not yet said. They washed him, told with *lauere*. What belongs to Xanthus is its flowing waters, which close the line as *fluentis*.

#### Lines 784–785

> 784 Inde iterum ad pugnam redeunt; fit maxima caedes
>
> 785 amborum et manat tellus infecta cruore.

A place is set, marked by *Inde*. Something is done again, as *iterum*. The fight comes in, given as *ad pugnam*. They return, told with *redeunt*. Something takes place, told with *fit*. Very great slaughter closes the line as *maxima caedes*.

It is on both sides, described as *amborum*. More is added, marked by *et*. Something runs, told with *manat*. The earth comes in, named *tellus*. It is stained, described as *infecta*. Gore comes in, which closes the line as *cruore*.
