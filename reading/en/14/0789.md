### 789 (*Iliad* 14.487–489)

#### Line 789

> 789 Penelei dextra; inde cadit Priameia pubes

The fierce one is Peneleus, called *Penelei*. As the commentary says, Peneleus is one of the leaders of the Greeks from Boeotia. What lays Acamas low is his right hand, named *dextra*. A time is set, marked by *inde*. Someone falls, told with *cadit*. Some belong to Priam, described as *Priameia*, though who they are is not yet said. As the commentary says, Priam is Troy's king. Those who belong to Priam are the young men of his city, which ends the fourteenth book as *pubes*.
