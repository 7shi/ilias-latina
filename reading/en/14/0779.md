### 779 (*Iliad* 14.388–391)

#### Line 779

> 779 Hector ubique ferus uiolento pectore saeuit,

Hector comes in, called *Hector*. It is everywhere, marked by *ubique*. He is fierce, described as *ferus*. Something is violent, described as *uiolento*, though what it is is not yet said. What is violent is his heart, named *pectore*. He rages, told with *saeuit*.
