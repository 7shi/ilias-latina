### 788 (*Iliad* 14.476–477)

#### Line 788

> 788 Boeotumque Acamas Promachum, quem sternit atrocis

The Boeotians come in as well, named *Boeotumque*, though who it is is not yet said. As the commentary says, Boeotia is a region of central Greece. Acamas comes in, called *Acamas*. As the commentary says, Acamas is a son of Antenor. The Boeotian one is Promachus, named *Promachum*. Acamas comes back, as *quem*. Someone lays him low, told with *sternit*. Someone is fierce, which closes the line as *atrocis*. Who is fierce, and what lays Acamas low, comes only in the next section.
