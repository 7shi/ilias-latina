### 786 (*Iliad* 14.449–451)

#### Line 786

> 786 Polydamas ualido Prothoenora percutit ictu,

Polydamas comes in, called *Polydamas*. Something is powerful, described as *ualido*, though what it is is not yet said. Prothoenor comes in, named *Prothoenora*. Polydamas strikes him, told with *percutit*. What is powerful is the blow, which closes the line as *ictu*.
