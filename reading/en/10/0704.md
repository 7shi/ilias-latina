### 704–706 (*Iliad* 10.314, 10.318–319)

#### Lines 704–706

> 704 carpebant, uenit ecce Dolon, quem Troia pubes
>
> 705 miserat, ut Danaum sollerti pectore uires
>
> 706 perspiceret sensusque ducum plebisque referret.

They were making that way, told with *carpebant*. Someone comes, told with *uenit*. He is suddenly in sight, marked by *ecce*. The one who comes is Dolon, named *Dolon*. Dolon comes back, as *quem*. Some are Trojan, described as *Troia*, though who they are is not yet said. Those who are Trojan are the warriors, which close the line as *pubes*.

The warriors had sent him, told with *miserat*. A purpose follows, marked by *ut*. The Danaans come back, named *Danaum*. Something is shrewd, described as *sollerti*, though what it is is not yet said. What was shrewd is his mind, named *pectore*. Their strength comes in, which closes the line as *uires*.

He should examine it, told with *perspiceret*. Counsels come in as well, named *sensusque*. The counsels are of the leaders, named *ducum*. The common people are joined as well, named *plebisque*. He should report them back, which closes the line as *referret*.
