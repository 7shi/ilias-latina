### 712–714 (*Iliad* 10.351–359, 10.374–377)

#### Lines 712–714

> 712 Post, ubi transierat fidens animoque manuque,
>
> 713 prosiluere uiri iuuenemque euadere cursu
>
> 714 conantem capiunt ferroque manuque minantur.

It is afterward, marked by *Post*. A time is set, marked by *ubi*. He had passed them, told with *transierat*. He is trusting, described as *fidens*. His courage comes in, named *animoque*. His hand comes in as well, which closes the line as *manuque*.

Someone sprang out, told with *prosiluere*. Those who sprang out are the men, named *uiri*. The young man comes in as well, named *iuuenemque*. Escaping comes next, told with *euadere*. It is at a run, which closes the line as *cursu*.

He is trying, described as *conantem*. They seize him, told with *capiunt*. Steel comes in as well, named *ferroque*. A hand is joined as well, named *manuque*. They threaten him with them, which closes the line as *minantur*.
