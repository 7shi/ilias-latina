### 697–703 (*Iliad* 10.252–253, 10.219–222, 10.242–247, 10.204–211, 10.272–275, 10.297–298)

#### Line 697

> 697 restabatque super tacitae pars tertia noctis,

Something remained as well, told with *restabatque*. It remained over, marked by *super*. Something is silent, described as *tacitae*, though what it is is not yet said. A part comes in, named *pars*. The part is the third, described as *tertia*. What was silent is the night, which closes the line as *noctis*.

#### Lines 698–699

> 698 cum Danaum iussu castris Aetolius heros
>
> 699 egreditur sociumque sibi delegit Vlixem,

A time is set, marked by *cum*. The Danaans come back, named *Danaum*. Their command comes in, named *iussu*. The camp comes in, named *castris*. Someone is Aetolian, described as *Aetolius*, though who it is is not yet said. The Aetolian one is a hero, who closes the line as *heros*. As the commentary says, the Aetolian hero is Diomedes.

He goes out, told with *egreditur*. A companion comes in as well, named *sociumque*. The companion is for himself, as *sibi*. He chooses him, told with *delegit*. The chosen companion is Ulysses, who closes the line as *Vlixem*.

#### Lines 700–702

> 700 qui secum tacitae sublustri noctis in umbra
>
> 701 scrutetur studio quae sit fiducia Troum
>
> 702 quidue agitent quantasue parent in proelia uires.

Ulysses comes back, as *qui*. He is with Diomedes, given as *secum*. Something is silent, described as *tacitae*, though what it is is not yet said. Something is dim, described as *sublustri*, though what it is is not yet said. What was silent is the night, named *noctis*. What was dim is shadow, which closes the line as *in umbra*.

He should search something out, told with *scrutetur*. It is with zeal, named *studio*. What something is comes next, marked by *quae*. It is so, told with *sit*. What it is is confidence, named *fiducia*. The confidence belongs to the Trojans, who close the line as *Troum*.

An alternative question comes, marked by *quidue*. They plan it, told with *agitent*. Another alternative is how great, described as *quantasue*, though what it is is not yet said. They prepare something, told with *parent*. Battle comes in, given as *in proelia*. What was so great is forces, which close the line as *uires*.

#### Line 703

> 703 Dumque iter horrendum loca pernoctata pauentes

A time is set as well, marked by *Dumque*. A way comes in, named *iter*. The way is dreadful, described as *horrendum*. Places come in, named *loca*. The places are passed through the night, described as *pernoctata*. They fear them, which closes the line as *pauentes*. What happens while they do so comes only in the next section.
