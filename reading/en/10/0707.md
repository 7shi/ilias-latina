### 707–711 (*Iliad* 10.339–341, 10.349–350, 10.314, 10.351, 10.346–348)

#### Lines 707–711

> 707 Quem procul ut uidit socius Diomedis Vlixes,
>
> 708 abdiderant occultantes sua corpora furtim
>
> 709 post densos frutices, dum spe percussus inani
>
> 710 Tros Eumediades cursu praecederet illos,
>
> 711 ne facile oppressus gressum in sua castra referret.

Dolon comes back, as *Quem*. He is from afar, marked by *procul*. A time is set, marked by *ut*. Someone saw him, told with *uidit*. The companion comes in, named *socius*. He is the companion of Diomedes, named *Diomedis*. The companion is Ulysses, who closes the line as *Vlixes*.

They had concealed something, told with *abdiderant*. They were hiding, described as *occultantes*. Something is their own, described as *sua*. What was their own is bodies, named *corpora*. It was in stealth, which closes the line as *furtim*.

Something is behind what is thick, given as *post densos*, though what it is is not yet said. What was thick is bushes, named *frutices*. A time is set, marked by *dum*. Hope comes in, named *spe*. Someone is struck by it, described as *percussus*, though who it is is not yet said. The hope is vain, which closes the line as *inani*.

The struck one is Trojan, described as *Tros*. He is the son of Eumedes, named *Eumediades*. As the commentary says, the son of Eumedes is Dolon. It is at a run, named *cursu*. He runs past, told with *praecederet*. Those he passes are the two Greeks, who close the line as *illos*.

A negative purpose is set, marked by *ne*. It would not be easily, marked by *facile*. Dolon would be overpowered, described as *oppressus*. Steps come in, named *gressum*. They would lead to his own, given as *in sua*, though where is not yet said. What was his own is camp, named *castra*. He could carry them back, which closes the line as *referret*.
