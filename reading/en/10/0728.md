### 728–729 (*Iliad* 10.454–456, 10.469–470)

#### Lines 728–729

> 728 cognouere uiri, fauces mucrone recluso
>
> 729 detrudunt iuuenis. Post haec tentoria Rhesi

They had learned it, told with *cognouere*. The men come back, named *uiri*. A throat comes in, named *fauces*. A blade comes in, named *mucrone*. The blade is drawn, which closes the line as *recluso*.

They sever it, told with *detrudunt*. The throat belongs to the young man, named *iuuenis*. A time is set, given as *Post haec*. Tents come in, named *tentoria*. The tents are of Rhesus, who closes the line as *Rhesi*. What happens at the tents comes only in the next section.
