### 696 (*Iliad* 10.251–252)

#### Line 696

> 696 Alterius tenebrae tarde labentibus astris

Something is of the second part, described as *Alterius*, though what it is is not yet said. What was of the second part is darkness, named *tenebrae*. It is slowly, marked by *tarde*. Some are gliding on, described as *labentibus*, though what they are is not yet said. Those gliding on are the stars, which close the line as *astris*. What happens comes only in the next section.
