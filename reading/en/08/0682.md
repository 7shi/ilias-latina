### 682 (*Iliad* 8.509–511)

#### Line 682

> 682 At Phryges obsidunt inclusos aggere Graios

Something is set against this, marked by *At*. The Phrygians come in, named *Phryges*. They besiege someone, told with *obsidunt*. Some are shut in, described as *inclusos*. A rampart comes in, named *aggere*. Those shut in are the Greeks, who close the line as *Graios*. What more the Phrygians do comes only in the next section.
