### 679–681 (*Iliad* 8.343–345)

#### Lines 679–681

> 679 Se rursus Danai turbati caede suorum
>
> 680 conuertunt iterumque leues in castra cateruae
>
> 681 confugiunt portasque obiecto robore firmant.

They themselves come in, as *Se*. It is back again, marked by *rursus*. The Danaans come in, named *Danai*. They are shaken, described as *turbati*. Slaughter comes in, named *caede*. The slaughter is of their own, which closes the line as *suorum*.

They turn, told with *conuertunt*. It is again as well, marked by *iterumque*. Some are swift, described as *leues*, though who they are is not yet said. The camp comes in, given as *in castra*. The swift ones are bands, which close the line as *cateruae*.

They flee, told with *confugiunt*. Gates come in as well, named *portasque*. Something is thrown across, described as *obiecto*. What is thrown across is oak, named *robore*. They secure the gates, which closes the line as *firmant*.
