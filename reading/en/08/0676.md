### 676 (*Iliad* 8.330–334)

#### Line 676

> 676 Ast illum fidi rapiunt de caede sodales

Something is set against this, marked by *Ast*. Teucer comes back, as *illum*. Some are faithful, described as *fidi*, though who they are is not yet said. They snatch him, told with *rapiunt*. Slaughter comes in, given as *de caede*. The faithful ones are comrades, which close the line as *sodales*.
