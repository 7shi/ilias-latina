### 655 (*Iliad* 8.52)

#### Line 655

> 655 Inde acies uidet Iliacas dextraque potenti

A place is set, marked by *Inde*. Battle lines come in, named *acies*. He sees them, told with *uidet*. The battle lines are Ilian, described as *Iliacas*. As the commentary says, Ilian is from Ilion, another name of Troy. A right hand comes in as well, named *dextraque*. It is mighty, which closes the line as *potenti*. What he does with it comes only in the next section.
