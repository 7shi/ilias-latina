### 675 (*Iliad* 8.327–329)

#### Line 675

> 675 occupat excussoque incautum proterit arcu.

Hector strikes first, told with *occupat*. Something is struck away as well, described as *excussoque*, though what it is is not yet said. Teucer is off guard, described as *incautum*. Hector lays him low, told with *proterit*. What was struck away is his bow, which closes the line as *arcu*.
