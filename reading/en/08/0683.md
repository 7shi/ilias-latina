### 683–685 (*Iliad* 8.554–563, 8.543–547)

#### Line 683

> 683 excubituque premunt muros flammisque coronant.

Sentries come in as well, named *excubituque*. They beset them, told with *premunt*. What they beset is the walls, named *muros*. Fires come in as well, named *flammisque*. They encircle the walls with them, which closes the line as *coronant*.

#### Lines 684–685

> 684 Cetera per campos sternunt sua corpora pubes
>
> 685 indulgentque mero curas\<que> animosque resoluunt.

Some are the rest, described as *Cetera*, though who they are is not yet said. The fields come in, given as *per campos*. They stretch out, told with *sternunt*. What they stretch out is their own, described as *sua*. What is their own is bodies, named *corpora*. Those who are the rest are the warriors, who close the line as *pubes*.

They indulge as well, told with *indulgentque*. Wine comes in, named *mero*. Cares come in as well, named *curas\<que>*. Spirits come in as well, named *animosque*. They relax them, which ends the eighth book as *resoluunt*.
