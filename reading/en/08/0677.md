### 677–678 (*Iliad* 8.335–342)

#### Lines 677–678

> 677 prostratumque leuant. Ruit undique turbidus Hector
>
> 678 aduersasque acies infesta cuspide terret.

Teucer is laid low as well, described as *prostratumque*. The comrades lift him, told with *leuant*. Someone rushes, told with *Ruit*. It is on every side, marked by *undique*. He is wild, described as *turbidus*. The wild one is Hector, who closes the line as *Hector*.

Something is opposing as well, described as *aduersasque*. What is opposing is the battle lines, named *acies*. Something is threatening, described as *infesta*. What is threatening is a spear-point, named *cuspide*. Hector terrifies them, which closes the line as *terret*.
