### 673–674 (*Iliad* 8.312–315, 8.320–322)

#### Lines 673–674

> 673 mox alias acies petit aurigamque superbi
>
> 674 Hectoris obtruncat, quem saxo Troius heros

It is soon, marked by *mox*. Something is other, described as *alias*. What is other is battle lines, named *acies*. He attacks them, told with *petit*. A charioteer comes in as well, named *aurigamque*. Someone is proud, described as *superbi*, though who it is is not yet said.

The proud one is Hector, named *Hectoris*. Teucer cuts the charioteer down, told with *obtruncat*. Teucer comes back, as *quem*. A stone comes in, given as *saxo*. Someone is Trojan, described as *Troius*. The Trojan one is a hero, who closes the line as *heros*. As the commentary says, the Trojan hero is Hector. What he does with the stone comes only in the next section.
