### 663–664 (*Iliad* 8.218–229)

#### Lines 663–664

> 663 Hortatur socios muris inclusus Atrides
>
> 664 languentesque animos iuuenum in certamina firmat.

Someone urges others on, told with *Hortatur*. Those urged on are his comrades, named *socios*. Walls come in, named *muris*. He is shut in, described as *inclusus*. The one who urges them on is the son of Atreus, who closes the line as *Atrides*. As the commentary says, Atrides is Agamemnon.

Something is flagging as well, described as *languentesque*. What is flagging is spirits, named *animos*. They are the spirits of the young men, named *iuuenum*. Battle comes in, given as *in certamina*. He strengthens them, which closes the line as *firmat*.
