### 672 (*Iliad* 8.302–308)

#### Line 672

> 672 Gorgythiona ferum letali uulnere fundit;

Gorgythion comes in, named *Gorgythiona*. As the commentary says, Gorgythion is one of the sons of Priam. He is fierce, described as *ferum*. Something is fatal, described as *letali*. What is fatal is a wound, named *uulnere*. Teucer lays him low, which closes the line as *fundit*.
