### 650 (*Iliad* 8.1)

#### Line 650

> 650 Vt nitidum Titan radiis patefecerat orbem,

A time is set, marked by *Vt*. Something is bright, described as *nitidum*, though what it is is not yet said. Titan comes in, named *Titan*. As the commentary says, Titan is the sun god. His rays come in, given as *radiis*. He had revealed something, told with *patefecerat*. What is bright is the world, which closes the line as *orbem*. What happens then comes only in the next section.
