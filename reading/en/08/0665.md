### 665–670 (*Iliad* 8.253–260, 8.267–268)

#### Lines 665–666

> 665 Princeps Tydides fulgens ardentibus armis
>
> 666 per medios hostes immani pondere fertur.

Someone is foremost, described as *Princeps*. The son of Tydeus comes in, named *Tydides*. As the commentary says, Tydides is Diomedes. He is gleaming, described as *fulgens*. Something is blazing, described as *ardentibus*. What is blazing is his armor, which closes the line as *armis*.

The midst comes in, given as *per medios*. The midst is of the enemy, named *hostes*. Something is tremendous, described as *immani*. What is tremendous is force, named *pondere*. He charges, which closes the line as *fertur*.

#### Lines 667–669

> 667 Hic illi occurrit fatis Agelaus iniquis,
>
> 668 telum immane manu quatiens, quem maximus heros
>
> 669 occupat et duro medium transuerberat ense.

A place is set, marked by *Hic*. Diomedes comes back, as *illi*. Someone meets him, told with *occurrit*. Fate comes in, named *fatis*. The one who meets him is Agelaus, named *Agelaus*. His fate is against him, which closes the line as *iniquis*.

A weapon comes in, named *telum*. The weapon is huge, described as *immane*. A hand comes in, given as *manu*. He is brandishing it, described as *quatiens*. Agelaus comes back, as *quem*. Someone is mighty, described as *maximus*. The mighty one is a hero, who closes the line as *heros*.

He strikes first, told with *occupat*. More is added, marked by *et*. Something is hard, described as *duro*, though what it is is not yet said. Agelaus is taken at the middle, described as *medium*. The hero pierces him, told with *transuerberat*. What is hard is his sword, which closes the line as *ense*.

#### Line 670

> 670 Hinc Phrygas Aiacis uastis protectus in armis

A place is set, marked by *Hinc*. The Phrygians come in, named *Phrygas*. Ajax comes in, named *Aiacis*. Something is vast, described as *uastis*, though what it is is not yet said. Someone is sheltered, described as *protectus*. What is vast is armor, which closes the line as *in armis*. Who is sheltered and what he does comes only in the next section.
