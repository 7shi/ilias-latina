### 671 (*Iliad* 8.266, 8.269–270)

#### Line 671

> 671 Teucer agit spargitque leues in terga sagittas.

The one sheltered is Teucer, named *Teucer*. He drives the Phrygians, as *agit*. He scatters them as well, told with *spargitque*. Something is light, described as *leues*, though what it is is not yet said. Their backs come in, given as *in terga*. What was light is arrows, which close the line as *sagittas*.
