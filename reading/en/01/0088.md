### 88–92 (*Iliad* 1.502–505, 1.508, 1.506–507)

#### Lines 88–92

> 88 "Pro nato ueni genetrix en ad tua supplex
>
> 89 numina, summe parens; ulciscere meque meumque
>
> 90 corpus ab Atrida, quodsi permittitur illi
>
> 91 ut flammas impune mei uiolarit Achillis,
>
> 92 turpiter occiderit superata libidine uirtus."

She opens her plea on behalf of her son, spoken of as *Pro nato*. She tells the god that she has arrived, given as *ueni*. She speaks as his mother, appearing as *genetrix*. She presents herself before him, marked by *en*. She comes before what belongs to the god, designated as *ad tua*, though what she approaches is not yet said. She offers her prayer as a suppliant, closing the line as *supplex*.

What was designated as *ad tua* is revealed as his divine power, returning as *numina*. She addresses him as the highest ruler, named *summe parens*. She demands that he take vengeance, commanded with *ulciscere*. She bids him avenge her own person, told as *meque*. She asks vengeance also for what is hers, described as *meumque*, though what belongs to her is not yet said.

What was described as *meumque* is revealed as her own flesh, appearing as *corpus*. The vengeance is to be taken upon the king, spoken of as *ab Atrida*. She sets out the consequence if this is refused, marked by *quodsi*. She supposes that the wrong is allowed, told with *permittitur*. That license would be granted to him, closing the line as *illi*.

What would be permitted follows with *ut*. The king would strike at her son's love, spoken of as *flammas*. He would do so without punishment, told with *impune*. That love belongs to her own warrior, claimed as *mei*. The king would outrage it, expressed by *uiolarit*. The son she defends stands at the end of the line as *Achillis*.

The outcome would be disgraceful, marked by *turpiter*. True worth would fall, told with *occiderit*. It would be crushed, described as *superata*. The force that conquers it is lust, named *libidine*. What is brought down is valor, which closes her speech as *uirtus*.
