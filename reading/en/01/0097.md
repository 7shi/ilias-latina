### 97 (*Iliad* 1.532)

#### Line 97

> 97 litus adit patrium gratasque sororibus undas.

She reaches a shore, named *litus*. She approaches it, told with *adit*. That shore belongs to her native realm, described as *patrium*. What she approaches next is also dear, described as *gratasque*, though what is welcome is not yet said. It is beloved by her sisters, spoken of as *sororibus*. As the commentary says, these sisters are the Nereids. What was described as *gratasque* is revealed as the waters, which close the line as *undas*.
