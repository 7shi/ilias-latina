### 44 (*Iliad* 1.43)

#### Line 44

> 44 Dixerat. Ille sui uatis prece motus acerbis

Chryses has finished speaking, marked by *Dixerat*. The god who hears him is taken up as *Ille*. The one who prayed is his own priest, spoken of as *sui uatis*. What reaches Apollo is the priest's prayer, named *prece*. The god is stirred by it, told with *motus*. Something bitter comes at the end of the line, described as *acerbis*, though what is bitter is not yet said.
