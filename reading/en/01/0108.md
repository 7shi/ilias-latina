### 108 (*Iliad* 1.605)

#### Line 108

> 108 Interea sol emenso decedit Olympo

Meanwhile time passes, marked by *Interea*. The sun comes in, appearing as *sol*. Something has been crossed, described as *emenso*, though what it is is not yet said. The sun sets, told with *decedit*. What was crossed is the sky, here named *Olympo*, which closes the line.
