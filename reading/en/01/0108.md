### 108 (*Iliad* 1.605)

#### Line 108

> 108 Interea sol emenso decedit Olympo

The poem marks the passing of time, beginning with *Interea*. The light of the day comes from the sun, appearing as *sol*. Its journey across the heavens is completed, described as *emenso*, though what it has traversed is not yet said. The sun begins to set, told with *decedit*. What was traversed is revealed as the sky of heaven, closing the line as *Olympo*.
