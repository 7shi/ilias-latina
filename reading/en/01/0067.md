### 67 (*Iliad* 1.478–487)

#### Line 67

> 67 atque iterum ad classes Danaum sua uela retorsit.

More follows, joined by *atque*. Something happens again, marked by *iterum*. Where it goes is to the fleets, told as *ad classes*. The fleets are those of the Danaans, appearing as *Danaum*. What goes there are his own sails, spoken of as *sua uela*. He turns them back, told with *retorsit*.
