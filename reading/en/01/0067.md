### 67 (*Iliad* 1.478–487)

#### Line 67

> 67 atque iterum ad classes Danaum sua uela retorsit.

The journey does not end there, continuing with *atque*. Ulysses makes his way back once more, marked by *iterum*. He directs his ship toward the fleet, given as *ad classes*. Those ships belong to the Greeks, appearing as *Danaum*. He sets his own sails to the wind, spoken of as *sua uela*. He turns them around toward the camp, told with *retorsit*.
