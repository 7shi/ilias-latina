### 65–66 (*Iliad* 1.309–312, 1.430–439)

#### Lines 65–66

> 65 multaque dona super. Quam cunctis notus Vlixes
>
> 66 impositam puppi patrias deuexit ad arces

Agamemnon adds gifts of his own, appearing as *multaque dona*. These are sent over and above the restored daughter, told with *super*. Chryseis is referred to again as *Quam*. The one who leads the journey is renowned among all the Greeks, described as *cunctis notus*. The hero who conducts her is named *Vlixes*.

She is placed on board, told with *impositam*. The vessel that carries her is a ship, named *puppi*. The haven she reaches belongs to her homeland, described as *patrias*, though where she lands is not yet said. Ulysses brings her there by sea, given as *deuexit*. What was described as *patrias* is her native stronghold, closing the line as *ad arces*.
