### 65–66 (*Iliad* 1.309–312, 1.430–439)

#### Lines 65–66

> 65 multaque dona super. Quam cunctis notus Vlixes
>
> 66 impositam puppi patrias deuexit ad arces

With her go many gifts, appearing as *multaque dona*. They come besides, told with *super*. Chryseis is referred to again as *Quam*. Someone known to all comes next, described as *cunctis notus*. He is named *Vlixes*.

She is put aboard, told with *impositam*. What she is put aboard is a ship, named *puppi*. Something belongs to her homeland, described as *patrias*, though what it is is not yet said. Ulysses carries her there, given as *deuexit*. What was described as *patrias* is the citadel of her home, closing the line as *ad arces*.
