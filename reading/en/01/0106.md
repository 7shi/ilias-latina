### 106–107 (*Iliad* 1.571–600, 1.606)

#### Lines 106–107

> 106 Tandem interposito lis Ignipotente resedit
>
> 107 conciliumque simul genitor dimittit Olympi.

At last something changes, marked by *Tandem*. Someone has stepped in, told with *interposito*, though who it is is not yet said. What is at stake is a quarrel, appearing as *lis*. The one who stepped in is the god who rules fire, named *Ignipotente*. As the commentary says, Ignipotens is Vulcan. The quarrel settles, closing the line as *resedit*.

An assembly comes next, named *conciliumque*. It happens at the same time, marked by *simul*. The one who acts is a father, spoken of as *genitor*. He sends the assembly away, told with *dimittit*. He is the father of Olympus, which closes the line as *Olympi*.
