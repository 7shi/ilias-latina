### 106–107 (*Iliad* 1.571–600, 1.606)

#### Lines 106–107

> 106 Tandem interposito lis Ignipotente resedit
>
> 107 conciliumque simul genitor dimittit Olympi.

Peace returns at last, marked by *Tandem*. An intervention takes place, told with *interposito*, though who intervenes is not yet said. What is quieted is the quarrel, appearing as *lis*. The one who intervened is the god of fire, named *Ignipotente*. As the commentary says, Ignipotens is Vulcan. The strife settles down, closing the line as *resedit*.

The gathering of the gods is also brought to a close, described as *conciliumque*. The action follows at the same moment, indicated by *simul*. The ruler who acts is the father of the gods, spoken of as *genitor*. He sends the council away, told with *dimittit*. The realm he governs is Olympus, which closes the line as *Olympi*.
