### 85 (*Iliad* 1.422, 1.496–497)

#### Line 85

> 85 abstineat dextram ac congressibus; inde per auras

Her counsel bids him hold back, told with *abstineat*. He is to stay his right hand, appearing as *dextram*. He must also keep away from combat, joined by *ac* and named *congressibus*. Her departure follows next, marked by *inde*. She makes her way through the air, closing the line as *per auras*.
