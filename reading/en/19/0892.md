### 892–893 (*Iliad* 19.364–369)

#### Lines 892–893

> 892 Talibus ornatus donis Thetideius heros
>
> 893 in medias acies immani turbine fertur,

Something is such, described as *Talibus*, though what it is is not yet said. Someone is equipped, described as *ornatus*. What is such is gifts, named *donis*. The son of Thetis comes in, called *Thetideius*. As the commentary says, Thetideius is Achilles, son of the sea-goddess Thetis. The hero comes in, who closes the line as *heros*.

The midst comes in, given as *in medias*. Battle lines come in, named *acies*. A huge whirlwind comes in, given as *immani turbine*. He is borne along, which closes the line as *fertur*. What follows comes only in the next section.
