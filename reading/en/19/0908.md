### 908–910 (*Iliad* 21.15–21)

#### Line 908

> 908 instat et in mediis pugnatur gurgitis undis.

He presses on, told with *instat*. As the commentary says, the pursuer is Achilles. More is added, marked by *et*. Something is in the midst, given as *in mediis*, though what it is is not yet said. Battle is joined, told with *pugnatur*. An eddy comes in, named *gurgitis*. What is in the midst is waters, which close the line as *undis*.

#### Line 909

> 909 Ira dabat uires; stringuntur sanguine ripae

Anger comes in, named *Ira*. It gave something, told with *dabat*. Strength comes in, named *uires*. Some are streaked, told with *stringuntur*. Blood comes in, named *sanguine*. Those that are streaked are banks, which close the line as *ripae*.

#### Line 910

> 910 sparsaque per totos uoluuntur corpora fluctus.

Some are scattered as well, described as *sparsaque*, though what they are is not yet said. Something is whole, given as *per totos*, though what it is is not yet said. They tumble, told with *uoluuntur*. What is scattered is bodies, named *corpora*. What is whole is waves, which ends the nineteenth book as *fluctus*.
