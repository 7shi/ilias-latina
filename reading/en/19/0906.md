### 906–907 (*Iliad* 21.1–8)

#### Lines 906–907

> 906 confugit ad Xanthi rapidos perterrita fluctus
>
> 907 auxiliumque petit diuini fluminis; ille

They flee for refuge, told with *confugit*. Xanthus comes in, given as *ad Xanthi*. As the commentary says, Xanthus is a river near Troy. Something is rushing, described as *rapidos*, though what it is is not yet said. They are in terror, described as *perterrita*. What is rushing is waters, which close the line as *fluctus*.

Aid comes in as well, named *auxiliumque*. They seek it, told with *petit*. The divine river comes in, given as *diuini fluminis*. Someone comes in, as *ille*. What he does comes only in the next section.
