### 903–905 (*Iliad* 20.381–383, 20.490–499)

#### Lines 903–905

> 903 Inde agit Aeacides infesta cuspide Teucros
>
> 904 ingentemque modum prosternit caede uirorum,
>
> 905 sanguinis Hectorei sitiens. At Dardana pubes

A time is set, marked by *Inde*. He drives someone, told with *agit*. Someone is called *Aeacides*. Something is threatening, described as *infesta*, though what it is is not yet said. What is threatening is a spear, given as *cuspide*. Those he drives are Teucrians, who close the line as *Teucros*. As the commentary says, the Teucrians are the Trojans.

A vast number comes in as well, given as *ingentemque modum*. He lays them low, told with *prosternit*. Slaughter comes in, named *caede*. Men come in, who close the line as *uirorum*.

Hector's blood comes in, given as *sanguinis Hectorei*. He is thirsting for it, described as *sitiens*. But something turns, marked by *At*. Some are Dardanian, described as *Dardana*, though who they are is not yet said. Who are Dardanian is the youth, which closes the line as *pubes*. As the commentary says, the Dardanian youth are the Trojans. What they do comes only in the next section.
