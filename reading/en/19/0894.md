### 894 (*Iliad* 19.349–354)

#### Line 894

> 894 cui uires praebet casta cum Pallade Iuno

Achilles comes back, as *cui*. Strength comes in, named *uires*. Someone gives it, told with *praebet*. Someone is chaste, described as *casta*. Who is chaste is Pallas, given as *cum Pallade*. As the commentary says, Pallas is Minerva, goddess of wisdom and warfare. Juno comes in, who closes the line as *Iuno*. As the commentary says, Juno is the Roman counterpart of Hera, wife of Zeus.
