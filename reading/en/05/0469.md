### 469–470 (*Iliad* 5.336–339, 5.343, 5.352–367)

#### Lines 469–470

> 469 caelestemque manum mortali uulnerat hasta.
>
> 470 Icta petit caelum terris Cytherea relictis

Something is heavenly as well, given as *caelestemque*. It is her hand, named *manum*. Something is mortal, described as *mortali*, though what it is is not yet said. Diomedes wounds her, told with *uulnerat*. What was mortal is his spear, which closes the line as *hasta*.

She has been struck, described as *Icta*. She makes her way, told with *petit*. What she seeks is heaven, named *caelum*. The earth comes in, as *terris*. She is named from Cythera, called *Cytherea*. As the commentary says, Cytherea is Venus. That earth is left behind, which closes the line as *relictis*.
