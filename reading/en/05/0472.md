### 472 (*Iliad* 5.344–345)

#### Line 472

> 472 Dardanium Aenean seruat Troianus Apollo

Someone is Dardanian, described as *Dardanium*. He is Aeneas, named *Aenean*. Someone saves him, told with *seruat*. He is Trojan, described as *Troianus*. The one who saves him is Apollo, who closes the line as *Apollo*. What Apollo does next comes only in the next section.
