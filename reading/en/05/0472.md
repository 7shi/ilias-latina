### 472 (*Iliad* 5.344–345)

#### Line 472

> 472 Dardanium Aenean seruat Troianus Apollo

Someone is Dardanian, described as *Dardanium*. He is Aeneas, named *Aenean*. He is protected, told with *seruat*. The protector stands with Troy, called *Troianus*. The god is Apollo, who closes the line as *Apollo*.
