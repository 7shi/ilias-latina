### 431 (*Iliad* 5.49)

#### Line 431

> 431 et Strophio genitum Stygias demittit ad umbras.

In his joy he goes on, joined by *et*. Strophius comes in, named *Strophio*. The one born of him comes in, described as *genitum*. As the commentary says, the son of Strophius is Scamandrius. Something belongs to the Styx, called *Stygias*, though what it is is not yet said. Idomeneus sends him down, told with *demittit*. What belonged to the Styx is the shades, which close the line as *ad umbras*.
