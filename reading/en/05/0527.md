### 527 (*Iliad* 5.669–670)

#### Line 527

> 527 Sarpedon fraudisque subit commentor Vlixes

The warrior who withdrew is named *Sarpedon*. Deceit comes in as well, called *fraudisque*, though whose it is is not yet said. Someone comes forward, told with *subit*. He is a contriver of deceit, named *commentor*. The contriver is Ulysses, who closes the line as *Vlixes*.
