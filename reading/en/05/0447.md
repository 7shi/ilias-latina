### 447–448 (*Iliad* 5.159–160, 5.163–164)

#### Lines 447–448

> 447 Post hos infestus Chromiumque et Echemmona telo
>
> 448 proturbat celeri pariterque ad Tartara mittit.

It happens after these warriors, given as *Post hos*. Diomedes is hostile, described as *infestus*. Chromius comes in as well, named *Chromiumque*. Another is joined to him by *et*. He is Echemmon, called *Echemmona*. A weapon comes in, which closes the line as *telo*, though what kind it is is not yet said.

Diomedes drives them back, told with *proturbat*. What was brought against them is swift, described as *celeri*. It is done alike as well, marked by *pariterque*. The place is Tartarus, the realm of the dead, given as *ad Tartara*. Diomedes sends them there, which closes the line as *mittit*.
