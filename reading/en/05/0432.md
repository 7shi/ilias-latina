### 432 (*Iliad* 5.59, 5.65–66)

#### Line 432

> 432 Meriones Phereclum librata percutit hasta

Meriones comes in, named *Meriones*. Phereclus comes in, called *Phereclum*. Something is poised, described as *librata*, though what it is is not yet said. Meriones strikes him, told with *percutit*. What was poised is a spear, which closes the line as *hasta*.
