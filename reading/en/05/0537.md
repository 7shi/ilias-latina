### 537 (*Iliad* 5.888–898)

#### Line 537

> 537 saucius et magni genitoris iurgia suffert.

He is wounded, described as *saucius*. More follows, joined by *et*. Someone is mighty, described as *magni*. The mighty one is his father, named *genitoris*. Rebukes come in, named *iurgia*. He endures them, which ends the fifth book as *suffert*.
