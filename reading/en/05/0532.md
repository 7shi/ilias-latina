### 532–534 (*Iliad* 5.846, 5.850–858)

#### Line 532

> 532 Pugnat bellipotens casta cum Pallade Mauors

Someone fights, told with *Pugnat*. He is mighty in war, described as *bellipotens*, though who it is is not yet said. Someone is chaste, described as *casta*. It is with Pallas, given as *cum Pallade*. The one mighty in war is Mavors, who closes the line as *Mauors*. As the commentary says, Mavors is Mars, who is supporting the Trojans.

#### Lines 533–534

> 533 ingentemque mouet clipeum, quem sancta uirago
>
> 534 egit et extrema percussum cuspide caedit

Something is huge as well, described as *ingentemque*. He wields it, told with *mouet*. What is huge is his shield, named *clipeum*. Mavors comes back, as *quem*. Someone is holy, described as *sancta*. She is a warrior-maiden, which closes the line as *uirago*. As the commentary says, the holy warrior-maiden is Pallas.

She drove him, told with *egit*. More follows, joined by *et*. Something is at the tip, described as *extrema*, though what it is is not yet said. He has been struck, described as *percussum*. What was at the tip is her spear-point, named *cuspide*. She wounds him, which closes the line as *caedit*.
