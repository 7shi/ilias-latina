### 532–534 (*Iliad* 5.846, 5.850–858)

#### Line 532

> 532 Pugnat bellipotens casta cum Pallade Mauors

Fighting breaks out, told with *Pugnat*. The fighter is mighty in war, described as *bellipotens*, though who it is is not yet said. The one he faces is chaste, described as *casta*. The fight is with Pallas, given as *cum Pallade*. The one mighty in war is Mavors, who closes the line as *Mauors*.

#### Lines 533–534

> 533 ingentemque mouet clipeum, quem sancta uirago
>
> 534 egit et extrema percussum cuspide caedit

Something also is huge, described as *ingentemque*. He wields it, told with *mouet*. What is huge is his shield, called *clipeum*. Mavors comes back, as *quem*. The one who faces him is holy, described as *sancta*. She is a warrior-maiden, who closes the line as *uirago*.

Driving him is told with *egit*. More follows, marked by *et*. It was with the tip, described as *extrema*. He was struck by it, described as *percussum*. The tip was of a spear, named *cuspide*. She wounds him, told with *caedit*.
