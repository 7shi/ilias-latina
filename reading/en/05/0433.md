### 433 (*Iliad* 5.69)

#### Line 433

> 433 Pedaeumque Meges. Tum uastis horridus armis

Pedaeus comes in as well, named *Pedaeumque*. Meges comes against him, called *Meges*. Then the scene turns, marked by *Tum*. Something is massive, described as *uastis*, though what it is is not yet said. He is fearsome, given as *horridus*. What was massive is armor, which closes the line as *armis*, though who wears it comes only in the next section.
