### 530–531 (*Iliad* 5.793)

#### Lines 530–531

> 530 illinc Tydides: sternuntur utrimque uirorum
>
> 531 corpora per campos et sanguine prata rigantur.

The other side comes in, marked by *illinc*. The champion there is called *Tydides*. Being laid low is told with *sternuntur*. It happens on both sides, marked by *utrimque*. Those who fall are men, who close the line as *uirorum*, though what of theirs is laid low is not yet said.

What is laid low is their bodies, named *corpora*. They lie across the fields, given as *per campos*. More follows, marked by *et*. Blood comes in, called *sanguine*. The land there is meadows, named *prata*. They are watered with it, told with *rigantur*.
