### 530–531 (*Iliad* 5.793)

#### Lines 530–531

> 530 illinc Tydides: sternuntur utrimque uirorum
>
> 531 corpora per campos et sanguine prata rigantur.

It is on that side, marked by *illinc*. Tydides comes in, named *Tydides*. Someone is laid low, told with *sternuntur*. It is on both sides, marked by *utrimque*. Men come in, named *uirorum*, though what of theirs is meant is not yet said.

What was theirs is their bodies, named *corpora*. It is across the fields, given as *per campos*. More follows, joined by *et*. Blood comes in, named *sanguine*. Meadows come in, named *prata*. They are watered with it, which closes the line as *rigantur*.
