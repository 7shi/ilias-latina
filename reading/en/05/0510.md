### 510–512 (*Iliad* 5.561–563)

#### Lines 510–512

> 510 conspicit Atrides: stricto concurrere ferro
>
> 511 comparat et iaculum, quantum furor ipse mouebat,
>
> 512 uiribus intorquet, quod detulit error ab illo

Someone catches sight of him, told with *conspicit*. The one who sees him is the son of Atreus, named *Atrides*. Something has been drawn, described as *stricto*, though what it is is not yet said. He seeks to clash, given as *concurrere*. What was drawn is iron, which closes the line as *ferro*, though what he prepares comes only in the next line.

He prepares for it, told with *comparat*. More follows, joined by *et*. A javelin comes in, called *iaculum*. It is as far as something can go, marked by *quantum*. Fury comes in, named *furor*. It is his fury itself, given as *ipse*. His fury moved him, which closes the line as *mouebat*, though what he does with the weapon comes only in the next line.

Strength is behind it, given as *uiribus*. He hurls the weapon, told with *intorquet*. The javelin comes back, as *quod*. Something carried it away, described as *detulit*. The cause was a miss, named *error*. It flew wide of him, which closes the line as *ab illo*.
