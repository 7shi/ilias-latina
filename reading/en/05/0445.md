### 445 (*Iliad* 5.148)

#### Line 445

> 445 inde premit Polyidon Abantaque cuspide forti

Then the assault moves on, marked by *inde*. Diomedes presses hard, told with *premit*. Polyidus comes in, named *Polyidon*. Abas comes in as well, called *Abantaque*. A spearpoint comes in, given as *cuspide*. That spearpoint is strong, which closes the line as *forti*.
