### 529 (*Iliad* 5.680)

#### Line 529

> 529 Hinc pugnat patriae columen Mauortius Hector,

It is on this side, marked by *Hinc*. Someone fights, told with *pugnat*. His homeland comes in, named *patriae*. He is its pillar, named *columen*. He is of Mars, described as *Mauortius*. As the commentary says, Mavortian, from Mavors, a name of Mars, marks him as warlike. The fighter is Hector, who closes the line as *Hector*.
