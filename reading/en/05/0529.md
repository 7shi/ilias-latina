### 529 (*Iliad* 5.680)

#### Line 529

> 529 Hinc pugnat patriae columen Mauortius Hector,

This side comes in, marked by *Hinc*. Someone fights, told with *pugnat*. It is for his homeland, named *patriae*. The fighter is its pillar, called *columen*. He is warlike, described as *Mauortius*. The warrior is Hector, who closes the line as *Hector*.
