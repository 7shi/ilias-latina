### 519 (*Iliad* 5.576–579)

#### Line 519

> 519 Paphlagonum ductor Menelai concidit armis,

The one overcome belongs to the Paphlagonians, named *Paphlagonum*. He is their leader, named *ductor*. As the commentary says, their commander is Pylaemenes. Menelaus comes in, named *Menelai*, though what of his is meant is not yet said. The leader falls, told with *concidit*. What was Menelaus' is his weapons, which close the line as *armis*.
