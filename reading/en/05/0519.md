### 519 (*Iliad* 5.576–579)

#### Line 519

> 519 Paphlagonum ductor Menelai concidit armis,

The one overcome belongs to the Paphlagonians, named *Paphlagonum*. He is their leader, called *ductor*. As the commentary says, the leader of the Paphlagonians is Pylaemenes. Menelaus comes in, given as *Menelai*. The leader falls, told with *concidit*. He fell beneath his weapons, which closes the line as *armis*.
