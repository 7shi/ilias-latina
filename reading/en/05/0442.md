### 442 (*Iliad* 5.136)

#### Line 442

> 442 in mediasque acies animosi more leonis

The midst comes in as well, given as *in mediasque*. It is the midst of the battle lines, named *acies*. Someone is spirited, described as *animosi*, though who it is is not yet said. It is in the manner of something, named *more*. The spirited creature is a lion, which closes the line as *leonis*, though what Diomedes does among the ranks comes only in the next section.
