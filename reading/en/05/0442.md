### 442 (*Iliad* 5.136)

#### Line 442

> 442 in mediasque acies animosi more leonis

His anger carries him into the midst as well, given as *in mediasque*. Those ranks are battle lines, named *acies*. Someone is spirited, described as *animosi*, though who it is is not yet said. It is in someone's manner, called *more*. The spirited creature is a lion, which closes the line as *leonis*, though what Diomedes does among the ranks comes only in the next section.
