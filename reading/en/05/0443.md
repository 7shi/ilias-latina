### 443–444 (*Iliad* 5.144–147)

#### Lines 443–444

> 443 fertur et Astynoum, magnum quoque Hypirona fundit,
>
> 444 comminus hunc gladio, iaculo ferit eminus illum;

Diomedes is borne on, told with *fertur*. More follows, joined by *et*. Astynous comes in, named *Astynoum*. Someone is mighty, described as *magnum*. It is so as well, marked by *quoque*. The mighty one is Hypiron, called *Hypirona*. Diomedes lays them low, which closes the line as *fundit*.

It is at close quarters, marked by *comminus*. Astynous comes back, as *hunc*. Diomedes uses a sword, called *gladio*. A javelin comes in, named *iaculo*. Diomedes strikes, told with *ferit*. It is from afar, given as *eminus*. Hypiron comes back, who closes the line as *illum*.
