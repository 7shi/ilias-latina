### 446 (*Iliad* 5.152)

#### Line 446

> 446 et notum bello Xanthum uastumque Thoonem.

More are pressed by him, joined by *et*. One is known, described as *notum*. His fame is in war, named *bello*. He is Xanthus, called *Xanthum*. Another is huge as well, given as *uastumque*. The huge one is Thoon, who closes the line as *Thoonem*.
