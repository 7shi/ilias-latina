### 454–459 (*Iliad* 5.297–302)

#### Lines 454–455

> 454 Iamque manum Aeneas simul et Calydonius heros
>
> 455 contulerant, iactis inter se comminus hastis;

Things have moved on, marked by *Iamque*. A hand comes in, named *manum*, though what is done with it is not yet said. Aeneas comes in, named *Aeneas*. It is at the same time, marked by *simul*. Another is joined to him by *et*. He is the Calydonian hero, who closes the line as *Calydonius heros*. As the commentary says, the Calydonian hero is Diomedes.

They had brought their hands together, told with *contulerant*. Something had been hurled, described as *iactis*, though what it is is not yet said. It was at each other, given as *inter se*. It was at close quarters, marked by *comminus*. What had been hurled is spears, which close the line as *hastis*.

#### Lines 456–457

> 456 undique rimabant inimico corpora ferro,
>
> 457 et modo cedebant retro, modo deinde coibant.

It is on every side, marked by *undique*. They probe, told with *rimabant*. Something is hostile, described as *inimico*. What they probe is bodies, named *corpora*. What was hostile is steel, which closes the line as *ferro*.

More follows, joined by *et*. It is now one way, marked by *modo*. They fell back, told with *cedebant*. It was backward, marked by *retro*. Now it is the other way, marked by *modo*. It comes next, marked by *deinde*. They closed in, which closes the line as *coibant*.

#### Lines 458–459

> 458 Postquam utrique diu steterant nec uulnera magnus
>
> 459 qua daret infesto Tydides ense uidebat,

A time comes, marked by *Postquam*. It is both of them, named *utrique*. It was long, marked by *diu*. They had stood, told with *steterant*. A denial follows, marked by *nec*. Wounds come in, named *uulnera*. Someone is mighty, described as *magnus*, though who it is is not yet said.

It is a place, marked by *qua*. He would give them, told with *daret*. Something is hostile, described as *infesto*. The mighty one is the son of Tydeus, named *Tydides*. What was hostile is his sword, named *ense*. He saw none, which closes the line as *uidebat*. What he does then comes only in the next section.
