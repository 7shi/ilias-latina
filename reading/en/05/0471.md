### 471 (*Iliad* 5.370–374)

#### Line 471

> 471 atque ibi sidereae queritur sua uulnera matri.

More follows, joined by *atque*. It takes place there, marked by *ibi*. Someone is heavenly, described as *sidereae*, though who it is is not yet said. Venus complains, told with *queritur*. Something is her own, described as *sua*. It is her wounds, named *uulnera*. The heavenly one is her mother, who closes the line as *matri*. As the commentary says, her mother is Dione.
