### 526 (*Iliad* 5.663–664)

#### Line 526

> 526 Saucius egreditur medio certamine belli

Someone is wounded, described as *Saucius*, though who it is comes only in the next section. He withdraws, told with *egreditur*. The midst comes in, described as *medio*. It is the midst of a contest, named *certamine*. The contest is of war, which closes the line as *belli*.
