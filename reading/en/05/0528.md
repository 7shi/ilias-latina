### 528 (*Iliad* 5.677–678)

#### Line 528

> 528 et septem iuuenum fortissima corpora fundit.

More follows, marked by *et*. They were seven, described as *septem*. The seven were young men, called *iuuenum*. They were very brave, described as *fortissima*. What was very brave is their bodies, named *corpora*. Ulysses lays them low, told with *fundit*.
