### 528 (*Iliad* 5.677–678)

#### Line 528

> 528 et septem iuuenum fortissima corpora fundit.

More follows, joined by *et*. The count is seven, given as *septem*. They are of young men, named *iuuenum*. Something is very brave, described as *fortissima*. What was very brave is their bodies, named *corpora*. Ulysses lays them low, which closes the line as *fundit*.
