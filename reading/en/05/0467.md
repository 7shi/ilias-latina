### 467–468 (*Iliad* 5.330, 5.334–335)

#### Lines 467–468

> 467 fertur et in Venerem flagrantibus irruit armis,
>
> 468 et neque quem demens ferro petat inspicit aruis

Through the clouds Diomedes is borne on, told with *fertur*. More follows, joined by *et*. His attack turns against Venus, given as *in Venerem*. Something is blazing, described as *flagrantibus*, though what it is is not yet said. He rushes forward, told with *irruit*. What was blazing is his armor, which closes the line as *armis*.

Another thought is joined by *et*. A denial follows, marked by *neque*. The one attacked comes in, as *quem*. Diomedes is mad, described as *demens*. Iron comes in, called *ferro*. He attacks, told with *petat*. He takes regard, given as *inspicit*. It happens on the fields, which closes the line as *aruis*.
