### 394–395 (*Iliad* 5.1–2, 5.4–7, 5.3)

#### Lines 394–395

> 394 Bellica Pallas adest flagrantiaque ignibus arma
>
> 395 adiuuat atque animos iuueni uiresque ministrat.

Someone is warlike, described as *Bellica*. She is Pallas, named *Pallas*. As the commentary says, Pallas is Minerva, who gives particular aid to Diomedes. She is at hand, told with *adest*. Something is blazing as well, described as *flagrantiaque*, though what it is is not yet said. It blazes with fires, named *ignibus*. What blazes is his arms, named *arma*.

She aids them, told with *adiuuat*. More follows, joined by *atque*. Courage comes in, named *animos*. It is for the young man, named *iuueni*. Strength comes in as well, as *uiresque*. She supplies them, which closes the line as *ministrat*.
