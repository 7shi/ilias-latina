### 394–395 (*Iliad* 5.1–2, 5.4–7, 5.3)

#### Lines 394–395

> 394 Bellica Pallas adest flagrantiaque ignibus arma
>
> 395 adiuuat atque animos iuueni uiresque ministrat.

Someone is warlike, called *Bellica*. She is Pallas, named *Pallas*. As the commentary says, Pallas is Minerva. She is at hand, told with *adest*. Something is blazing as well, described as *flagrantiaque*. It blazes with fire, given as *ignibus*. What blazes is his armor, which closes the line as *arma*.

She aids his armor, told with *adiuuat*. More follows, joined by *atque*. Courage comes in, named *animos*. It is for the young man, called *iuueni*. Strength is added as well, as *uiresque*. The goddess supplies them, which closes the line as *ministrat*.
