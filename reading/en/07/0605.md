### 605–607 (*Iliad* 7.254, 7.261)

#### Line 605

> 605 Ille ictum celeri praeuidit callidus astu

Hector comes back, pointed to as *Ille*. A blow comes in, named *ictum*. Something is quick, described as *celeri*, though what it is is not yet said. He foresaw the blow, told with *praeuidit*. He is cunning, described as *callidus*. What is quick is his cunning, which closes the line as *astu*.

#### Line 606

> 606 tergaque summisit ferrumque umbone repellit.

His back comes in as well, named *tergaque*. He lowered it, told with *summisit*. The steel comes in as well, named *ferrumque*. A shield-boss comes in, named *umbone*. He drives the steel off, which closes the line as *repellit*.

#### Line 607

> 607 Sed leuis extremas clipei perlabitur oras

Something stands against this, marked by *Sed*. Something is light, described as *leuis*, though what it is is not yet said. Something is outer, described as *extremas*. It belongs to the shield, named *clipei*. Something slips along it, told with *perlabitur*. What is outer is the rims, which close the line as *oras*. What slips comes only in the next section.
