### 605–607 (*Iliad* 7.254, 7.261)

#### Line 605

> 605 Ille ictum celeri praeuidit callidus astu

Hector comes in, as *Ille*. A blow comes toward him, named *ictum*. Something was quick, described as *celeri*, though what was quick is not yet said. He anticipated it, told with *praeuidit*. He was cunning, called *callidus*. What was quick was his cunning, which closes the line as *astu*.

#### Line 606

> 606 tergaque summisit ferrumque umbone repellit.

His back also comes into motion, named *tergaque*. He lowered it, told with *summisit*. The steel too is turned, called *ferrumque*. He meets it with his shield's boss, given as *umbone*. He drives it off, told with *repellit*.

#### Line 607

> 607 Sed leuis extremas clipei perlabitur oras

The action turns, marked by *Sed*. The movement was light, described as *leuis*. Something was outer, called *extremas*, though what it is is not yet said. It belongs to the shield, named *clipei*. The blade slips along it, told with *perlabitur*. What was outer were the rims, which close the line as *oras*, though its further course comes only in the next section.
