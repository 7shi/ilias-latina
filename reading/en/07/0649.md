### 649 (*Iliad* 7.436–441)

#### Line 649

> 649 Tum renouant fossas et uallum robore cingunt.

A time then is marked by *Tum*. They restore something, told with *renouant*. What they restore is the trenches, named *fossas*. The rampart is joined to them by *et*, called *uallum*. Oak wood comes in, given as *robore*. They surround it with this, which ends the seventh book as *cingunt*.
