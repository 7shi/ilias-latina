### 649 (*Iliad* 7.436–441)

#### Line 649

> 649 Tum renouant fossas et uallum robore cingunt.

A time is set, marked by *Tum*. They restore something, told with *renouant*. What they restore is the trenches, named *fossas*. More is added, marked by *et*. The rampart comes in, named *uallum*. Oak comes in, named *robore*. They surround the rampart with it, which ends the seventh book as *cingunt*.
