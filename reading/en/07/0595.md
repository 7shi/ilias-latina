### 595–604 (*Iliad* 7.256–257, 7.260–262)

#### Lines 595–597

> 595 Non sic saetigeri exacuunt feruoribus iras
>
> 596 pectoribusque petunt uastis, modo dentibus uncis
>
> 597 alterni librant gladios et uulnera miscent.

It is not so, given as *Non sic*. Bristling ones come in, named *saetigeri*. As the commentary says, they are boars. They whet something, told with *exacuunt*. Frenzy comes in, named *feruoribus*. What they whet is rage, which closes the line as *iras*.

Chests come in as well, named *pectoribusque*. They charge, told with *petunt*. The chests are massive, described as *uastis*. It is now another way, marked by *modo*. Tusks come in, named *dentibus*. They are hooked, which closes the line as *uncis*.

They take turns, described as *alterni*. They swing something, told with *librant*. It is swords, named *gladios*. As the commentary says, the tusks are wielded like swords. More follows, joined by *et*. Wounds come in, named *uulnera*. They exchange them, which closes the line as *miscent*.

#### Line 598

> 598 fortia terga premunt spumantque per ora uicissim;

Something is sturdy, described as *fortia*. It is backs, named *terga*. They press on them, told with *premunt*. They foam as well, told with *spumantque*. It is at the mouth, given as *per ora*. It is in turn, which closes the line as *uicissim*.

#### Lines 599–600

> 599 fumiferae nubes concretaque fulgura et ignes
>
> 600 iactantur magnoque implentur murmure siluae.

Something is smoky, described as *fumiferae*. It is clouds, named *nubes*. Something is massed as well, described as *concretaque*. It is lightning, named *fulgura*. More follows, joined by *et*. Flames come in, which close the line as *ignes*.

They are hurled, told with *iactantur*. Something is mighty as well, described as *magnoque*, though what it is is not yet said. Something is filled, told with *implentur*. What is mighty is a rumbling, named *murmure*. What is filled is the woods, which close the line as *siluae*.

#### Line 601

> 601 Tales Priamides ardorque Aiacis in armis

Such ones come in, given as *Tales*. The son of Priam comes in, named *Priamides*. As the commentary says, Priamides is Hector. Ardor comes in as well, named *ardorque*. It is Ajax's, named *Aiacis*. It is in arms, which close the line as *in armis*.

#### Lines 602–604

> 602 Tandem animis teloque furens Telamonius Aiax
>
> 603 insignem bello petit Hectora, quaque patebat
>
> 604 nuda uiri ceruix, fulgentem derigit ensem.

At last something changes, marked by *Tandem*. Spirit comes in, named *animis*. A weapon comes in as well, named *teloque*. Someone rages with them, described as *furens*. He is Telamon's son, described as *Telamonius*. The one who rages is Ajax, who closes the line as *Aiax*.

Someone is famed, described as *insignem*. It is in war, named *bello*. Ajax attacks him, told with *petit*. The famed one is Hector, named *Hectora*. A place comes in, marked by *quaque*. It lay open, told with *patebat*.

Something is bare, described as *nuda*. It is the man's, named *uiri*. What is bare is his neck, named *ceruix*. Something is gleaming, described as *fulgentem*. Ajax directs it, told with *derigit*. What is gleaming is his sword, which closes the line as *ensem*.
