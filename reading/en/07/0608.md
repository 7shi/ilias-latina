### 608–610 (*Iliad* 7.262–264)

#### Line 608

> 608 ensis et exiguo ceruicem uulnere libat.

What slipped is the sword, named *ensis*. More follows, marked by *et*. Something was slight, described as *exiguo*, though what was slight is not yet said. The stroke reaches a neck, called *ceruicem*. What was slight was a wound, spoken of as *uulnere*. The sword grazes it, told with *libat*.

#### Lines 609–610

> 609 Acrius impugnans rursus consurgit in hostem
>
> 610 Priamides nec iam ferro Telamone creatum,

He fights more fiercely, as *Acrius*. He attacks his opponent, described as *impugnans*. He moves again, marked by *rursus*. He rises up, told with *consurgit*. The attack turns against the foe, given as *in hostem*.

The warrior is Priamides, named *Priamides*. He does not use his usual weapon, and that he does not is told with *nec*. It is no longer as before, as *iam*. The weapon was steel, called *ferro*. His opponent was born of Telamon, spoken of as *Telamone*. He was begotten by him, which closes the line as *creatum*, though how he attacks him comes only in the next section.
