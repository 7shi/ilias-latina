### 608–610 (*Iliad* 7.262–264)

#### Line 608

> 608 ensis et exiguo ceruicem uulnere libat.

What is light and slips is the sword, named *ensis*. More follows, joined by *et*. Something is slight, described as *exiguo*. A neck comes in, named *ceruicem*. What is slight is a wound, named *uulnere*. The sword grazes the neck, which closes the line as *libat*.

#### Lines 609–610

> 609 Acrius impugnans rursus consurgit in hostem
>
> 610 Priamides nec iam ferro Telamone creatum,

It is more fiercely, marked by *Acrius*. Someone is attacking, described as *impugnans*. It is again, marked by *rursus*. He rises, told with *consurgit*. It is against the foe, given as *in hostem*.

The one who rises is Priamides, named *Priamides*. A denial comes, marked by *nec*. It is no longer, marked by *iam*. Steel comes in, named *ferro*. Telamon comes in, named *Telamone*. Someone is his son, which closes the line as *creatum*. As the commentary says, Telamon's son is Ajax. How Priamides attacks him comes only in the next section.
