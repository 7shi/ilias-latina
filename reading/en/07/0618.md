### 618–619 (*Iliad* 7.274–279)

#### Lines 618–619

> 618 noxque subire polum: iuxta mittuntur, utrosque
>
> 619 qui dirimant a caede uiros, nec segnius illi

Night comes in as well, named *noxque*. It had begun to climb, told with *subire*. What it climbs is the sky, named *polum*. Something is close by, marked by *iuxta*. Some are sent, told with *mittuntur*. Some are both, described as *utrosque*, though who they are is not yet said.

The ones sent come in, as *qui*. They are to part someone, told with *dirimant*. They part them from slaughter, given as *a caede*. Those who are both are the men, named *uiros*. A denial comes, marked by *nec*. It is no less promptly, marked by *segnius*. Some come in, as *illi*. What they do comes only in the next section.
