### 618–619 (*Iliad* 7.274–279)

#### Lines 618–619

> 618 noxque subire polum: iuxta mittuntur, utrosque
>
> 619 qui dirimant a caede uiros, nec segnius illi

Night also approaches, named *noxque*. It was to climb, told with *subire*. What it climbs is the sky, called *polum*. Close by, someone approaches, marked by *iuxta*. Men are sent, told with *mittuntur*. Both fighters are meant, described as *utrosque*, though who they are is not yet said.

Those who are sent come in, as *qui*. They are to part them, told with *dirimant*. They part them from slaughter, given as *a caede*. Those who are parted are the men, named *uiros*. They do not delay, and that they do not is told with *nec*. They act no less promptly, marked by *segnius*. The champions answer them, as *illi*, though what they do comes only in the next section.
