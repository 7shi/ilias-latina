### 586 (*Iliad* 9.186–189)

#### Line 586

> 586 et cithara dulci durum lenibat amorem.

More follows, joined by *et*. A lyre comes in, named *cithara*. It is sweet, described as *dulci*. Something is harsh, described as *durum*, though what it is is not yet said. He was soothing it, told with *lenibat*. What was harsh is his love, which closes the line as *amorem*. As the commentary says, his harsh love suggests longing for Briseis, whom Agamemnon has taken from him.
