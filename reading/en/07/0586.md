### 586 (*Iliad* 9.186–189)

#### Line 586

> 586 et cithara dulci durum lenibat amorem.

He does something more, marked by *et*. A lyre comes in, named *cithara*. It was sweet, described as *dulci*. Something was harsh, called *durum*, though what was harsh is not yet said. He was soothing it, told with *lenibat*. What was harsh was his love, which closes the line as *amorem*.
