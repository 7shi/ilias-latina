### 614–616 (*Iliad* 7.272–273)

#### Lines 614–616

> 614 Quem leuat exceptum Grais inimicus Apollo
>
> 615 integratque animum; iam rursus ad arma coibant
>
> 616 stringebantque iterum gladios, cum fessus in undas

Hector comes back, as *Quem*. Someone lifts him, told with *leuat*. He is caught, described as *exceptum*. The Greeks come in, named *Grais*. Someone is hostile to them, described as *inimicus*. The hostile one is Apollo, who closes the line as *Apollo*.

Apollo restores something as well, told with *integratque*. It is his spirit, named *animum*. It is already, marked by *iam*. It is again, marked by *rursus*. Arms come in, given as *ad arma*. They were coming together, which closes the line as *coibant*.

They were drawing something as well, told with *stringebantque*. It is again, marked by *iterum*. It is swords, named *gladios*. A time is set, marked by *cum*. Someone is weary, described as *fessus*, though who it is is not yet said. The waves come in, given as *in undas*. What happens then comes only in the next section.
