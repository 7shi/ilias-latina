### 575–577 (*Iliad* 7.1–2, 7.55)

#### Line 575

> 575 Haec ait et portis acies petit acer apertis,

These words are finished, as *Haec*. He spoke them, told with *ait*. He turns to action, marked by *et*. Gates come in, named *portis*, though what kind they are is not yet said. The battle lines come in, called *acies*. He made for them, told with *petit*. He was eager, described as *acer*. The gates were open, which closes the line as *apertis*.

#### Lines 576–577

> 576 una deinde Paris. Postquam in certamina uentumst,
>
> 577 protinus in medium procedit maximus Hector

They went together, as *una*. Then another follows, marked by *deinde*. That companion is Paris, named *Paris*. A time after is marked by *Postquam*. The fighting comes in, given as *in certamina*. They reached it, told with *uentumst*.

At once something follows, marked by *protinus*. The midst comes in, given as *in medium*. Someone advances, told with *procedit*. He is mighty, described as *maximus*. The mighty one is Hector, who closes the line as *Hector*.
