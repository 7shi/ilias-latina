### 575–577 (*Iliad* 7.1–2, 7.55)

#### Line 575

> 575 Haec ait et portis acies petit acer apertis,

These things come in, pointed to as *Haec*. He says them, told with *ait*. More follows, joined by *et*. Gates come in, named *portis*, though what kind they are is not yet said. The battle lines come in, named *acies*. He makes for them, told with *petit*. He is eager, described as *acer*. The gates are open, which closes the line as *apertis*.

#### Lines 576–577

> 576 una deinde Paris. Postquam in certamina uentumst,
>
> 577 protinus in medium procedit maximus Hector

Someone goes with him, marked by *una*. It comes next, marked by *deinde*. The one who goes with him is Paris, named *Paris*. A time is set, marked by *Postquam*. The fighting comes in, given as *in certamina*. They reached it, told with *uentumst*.

It is at once, marked by *protinus*. The midst comes in, given as *in medium*. Someone advances, told with *procedit*. He is mighty, described as *maximus*. The mighty one is Hector, who closes the line as *Hector*.
