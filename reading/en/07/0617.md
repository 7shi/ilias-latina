### 617 (*Iliad* 7.282, 7.293)

#### Line 617

> 617 coeperat igniferos Titan immergere currus

Someone had begun, told with *coeperat*. Something is fire-bearing, described as *igniferos*, though what it is is not yet said. The weary one is Titan, named *Titan*. As the commentary says, Titan is the sun god. He had begun to plunge something, told with *immergere*. What is fire-bearing is his chariot, which closes the line as *currus*.
