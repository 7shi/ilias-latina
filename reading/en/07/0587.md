### 587–588 (*Iliad* 7.176, 7.181–183)

#### Lines 587–588

> 587 Ergo ubi deiectis auratam regis Atridae
>
> 588 sortibus in galeam magnus processerat Aiax,

So the story turns, as *Ergo*. When something happened is marked by *ubi*. Something was cast in, described as *deiectis*, though what was cast in is not yet said. Something was gilded, called *auratam*, though what was gilded is not yet said. It belonged to a king, named *regis*. That king is Atrides, who closes the line as *Atridae*.

What was cast in were the lots, spoken of as *sortibus*. What was gilded was the helmet, given as *in galeam*. Someone was mighty, described as *magnus*. He had stepped forth, told with *processerat*. The mighty one is Ajax, who closes the line as *Aiax*, though what follows comes only in the next section.
