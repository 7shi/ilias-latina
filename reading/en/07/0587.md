### 587–588 (*Iliad* 7.176, 7.181–183)

#### Lines 587–588

> 587 Ergo ubi deiectis auratam regis Atridae
>
> 588 sortibus in galeam magnus processerat Aiax,

It follows from this, marked by *Ergo*. A time is set, marked by *ubi*. Something is cast in, described as *deiectis*, though what it is is not yet said. Something is gilded, described as *auratam*. It belongs to a king, named *regis*. The king is Atrides, which closes the line as *Atridae*. As the commentary says, King Atrides is Agamemnon.

What was cast in is the lots, named *sortibus*. What was gilded is the helmet, given as *in galeam*. Someone is mighty, described as *magnus*. He had come forth, told with *processerat*. The mighty one is Ajax, who closes the line as *Aiax*. As the commentary says, this Ajax is the son of Telamon. What follows comes only in the next section.
