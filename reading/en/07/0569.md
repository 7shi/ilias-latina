### 569–571 (*Iliad* 6.469–470, 6.472–474)

#### Line 569

> 569 terribilemque fugit galeam cristamque comantem.

Something is terrifying, described as *terribilemque*. The child recoils from it, told with *fugit*. What is terrifying is the helmet, named *galeam*. A crest comes in as well, named *cristamque*. It is flowing, which closes the line as *comantem*.

#### Lines 570–571

> 570 Vtque caput iuuenis posito detexerat aere,
>
> 571 protinus infantem geminis amplectitur ulnis

A time is set, marked by *Vtque*. A head comes in, named *caput*. It is the young man's, named *iuuenis*. Something is laid aside, described as *posito*, though what it is is not yet said. He had uncovered his head, told with *detexerat*. What was laid aside is bronze, which closes the line as *aere*.

It is at once, marked by *protinus*. The infant comes back, named *infantem*. Something is twofold, described as *geminis*. He embraces him, told with *amplectitur*. What was twofold is his arms, which close the line as *ulnis*.
