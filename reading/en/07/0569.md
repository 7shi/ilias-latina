### 569–571 (*Iliad* 6.469–470, 6.472–474)

#### Line 569

> 569 terribilemque fugit galeam cristamque comantem.

What frightened him was terrifying, described as *terribilemque*, though what is terrifying is not yet said. The child recoils from it, told with *fugit*. What was terrifying is the helmet, called *galeam*. A crest too is joined to it, named *cristamque*. It was flowing, which closes the line as *comantem*.

#### Lines 570–571

> 570 Vtque caput iuuenis posito detexerat aere,
>
> 571 protinus infantem geminis amplectitur ulnis

A time when something happens is marked by *Vtque*. His head comes in, named *caput*. The warrior was young, called *iuuenis*. Something was laid aside, described as *posito*, though what it is is not yet said. He had uncovered his *caput*, told with *detexerat*. What was laid aside is bronze, which closes the line as *aere*.

At once something follows, marked by *protinus*. The child comes back, named *infantem*. They were a pair, described as *geminis*. He embraces him, told with *amplectitur*. The pair was his arms, which close the line as *ulnis*.
