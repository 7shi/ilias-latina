### 589–594 (*Iliad* 7.244–254)

#### Line 589

> 589 principio iactis committunt proelia telis:

It is at first, marked by *principio*. Something is hurled, described as *iactis*, though what it is is not yet said. They begin something, told with *committunt*. It is battle, named *proelia*. What was hurled is spears, which close the line as *telis*.

#### Lines 590–591

> 590 mox rigidos stringunt enses et fortibus armis
>
> 591 decernunt partesque oculis rimantur apertas

It is soon, marked by *mox*. Something is stout, described as *rigidos*. They draw it, told with *stringunt*. What is stout is swords, named *enses*. More follows, joined by *et*. Something is strong, described as *fortibus*. It is weapons, which close the line as *armis*.

They fight it out, told with *decernunt*. Places come in as well, named *partesque*. Eyes come in, named *oculis*. They search the places, told with *rimantur*. The places are unprotected, which closes the line as *apertas*.

#### Lines 592–594

> 592 et modo terga petunt, duros modo fortibus ictus
>
> 593 depellunt clipeis; ingens ad sidera clamor
>
> 594 tollitur et uastis impletur uocibus aer.

More follows, joined by *et*. It is now one way, marked by *modo*. Backs come in, named *terga*. They aim at them, told with *petunt*. Something is hard, described as *duros*, though what it is is not yet said. Now it is the other way, marked by *modo*. Something is strong, described as *fortibus*, though what it is is not yet said. What is hard is blows, which close the line as *ictus*.

They drive them off, told with *depellunt*. What is strong is shields, named *clipeis*. Something is huge, described as *ingens*. The stars come in, given as *ad sidera*. What is huge is a shout, which closes the line as *clamor*.

The shout is raised, told with *tollitur*. More follows, joined by *et*. Something is vast, described as *uastis*. Something is filled, told with *impletur*. What is vast is voices, named *uocibus*. What is filled is the air, which closes the line as *aer*.
