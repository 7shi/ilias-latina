### 611–613 (*Iliad* 7.264–272)

#### Lines 611–613

> 611 sed magno saxi iactu petit. At ferus Aiax
>
> 612 ingentem clipeo septemplice reppulit ictum
>
> 613 et iuuenem saxo percussum sternit eodem.

Something else is set against this, marked by *sed*. Something is mighty, described as *magno*. A stone comes in, named *saxi*. What is mighty is a throw, named *iactu*. He attacks with it, told with *petit*. Something is set against it, marked by *At*. Someone is fierce, described as *ferus*. The fierce one is Ajax, who closes the line as *Aiax*.

Something is massive, described as *ingentem*, though what it is is not yet said. A shield comes in, named *clipeo*. It is sevenfold, described as *septemplice*. Ajax beat something back, told with *reppulit*. What is massive is the blow, which closes the line as *ictum*.

More follows, joined by *et*. A young man comes in, named *iuuenem*. As the commentary says, the young man is Hector. A stone comes in, named *saxo*, though which stone is not yet said. He is struck, described as *percussum*. Ajax lays him low, told with *sternit*. The stone is the same one, which closes the line as *eodem*.
