### 611–613 (*Iliad* 7.264–272)

#### Lines 611–613

> 611 sed magno saxi iactu petit. At ferus Aiax
>
> 612 ingentem clipeo septemplice reppulit ictum
>
> 613 et iuuenem saxo percussum sternit eodem.

He turns instead, marked by *sed*. A throw was mighty, described as *magno*. It was with a stone, named *saxi*. He attacks with that throw, called *iactu*. He attacks his foe, told with *petit*. The combat turns at once, as *At*. Someone was fierce, spoken of as *ferus*. The fierce one is Ajax, who closes the line as *Aiax*.

Something was massive, described as *ingentem*, though what was massive is not yet said. He meets it with a shield, named *clipeo*. The shield was sevenfold, called *septemplice*. Ajax beat it back, told with *reppulit*. What was massive was the blow, which closes the line as *ictum*.

More follows, as *et*. He turns on the young warrior, named *iuuenem*. A stone is turned against him, called *saxo*. Hector was struck, described as *percussum*. Ajax lays him low, told with *sternit*. The stone was the same, which closes the line as *eodem*.
