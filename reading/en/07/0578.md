### 578 (*Iliad* 7.67, 7.73–75)

#### Line 578

> 578 Graiorumque duces inuictis prouocat armis.

The Greeks come in, named *Graiorumque*. He turns to their leaders, called *duces*. Something was unconquered, described as *inuictis*, though what it is is not yet said. Hector challenges them, told with *prouocat*. What was unconquered is his arms, which close the line as *armis*.
