### 644–645 (*Iliad* 7.413)

#### Lines 644–645

> 644 Paruit is monitis iterumque ad castra reuersus
>
> 645 Troiae contemptum duro se reddit ab hoste.

Someone obeyed, told with *Paruit*. The one who obeyed is Idaeus, as *is*. What he obeyed is the orders, named *monitis*. It is again as well, marked by *iterumque*. The camp comes in, given as *ad castra*. He has turned back, described as *reuersus*.

The camp is of Troy, named *Troiae*. He is scorned, described as *contemptum*. Someone is harsh, described as *duro*, though who it is is not yet said. He himself comes in, as *se*. He returns, told with *reddit*. The harsh one by whom he is scorned is the enemy, which closes the line as *ab hoste*.
