### 644–645 (*Iliad* 7.413)

#### Lines 644–645

> 644 Paruit is monitis iterumque ad castra reuersus
>
> 645 Troiae contemptum duro se reddit ab hoste.

He obeyed, told with *Paruit*. The messenger comes in, as *is*. What he obeyed was the orders, named *monitis*. It happens again, marked by *iterumque*. The way leads to the camp, given as *ad castra*. He has turned back, described as *reuersus*.

The camp belongs to Troy, named *Troiae*. He is spurned, called *contemptum*. Someone is harsh, described as *duro*, though who it is is not yet said. He presents himself, as *se*. He returns, told with *reddit*. The harsh one is the enemy, which closes the line as *ab hoste*. So *duro* and *ab hoste* come together, with *se reddit* between them.
