### 585 (*Iliad* 7.229–230)

#### Line 585

> 585 procedunt; aberat nam Troum terror Achilles

The champions step forward, told with *procedunt*. Someone was absent, told with *aberat*. The reason follows, marked by *nam*. The Trojans come in, called *Troum*. Their terror comes in, named *terror*. That terror was Achilles, who closes the line as *Achilles*.
