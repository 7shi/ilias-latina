### 585 (*Iliad* 7.229–230)

#### Line 585

> 585 procedunt; aberat nam Troum terror Achilles

They step forward, told with *procedunt*. Someone is absent, told with *aberat*. A reason comes, marked by *nam*. The Trojans come in, named *Troum*. Their terror comes in, named *terror*. The terror is Achilles, who closes the line as *Achilles*.
