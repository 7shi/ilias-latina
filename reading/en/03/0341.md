### 341–343 (*Iliad* 3.455–459)

#### Lines 341–343

> 341 Quem frater socias acuens in bella cateruas
>
> 342 adiuuat et forti pulsos Phrygas increpat ore
>
> 343 seruarique iubet leges Helenamque reposcit.

Menelaus comes back, as *Quem*. His brother comes in, named *frater*. As the commentary says, the brother of Menelaus is Agamemnon. Something is allied, described as *socias*, though what it is is not yet said. The brother is spurring them, described as *acuens*. It is into war, given as *in bella*. What was allied is bands, named *cateruas*.

He helps Menelaus, told with *adiuuat*. More follows, joined by *et*. Something is forceful, described as *forti*, though what it is is not yet said. Some have been driven back, described as *pulsos*. They are the Phrygians, named *Phrygas*. He rebukes them, told with *increpat*. What was forceful is his voice, named *ore*.

Something is to be kept, told with *seruarique*. He orders it, told with *iubet*. What is to be kept is the terms, named *leges*. Helen comes in, named *Helenamque*. He demands her back, which ends the third book as *reposcit*.
