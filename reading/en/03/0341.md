### 341–343 (*Iliad* 3.455–459)

#### Lines 341–343

> 341 Quem frater socias acuens in bella cateruas
>
> 342 adiuuat et forti pulsos Phrygas increpat ore
>
> 343 seruarique iubet leges Helenamque reposcit.

Menelaus comes back, as *Quem*. His brother comes forward to support him, named *frater*. As the commentary says, this brother is Agamemnon. He rouses what fights alongside him, described as *socias*, though what troops they are is not yet said. He stirs them to readiness, sharpened with *acuens*. He spurs them on to war, met *in bella*. What was described as *socias* is revealed as the allied battalions, marshaled as *cateruas*.

He aids his brother's claim, supported with *adiuuat*. He also turns to address the enemy, joined by *et*. He speaks with power, described as *forti*, though in what voice is not yet said. The Trojans are now beaten back, marked as *pulsos*. He directs his anger at the Phrygians, named *Phrygas*. He rebukes them loudly, denounced with *increpat*. What was described as *forti* is revealed as his powerful voice, met as *ore*.

He demands that the treaty be kept, urged as *seruarique*. He commands compliance from the Trojans, ordered with *iubet*. The solemn terms stand as the covenant, met as *leges*. He also demands Helen back, sought as *Helenamque*. He claims her return, bringing the third book to a close with *reposcit*.
