### 302–306 (*Iliad* 3.361–368)

#### Lines 302–305

> 302 Dardaniumque premit iuuenem. Mox ense rigente
>
> 303 cedentem retro dum desuper appetit hostem,
>
> 304 splendidus extremas galeae percussus ad oras
>
> 305 dissiluit mucro; gemuerunt agmina Graium.

Someone is Dardanian, described as *Dardaniumque*. Atrides presses him, told with *premit*. The Dardanian is a youth, named *iuuenem*. As the commentary says, the Dardanian youth is Paris. Something happens soon, marked by *Mox*. A sword comes in, named *ense*. It is unyielding, described as *rigente*.

Someone is retreating, described as *cedentem*. It is backward, marked by *retro*. A time is set, marked by *dum*. It is from above, marked by *desuper*. Atrides strikes at him, told with *appetit*. The retreating one is the enemy, named *hostem*.

Something gleams, described as *splendidus*, though what it is is not yet said. Something is outer, described as *extremas*, though what it is is not yet said either. It belongs to the helmet, named *galeae*. Something is struck, described as *percussus*. What was outer is the rim, given as *ad oras*.

Something shatters, told with *dissiluit*. What gleamed is the blade, named *mucro*. Someone groaned, told with *gemuerunt*. It is the ranks, named *agmina*. They are the ranks of the Greeks, which close the line as *Graium*.

#### Line 306

> 306 Tum uero ardescit, quamuis manus ense carebat,

Then something happens, marked by *Tum*. It happens indeed, marked by *uero*. Menelaus blazes up, told with *ardescit*. It is in spite of something, marked by *quamuis*. A hand comes in, named *manus*. A sword comes next, named *ense*. The hand was without it, which closes the line as *carebat*.
