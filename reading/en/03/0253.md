### 253 (*Iliad* 3.16)

#### Line 253

> 253 cum Paris, exitium Troiae funestaque flamma,

The action moves to a new moment, marked by *cum*. A Trojan warrior comes forward, named *Paris*. He is an embodiment of ruin, spoken of as *exitium*. The city that suffers this ruin is Troy, called *Troiae*. He is also a deadly flame, closing the line as *funestaque flamma*. What he does comes only in the next section.
