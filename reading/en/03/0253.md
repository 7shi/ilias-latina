### 253 (*Iliad* 3.16)

#### Line 253

> 253 cum Paris, exitium Troiae funestaque flamma,

Then a moment comes, marked by *cum*. Paris comes in, named *Paris*. He is a ruin, named *exitium*. It is the ruin of Troy, named *Troiae*. He is also a deadly flame, which closes the line as *funestaque flamma*. What Paris does comes only in the next section.
