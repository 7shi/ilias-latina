### 339–340 (*Iliad* 3.449–450)

#### Lines 339–340

> 339 Interea toto Menelaus in agmine Troum
>
> 340 quaerit Alexandrum uictorque huc fertur et illuc.

Meanwhile the action returns to the plain, marked by *Interea*. The search extends through the whole host, described as *toto*, though through what is not yet said. The Greek champion continues the hunt, appearing as *Menelaus*. What was described as *toto* is revealed as the enemy ranks, met *in agmine*. Those battle lines belong to the Trojans, named *Troum*.

He seeks his vanished rival, told with *quaerit*. The man he looks for is Alexander, named *Alexandrum*. He searches as the conqueror of the field, praised as *uictorque*. His pursuit carries him in one direction, turned *huc*. He is swept along by his anger, carried with *fertur*. His course turns again, joined by *et*. He ranges in the opposite direction, closing the section as *illuc*.
