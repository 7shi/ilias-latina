### 339–340 (*Iliad* 3.449–450)

#### Lines 339–340

> 339 Interea toto Menelaus in agmine Troum
>
> 340 quaerit Alexandrum uictorque huc fertur et illuc.

Time moves on, marked by *Interea*. Something is whole, described as *toto*, though what it is is not yet said. Menelaus comes in, named *Menelaus*. What was whole is the host, given as *in agmine*. It is the Trojans' host, named *Troum*.

He searches, told with *quaerit*. The one he seeks is Alexander, named *Alexandrum*. He is the victor, as *uictorque*. He goes this way, marked by *huc*. He is carried along, told with *fertur*. More follows, joined by *et*. He goes that way too, which closes the line as *illuc*.
