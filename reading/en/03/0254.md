### 254 (*Iliad* 3.30–31)

#### Line 254

> 254 armatum aduerso Menelaum ex agmine cernit

Someone is armed, described as *armatum*, though who it is is not yet said. Something stands opposite, described as *aduerso*, though what it is is not yet said. The armed one is Menelaus, named *Menelaum*. What was described as *aduerso* is the column he stands in, given as *ex agmine*. Paris sees him, which closes the line as *cernit*.
