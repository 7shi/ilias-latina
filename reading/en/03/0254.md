### 254 (*Iliad* 3.30–31)

#### Line 254

> 254 armatum aduerso Menelaum ex agmine cernit

What Paris encounters is someone in arms, described as *armatum*, though who is armed is not yet said. That enemy stands in the opposing ranks, situated as *aduerso*, though what formation it is is not yet said. What was described as *armatum* is revealed as Menelaus, entering as *Menelaum*. What was situated as *aduerso* is revealed as the battle line, spoken of as *ex agmine*. Paris catches sight of his foe, closing the line as *cernit*.
