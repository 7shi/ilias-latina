### 281–282 (*Iliad* 3.340–341, 3.335–338)

#### Lines 281–282

> 281 Interea toto procedit ab agmine Troum
>
> 282 pulcher Alexander, clipeoque insignis et hasta.

Meanwhile, attention turns to a new champion, marked by *Interea*. He comes out from the whole host, described as *toto*, though what body of troops it is is not yet said. He advances into the open field, told with *procedit*. What was described as *toto* is revealed as the battle column, emerging *ab agmine*. The army belongs to the Trojans, named *Troum*.

The warrior stepping forward is handsome, praised as *pulcher*. He comes into view as *Alexander*. As the commentary says, this Alexander is Paris. He is equipped also with a shield, joined as *clipeoque*. He stands out conspicuously, described as *insignis*. His spear is added by *et*, closing the line as *hasta*.
