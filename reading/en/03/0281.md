### 281–282 (*Iliad* 3.340–341, 3.335–338)

#### Lines 281–282

> 281 Interea toto procedit ab agmine Troum
>
> 282 pulcher Alexander, clipeoque insignis et hasta.

Time moves on, marked by *Interea*. Something is whole, described as *toto*, though what it is is not yet said. Someone comes forward, told with *procedit*. What was whole is the host, given as *ab agmine*. It is the host of the Trojans, named *Troum*.

He is handsome, described as *pulcher*. He is Alexander, named *Alexander*. As the commentary says, Alexander is another name for Paris. A shield comes next, named *clipeoque*. He is conspicuous, described as *insignis*. More follows, joined by *et*. It is a spear, which closes the line as *hasta*.
