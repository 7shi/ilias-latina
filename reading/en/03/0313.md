### 313 (*Iliad* 3.377–378)

#### Line 313

> 313 in medios mittit proceres rursumque recurrit

The middle of a crowd comes in, given as *in medios*, though whose crowd it is is not yet said. He throws the helmet, told with *mittit*. The crowd is the chiefs, named *proceres*. It happens once more, given as *rursumque*. He runs back, which closes the line as *recurrit*.
