### 313 (*Iliad* 3.377–378)

#### Line 313

> 313 in medios mittit proceres rursumque recurrit

He flings the helmet into the midst of the host, met *in medios*, though among whom is not yet said. He casts the empty prize away, told with *mittit*. What was met *in medios* is revealed as the Greek chiefs, named *proceres*. He also wheels around once more, pictured as *rursumque*. Menelaus charges back into the fray, closing the section with *recurrit*.
