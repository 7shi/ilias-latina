### 252 (*Iliad* 3.15)

#### Line 252

> 252 Iamque duae stabant acies fulgentibus armis,

Things have moved on, marked by *Iamque*. Two of something are counted, given as *duae*, though two of what is not yet said. They were standing, told with *stabant*. What was counted as *duae* is the battle lines, named *acies*. Their arms gleam, which close the line as *fulgentibus armis*.
