### 252 (*Iliad* 3.15)

#### Line 252

> 252 Iamque duae stabant acies fulgentibus armis,

Now a new moment has arrived, marked by *Iamque*. Their number is two, counted as *duae*, though what two are meant is not yet said. They were standing in place, told with *stabant*. What was counted as *duae* is revealed as the battle lines, named *acies*. They stand ready with gleaming weapons, closing the line as *fulgentibus armis*.
