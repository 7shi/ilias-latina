### 317–319 (*Iliad* 3.383–384, 3.424–427)

#### Lines 317–319

> 317 Ipsa dehinc Helenam muris accersit ab altis
>
> 318 Dardanioque suos Paridi deducit amores.
>
> 319 Quem tali postquam conspexit uoce locutast:

Venus herself comes in, as *Ipsa*. She acts next, marked by *dehinc*. Helen comes in, named *Helenam*. Walls come next, named *muris*. Venus summons her, told with *accersit*. It is from the high walls, given as *ab altis*.

Someone is Dardanian, described as *Dardanioque*. Something is his own, described as *suos*, though what it is is not yet said. The Dardanian is Paris, named *Paridi*. Venus leads her down, told with *deducit*. What was his own is his beloved, named *amores*.

Paris comes back, as *Quem*. Something is of such a kind, described as *tali*, though what it is is not yet said. It comes after, marked by *postquam*. She had seen him, told with *conspexit*. What was of such a kind is a voice, named *uoce*. She spoke, which closes the line as *locutast*.
