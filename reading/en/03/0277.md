### 277 (*Iliad* 3.76, 3.111)

#### Line 277

> 277 Dicta refert Hector: placuit sententia Grais.

The words come next, named *Dicta*. Someone carries them, told with *refert*. He is Hector, named *Hector*. Something pleased, told with *placuit*. It is the proposal, named *sententia*. It pleased the Greeks, who close the line as *Grais*.
