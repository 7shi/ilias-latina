### 277 (*Iliad* 3.76, 3.111)

#### Line 277

> 277 Dicta refert Hector: placuit sententia Grais.

The words just spoken by Paris are gathered up as *Dicta*. He carries them across to the other army, told with *refert*. The bearer of the message is named *Hector*. The proposal meets with approval, greeted with *placuit*. What wins their agreement is this plan, appearing as *sententia*. The ones who accept it are the Greeks, closing the line as *Grais*.
