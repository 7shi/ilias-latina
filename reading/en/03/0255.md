### 255 (*Iliad* 3.32–35)

#### Line 255

> 255 seque uelut uiso perterritus angue recepit

Paris also moves himself away, told as *seque*. His flight is like that of another, marked by *uelut*. Something has been seen, told with *uiso*, though what was seen is not yet said. That sight brings sudden terror, described as *perterritus*. What was seen with *uiso* is revealed as a snake, named *angue*. Paris drew back in retreat, closing the line as *recepit*.
