### 255 (*Iliad* 3.32–35)

#### Line 255

> 255 seque uelut uiso perterritus angue recepit

Paris does something to himself, given as *seque*. It is like something else, marked by *uelut*. Something has been seen, described as *uiso*, though what it is is not yet said. Someone is terrified, described as *perterritus*. What was seen is a snake, named *angue*. Paris drew back, which closes the line as *recepit*.
