### 920 (*Iliad* 21.328)

#### Line 920

> 920 propellit fluctus. Quem longe prouida Iuno

He drives something back, told with *propellit*. What is scattered is waves, named *fluctus*. Achilles comes back, as *Quem*. A distance comes in, marked by *longe*. Someone is provident, described as *prouida*. Juno comes in, who closes the line as *Iuno*. As the commentary says, Juno is the Roman counterpart of Hera, queen of the gods. What she does comes only in the next section.
