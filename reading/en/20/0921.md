### 921 (*Iliad* 21.330–333)

#### Line 921

> 921 asseruit, rapidae quia cederet, ignibus, undae,

She sustained him, told with *asseruit*. Something is rushing, described as *rapidae*, though what it is is not yet said. A reason comes in, marked by *quia*. He was yielding, told with *cederet*. Fire comes in, named *ignibus*. What is rushing is a wave, which closes the line as *undae*. What follows comes only in the next section.
