### 928–929 (*Iliad* 21.528–529, 21.540–543)

#### Line 928

> 928 Percussi dubitant trepida formidine Troes

Some are stricken, described as *Percussi*, though who they are is not yet said. They waver, told with *dubitant*. Trembling fear comes in, given as *trepida formidine*. Those who are stricken are Trojans, who close the line as *Troes*.

#### Line 929

> 929 atque intra muros exhausta paene salute

More is added, marked by *atque*. Within the walls comes in, given as *intra muros*. Something is exhausted, described as *exhausta*. It is almost so, marked by *paene*. Safety comes in, which closes the line as *salute*. What they do comes only in the next section.
