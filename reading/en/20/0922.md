### 922–923 (*Iliad* 21.385–391, 21.520–521)

#### Line 922

> 922 sanctaque pugnarunt inter se numina diuum.

Some are holy as well, described as *sanctaque*, though what they are is not yet said. They fought, told with *pugnarunt*. Among themselves comes in, given as *inter se*. What is holy is divine powers, named *numina*. The gods come in, who close the line as *diuum*.

#### Line 923

> 923 Rursus agit Phrygias ingenti caede cateruas

Again something happens, marked by *Rursus*. Someone drives something, told with *agit*. Some are Phrygian, described as *Phrygias*, though who they are is not yet said. Huge slaughter comes in, given as *ingenti caede*. Those who are Phrygian are companies, which close the line as *cateruas*. As the commentary says, the Phrygian companies are the Trojans. Who drives them comes only in the next section.
