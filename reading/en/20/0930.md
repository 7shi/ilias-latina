### 930 (*Iliad* 21.606–611)

#### Line 930

> 930 confugiunt portasque obiecto robore firmant.

They flee for refuge, told with *confugiunt*. Gates come in as well, named *portasque*. Timber set against them comes in, given as *obiecto robore*. They make them fast, which ends the twentieth book as *firmant*.
