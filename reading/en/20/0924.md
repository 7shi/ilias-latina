### 924–927 (*Iliad* 21.520–525)

#### Lines 924–925

> 924 horridus Aeacides bellique ardore resumpto
>
> 925 funereas acies horrendaque proelia miscet.

Someone is bristling, described as *horridus*. The one who drives them is Aeacides, called *Aeacides*. War comes in as well, named *bellique*. Ardor comes in, named *ardore*. It is renewed, which closes the line as *resumpto*.

Something is deadly, described as *funereas*. Battle lines come in, named *acies*. Dreadful battles come in as well, given as *horrendaque proelia*. He throws them into turmoil, which closes the line as *miscet*.

#### Lines 926–927

> 926 Non illum uis ulla mouet, non saeua fatigant
>
> 927 pectora bellando; uires successus adauget.

A denial comes, marked by *Non*. Achilles comes back, as *illum*. Any force comes in, given as *uis ulla*. It moves him, told with *mouet*. A denial comes again, marked by *non*. Something is savage, described as *saeua*, though what it is is not yet said. Something tires, which closes the line as *fatigant*.

What is savage is his heart, named *pectora*. Fighting comes in, told with *bellando*. Strength comes in, named *uires*. Success comes in, named *successus*. It increases it, which closes the line as *adauget*.
