### 1012 (*Iliad* 23.826, 23.844–849)

#### Line 1012

> 1012 et disco forti Polypoetes depulit omnes

More is added, marked by *et*. A heavy discus comes in, given as *disco forti*. Polypoetes comes in, called *Polypoetes*. As the commentary says, Polypoetes is a leader of the Lapiths. He outdid them, told with *depulit*. All of them come in, which close the line as *omnes*. What follows comes only in the next section.
