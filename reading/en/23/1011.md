### 1011 (*Iliad* 23.664–665)

#### Line 1011

> 1011 caestibus aduersis cunctos superauit Epeos

Gauntlets face to face come in, given as *caestibus aduersis*. All of them come in, described as *cunctos*. Someone overcame them, told with *superauit*. The one who overcame them is Epeus, who closes the line as *Epeos*. As the commentary says, Epeus is the son of Panopeus.
