### 1014 (*Iliad* 24.1–3)

#### Line 1014

> 1014 in sua castra redit turbis comitatus Achilles.

Someone's own place comes in, given as *in sua*, though what it is is not yet said. What is his own is the camp, named *castra*. Someone returns, told with *redit*. Crowds come in, named *turbis*. He is accompanied, described as *comitatus*. The one who returns is Achilles, which ends the twenty-third book as *Achilles*.
