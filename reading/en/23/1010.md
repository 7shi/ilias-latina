### 1010 (*Iliad* 23.725–736)

#### Line 1010

> 1010 cuius decepit uires Laertius astu;

Ajax comes back, as *cuius*. Someone foiled something, told with *decepit*. Strength comes in, named *uires*. Laertes' son comes in, called *Laertius*. As the commentary says, Laertes' son is Ulysses. Cunning comes in, which closes the line as *astu*.
