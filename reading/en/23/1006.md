### 1006 (*Iliad* 24.14–18)

#### Line 1006

> 1006 Tum circa tumulum miseros rapit Hectoris artus

A time is set, marked by *Tum*. A burial mound comes in, given as *circa tumulum*. As the commentary says, the mound is Patroclus'. Some are pitiable, described as *miseros*, though what they are is not yet said. He drags them, told with *rapit*. Hector comes in, called *Hectoris*. What they are is limbs, which close the line as *artus*. What follows comes only in the next section.
