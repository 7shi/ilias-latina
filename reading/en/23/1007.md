### 1007 (*Iliad* 23.257–261)

#### Line 1007

> 1007 et uapido cineri ludorum indicit honores.

More is added, marked by *et*. Spent ashes come in, given as *uapido cineri*. As the commentary says, the ashes are those of Patroclus. Games come in, named *ludorum*. He proclaims something, told with *indicit*. Honors come in, which close the line as *honores*.
