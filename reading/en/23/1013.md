### 1013 (*Iliad* 23.882–883)

#### Line 1013

> 1013 Merionesque arcu. Tandem certamine misso

Meriones comes back as well, called *Merionesque*. A bow comes in, named *arcu*. At last something changes, marked by *Tandem*. The contest is ended, given as *certamine misso*. What follows comes only in the next section.
