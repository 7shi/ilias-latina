### 845–846 (*Iliad* 18.23–24, 18.27, 18.25)

#### Lines 845–846

> 845 unguibus ora secat comptosque in puluere crines
>
> 846 deformat, scindit firmo de pectore uestes

His nails come in, named *unguibus*. His face comes in, named *ora*. He tears it, told with *secat*. Something is well-groomed as well, described as *comptosque*, though what it is is not yet said. The dust comes in, given as *in puluere*. What is well-groomed is his hair, which closes the line as *crines*.

He disfigures it, told with *deformat*. He rends something, told with *scindit*. Something is strong, described as *firmo*, though what it is is not yet said. What is strong is his chest, given as *de pectore*. His clothes come in, which close the line as *uestes*.
