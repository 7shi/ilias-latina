### 885 (*Iliad* 18.541–542)

#### Line 885

> 885 Rura colunt alii, sulcant grauia arua iuuenci

The countryside comes in, named *Rura*. Some till it, told with *colunt*. Others come in, as *alii*. Some furrow something, told with *sulcant*. Heavy fields come in, given as *grauia arua*. Young oxen come in, who close the line as *iuuenci*.
