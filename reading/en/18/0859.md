### 859–861 (*Iliad* 19.3, 19.12–13, 19.15–18)

#### Line 859

> 859 Mox effecta refert diuinis artibus arma.

A time is set, marked by *Mox*. Something is finished, described as *effecta*, though what it is is not yet said. He brings it back, told with *refert*. Divine arts come in, given as *diuinis artibus*. What is finished is armor, which closes the line as *arma*.

#### Lines 860–861

> 860 Euolat inde Thetis; quae postquam magnus Achilles
>
> 861 induit, in clipeum uultus conuertit atroces.

Someone flies off, told with *Euolat*. A place is set, marked by *inde*. Thetis comes in, called *Thetis*. The armor comes back, as *quae*. A time is set, marked by *postquam*. Great Achilles comes in, given as *magnus Achilles*.

He puts it on, told with *induit*. The shield comes in, given as *in clipeum*. His gaze comes in, named *uultus*. He turns it, told with *conuertit*. It is fierce, which closes the line as *atroces*.
