### 857–858 (*Iliad* 18.468–470, 18.474–477)

#### Lines 857–858

> 857 Excitat Aetnaeos calidis fornacibus ignes
>
> 858 Mulciber et ualidis fuluum domat ictibus aurum.

Someone stirs up something, told with *Excitat*. Something is Aetnaean, described as *Aetnaeos*, though what it is is not yet said. As the commentary says, Aetnaean is from Aetna, the volcano in Sicily associated with Vulcan's forge. Hot furnaces come in, given as *calidis fornacibus*. What is Aetnaean is fires, which close the line as *ignes*.

Mulciber comes in, called *Mulciber*. As the commentary says, Mulciber is another name for Vulcan. More is added, marked by *et*. Something is mighty, described as *ualidis*, though what it is is not yet said. Something is yellow, described as *fuluum*. He tames something, told with *domat*. What is mighty is blows, named *ictibus*. Gold comes in, which closes the line as *aurum*.
