### 862–863 (*Iliad* 18.483–485)

#### Lines 862–863

> 862 Illic Ignipotens mundi caelauerat arcem
>
> 863 sideraque et liquidis redimitas undique nymphis

A place is set, marked by *Illic*. Ignipotens comes in, called *Ignipotens*. As the commentary says, Ignipotens is Vulcan. The world comes in, named *mundi*. He had engraved something, told with *caelauerat*. Its vault comes in, which closes the line as *arcem*.

Stars come in as well, named *sideraque*. More is added, marked by *et*. Something is watery, described as *liquidis*, though what it is is not yet said. Something is wreathed, described as *redimitas*. Every side comes in, marked by *undique*. What is watery is nymphs, which close the line as *nymphis*. What is wreathed comes only in the next section.
