### 887 (*Iliad* 18.561–562)

#### Line 887

> 887 et gaudet pressis immundus uinitor uuis;

More is added, marked by *et*. Someone rejoices, told with *gaudet*. Something is pressed, described as *pressis*, though what it is is not yet said. A grimy vine-grower comes in, given as *immundus uinitor*. What is pressed is grapes, which close the line as *uuis*.
