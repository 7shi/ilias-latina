### 864–875 (*Iliad* 18.607–608, 18.485–489, 18.484)

#### Line 864

> 864 Oceani terras et cinctum Nerea circum

Oceanus comes in, called *Oceani*. As the commentary says, Oceanus is a great river encircling the world and the divine being who embodied it. What is wreathed is lands, named *terras*. More is added, marked by *et*. Someone is set around, described as *cinctum*. Who is set around is Nereus, called *Nerea*. As the commentary says, Nereus is a sea god. A place around is set, which closes the line as *circum*.

#### Line 865

> 865 astrorumque uices dimensaque tempora noctis,

Stars come in as well, named *astrorumque*. Their cycles come in, named *uices*. Something is measured as well, described as *dimensaque*, though what it is is not yet said. What is measured is hours, named *tempora*. Night comes in, which closes the line as *noctis*.

#### Lines 866–867

> 866 quattuor et mundi partes, quantum Arctos ab Austro
>
> 867 et quantum occasus roseo distaret ab ortu,

Four things come in, described as *quattuor*, though what they are is not yet said. More is added, marked by *et*. The world comes in, named *mundi*. What there are four of is parts, named *partes*. A distance comes in, marked by *quantum*. Arctos comes in, called *Arctos*. As the commentary says, Arctos is the Bear constellation, marking the north. Auster comes in, given as *ab Austro*. As the commentary says, Auster is the south wind, marking the south.

More is added, marked by *et*. Another distance comes in, marked by *quantum*. Sunset comes in, named *occasus*. Something is rosy, described as *roseo*, though what it is is not yet said. Something lay apart, told with *distaret*. What is rosy is sunrise, which closes the line as *ab ortu*.

#### Lines 868–870

> 868 Lucifer unde suis, unde Hesperus unus uterque
>
> 869 exoreretur equis, et quantum in orbe mearet
>
> 870 Luna caua et nitida lustraret lampade caelum;

Lucifer comes in, called *Lucifer*. A place is set, marked by *unde*. Something is his own, described as *suis*, though what it is is not yet said. Another place is set, marked by *unde*. Hesperus comes in, called *Hesperus*. As the commentary says, Lucifer and Hesperus are the morning and evening star, two names for the planet Venus. They are both one, given as *unus uterque*. One rose, told with *exoreretur*. What is his own is horses, named *equis*.

More is added, marked by *et*. Another distance comes in, marked by *quantum*. A circuit comes in, given as *in orbe*. Someone traveled, told with *mearet*. Luna comes in, called *Luna*. As the commentary says, Luna is the Moon, imagined as a goddess. She is crescent, described as *caua*. More is added, marked by *et*. Something is shining, described as *nitida*, though what it is is not yet said. She lit something, told with *lustraret*. What is shining is a lamp, named *lampade*. Heaven comes in, which closes the line as *caelum*.

#### Lines 871–873

> 871 addideratque fretis sua numina: Nerea magnum
>
> 872 Oceanumque senem nec eundem Protea semper,
>
> 873 Tritonasque feros et amantem Dorida fluctus;

He had added something as well, told with *addideratque*. The seas come in, named *fretis*. Their own deities come in, given as *sua numina*. Nereus comes back, called *Nerea*. He is mighty, which closes the line as *magnum*.

Oceanus comes back as well, called *Oceanumque*. He is aged, described as *senem*. A denial comes, marked by *nec*. Someone is the same, described as *eundem*. Proteus comes in, called *Protea*. As the commentary says, Proteus can change his shape. All time comes in, which closes the line as *semper*.

Tritons come in as well, called *Tritonasque*. As the commentary says, the Tritons are wild sea creatures, partly human with fishlike lower bodies. They are wild, described as *feros*. More is added, marked by *et*. Someone is loving, described as *amantem*. Doris comes in, called *Dorida*. As the commentary says, Doris is Nereus' wife. The waves come in, which close the line as *fluctus*.

#### Line 874

> 874 fecerat et liquidas mira Nereidas arte.

He had fashioned something, told with *fecerat*. More is added, marked by *et*. Some are watery, described as *liquidas*, though who they are is not yet said. Something is marvelous, described as *mira*. Those who are watery are Nereids, called *Nereidas*. As the commentary says, the Nereids are the daughters of Nereus and Doris, sea nymphs whose number includes Thetis. What is marvelous is skill, which closes the line as *arte*.

#### Line 875

> 875 Terra gerit siluas horrendaque monstra ferarum

The earth comes in, named *Terra*. It bears something, told with *gerit*. Forests come in, named *siluas*. Something is dreadful as well, described as *horrendaque*, though what it is is not yet said. What is dreadful is monsters, named *monstra*. Wild beasts come in, which close the line as *ferarum*. More of what it bears comes only in the next section.
