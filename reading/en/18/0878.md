### 878–879 (*Iliad* 18.503–508)

#### Lines 878–879

> 878 certantes populi; sedet illic aequus utrisque
>
> 879 iudex et litem discernit fronte serena.

Those who practice them are contending, described as *certantes*. Peoples come in, named *populi*. Someone sits, told with *sedet*. A place is set, marked by *illic*. Someone is impartial, described as *aequus*, though who it is is not yet said. To both comes in, which closes the line as *utrisque*.

Who is impartial is a judge, named *iudex*. More is added, marked by *et*. A dispute comes in, named *litem*. He settles it, told with *discernit*. A calm brow comes in, which closes the line as *fronte serena*.
