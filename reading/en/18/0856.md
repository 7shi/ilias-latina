### 856 (*Iliad* 18.146–148, 18.369)

#### Line 856

> 856 fluctibus auxilium Vulcani protinus orat.

What is left behind is the waves, named *fluctibus*. Help comes in, named *auxilium*. Vulcan comes in, named *Vulcani*. As the commentary says, Vulcan is the divine smith, the Roman counterpart of Hephaestus. A time is set, marked by *protinus*. She begs for it, which closes the line as *orat*.
