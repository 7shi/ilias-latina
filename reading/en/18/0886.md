### 886 (*Iliad* 18.550–551)

#### Line 886

> 886 maturasque metit robustus messor aristas

Something is ripe as well, described as *maturasque*, though what it is is not yet said. Someone cuts something, told with *metit*. A sturdy reaper comes in, given as *robustus messor*. What is ripe is ears of grain, which close the line as *aristas*.
