### 760–761 (*Iliad* 12.445, 12.453, 12.457–462)

#### Lines 760–761

> 760 obicibus muros. Tum saxo Martius Hector
>
> 761 perfringit portas ferrataque robora laxat.

What is massive is bars, spoken of as *obicibus*. What they secure is walls, named *muros*. A time is set, marked by *Tum*. A rock comes in, named *saxo*. Someone is warlike, described as *Martius*, though who it is is not yet said. The warlike one is Hector, who closes the line as *Hector*.

He breaks through something, told with *perfringit*. What he breaks through is gates, named *portas*. Something is iron-bound as well, described as *ferrataque*. What is iron-bound is timbers, spoken of as *robora*. He loosens them, which closes the line as *laxat*.
