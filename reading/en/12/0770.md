### 770–771 (*Iliad* 12.471)

#### Lines 770–771

> 770 et scandunt puppes. Vrget Troiana iuuentus
>
> 771 telaque crebra iacit: resonat clamoribus aether.

More is added, marked by *et*. They climb aboard, told with *scandunt*. What they climb aboard is ships, named *puppes*. Someone presses on, told with *Vrget*. Someone is Trojan, described as *Troiana*, though who it is is not yet said. The Trojan one is the youth, which closes the line as *iuuentus*.

Missiles come in as well, named *telaque*. They are thick, described as *crebra*. The youth hurls them, and that hurling is *iacit*. Something resounds, told with *resonat*. Shouts come in, named *clamoribus*. What resounds is the sky, which ends the twelfth book as *aether*.
