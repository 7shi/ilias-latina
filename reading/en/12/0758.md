### 758–759 (*Iliad* 12.37–39)

#### Lines 758–759

> 758 Incumbunt Troes, fugiunt in castra Pelasgi
>
> 759 uiribus exhaustis et uastis undique firmant

Someone presses forward, told with *Incumbunt*. Those who press forward are the Trojans, named *Troes*. Others flee, told with *fugiunt*. The camp comes in, given as *in castra*. Those who flee are the Pelasgians, who close the line as *Pelasgi*. As the commentary says, the Pelasgians are the Greeks.

Strength comes in, named *uiribus*. It is exhausted, described as *exhaustis*. More is added, marked by *et*. Something is massive, described as *uastis*, though what it is is not yet said. It is on every side, marked by *undique*. They secure it, which closes the line as *firmant*. What they secure and what is massive comes only in the next section.
