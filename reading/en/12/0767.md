### 767 (*Iliad* 12.287–289)

#### Line 767

> 767 Saxa uolant, subeunt acta testudine Troes

Stones come in, named *Saxa*. They fly, told with *uolant*. Someone comes up, told with *subeunt*. Something is driven forward, described as *acta*, though what it is is not yet said. What is driven forward is the tortoise, named *testudine*. Those who come up are the Trojans, who close the line as *Troes*.
