### 768–769 (*Iliad* 12.469–471)

#### Lines 768–769

> 768 ascenduntque aditus et portis uiribus instant.
>
> 769 Turbati fugiunt omnes iam castra Pelasgi

They climb something as well, told with *ascenduntque*. What they climb is the approaches, spoken of as *aditus*. More is added, marked by *et*. The gates come in, named *portis*. It is with force, given as *uiribus*. They press against them, which closes the line as *instant*.

Some are in panic, described as *Turbati*, though who they are is not yet said. They flee, told with *fugiunt*. All of them come in, described as *omnes*. A time is set, marked by *iam*. The camp comes in, named *castra*. Those in panic are the Pelasgians, who close the line as *Pelasgi*. Where they flee comes only in the next section.
