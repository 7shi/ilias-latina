### 937–938 (*Iliad* 22.136–138)

#### Lines 937–938

> 937 pertimuit clausisque fugit sua moenia circum
>
> 938 infelix portis, sequitur Nereius heros:

He was terrified, told with *pertimuit*. Some are shut as well, described as *clausisque*, though what they are is not yet said. He flees, told with *fugit*. His own walls come in, given as *sua moenia*. A place around is set, which closes the line as *circum*.

He is ill-fated, described as *infelix*. What is shut is the gates, named *portis*. Someone follows, told with *sequitur*. Someone is Nereian, described as *Nereius*. The hero comes in, who closes the line as *heros*. As the commentary says, the Nereian hero is Achilles. What follows comes only in the next section.
