### 931–932 (*Iliad* 22.35–36)

#### Lines 931–932

> 931 Vnus tota salus in quo Troiana manebat
>
> 932 Hector adest, quem non durae timor undique mortis,

One man comes in, described as *Vnus*. All safety comes in, given as *tota salus*. In him comes in, given as *in quo*. Safety is Trojan, described as *Troiana*. It remained, which closes the line as *manebat*.

The one man is Hector, called *Hector*. He is at hand, told with *adest*. Hector comes back, as *quem*. A denial comes, marked by *non*. Something is harsh, described as *durae*, though what it is is not yet said. Fear comes in, named *timor*. Every side comes in, marked by *undique*. What is harsh is death, which closes the line as *mortis*. What fear does comes only in the next section.
